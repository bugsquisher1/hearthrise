# 14. Premium and Crowns

**Outside v0.3.0.** Premium, Crowns and the $15 Early Access box are not part of "The Roads". This chapter describes them as designed, so you can see the whole game. Every price here is Tyler's call. Every other number is a starting value.

---

## 14.1 The business model
**[Later patch]**

Tyler (2026-11-22): "Yes 15 with a month of premium".

- **Steam Early Access is a one-time price of $15, with 30 days of Premium in the box.**
- Premium and Crowns are on top.
- Free to play gets a fresh look for full release, on Early Access data.
- Crowns are sold through Steam's in-game purchase system.

## 14.2 The line we hold
**[Later patch]**

Premium and Crowns **never** sell:
- Power per hour: XP, coin, drop chance, flame room, work speed, the Mastery cap or combat numbers.
- Stats on anything bought.
- Access to any ground, hall, floor, arena, trade or recipe.
- The first principle: plans, rules, copying, Carry on, the cause line, the risk reading and two Talent builds a hero stay free.

Nothing is reachable only with money. Tyler's own words on where the line sits (2026-11-21): "this game needs a model where buying crowns and selling for gold WILL provide advantages. Just not broken af ones."

## 14.3 Premium
**[Later patch]**

One Premium covers the whole company, all four heroes.

**Already ruled:**
- **Hunting day: 12 hours instead of 10.** About a fifth more XP, coin and drops a day for a hero who hunts the full day. Nothing more per hour. A free hero can match it with one Hunting block.
- **Town work day: 10 hours instead of 8.**

**Recommended on top:**

| Benefit | What a free player still has |
|---|---|
| **Talent freedom.** Each hero's first Talent reset of the day is free, plus a second saved build per hero | One saved build per hero, and any reset for gold |
| **A market seat.** Three times the listings and buy orders, twice the listing life, half the listing fee. Same sales tax | Every rule that lists and buys |
| **The company ledger.** 90 days of reports, trends by hero and ground, two plans side by side, price history | The cause line, the one fix, the risk reading, seven days of history |
| **The wardrobe.** Premium looks for heroes, mounts and the hall's fire while Premium runs, and one piece kept each paid month | Every look earned in play |
| **Loyalty marks.** A mark by the company name at 3, 6, 12 and 24 paid months, hideable | Nothing missing |
| Custom alerts (once phones can push) | The "day is done" alert |

**Refused:** storage that locks when Premium lapses, a lower sales tax, a faster mount, a fourth Mastery, more flame room, a fifth hero, more plans, auto-restart, faster work, more XP or coin an hour, and Premium-only grounds.

**A saved build in a Premium slot** is never deleted when Premium ends. It can still be applied, and can't be replaced until Premium returns.

**Today in the data:** Premium switches on a longer hunting day, the hunt analyzer, three Premium outfits (Quarry Brute, Hedge Witch, Dawnward) and a daily login item (an Oak Practice Blade). During the beta every hero has Premium.

Not final: the daily login item isn't in the recommended set. See the contradictions list.

## 14.4 Crowns
**[Later patch]**

Crowns are bought with real money. **Never earned in game.** A player can spend them, or sell them to another player for gold.

**What Crowns buy:**

| Sink | Pay-to-win |
|---|---|
| Premium time, 30 days | Per day only (hours) |
| Hero and hall looks: outfits, work animations, hall fire colors, clan banners | None |
| Mounts, with their own sprite and riding animation | None |
| **Tools that never break**, with their own look and work animation. They work like the best crafted tool the hero can use, never better | None |
| A saved Talent build slot for one hero | Mild |
| Storage tabs, kept forever | None |
| Premium as a gift to another account | None |
| Renames and restyles | None |

**Never for Crowns:** gear, materials, remains, gold, hunting or work hours, boosts, Mastery slots, plans, hunt bag room, and **anything random**. No paid random boxes. Everything bought with Crowns binds to the account. Only Crowns trade.

## 14.5 The Crowns book
**[Later patch]**

A server-run exchange where players set the price.

| Part | Design |
|---|---|
| The book | Crowns only, in fixed lots. Asks and standing bids, matched by the server, which never makes Crowns or gold to fill an order |
| One trade | A Crown bought with gold binds to its buyer |
| Fees | 1% to list and 4% of the gold on a sale, both burned |
| Guide price | The 7-day median, shown beside every Crowns price. A lot outside half to twice the guide is refused |
| Weekly cap | About one account-week of wage at the highest hero's Level (280 hours of wage on a 10-hour day) |
| Starter protection | Crowns bought with money wait out the refund window before they can be listed. Selling needs the company's first depth record |
| Standing orders | A plan rule: "buy up to two lots a week at no more than this" |

**Why rich players want Crowns.** Premium's extra hours would cost far more in Hunting and work blocks. Buying Premium on the book is the cheapest way to those hours. The wage rises with Level and the Crown's price doesn't, so far players are the bidders.

## 14.6 Is anything pay-to-win?
**[Later patch]**

**Per hour, no. Per day, yes.**

| Path | What money buys |
|---|---|
| Bought hunting (2 blocks a day) | Up to 40% to 60% more XP, coin, drops and remains a day. Nothing more per hour |
| Rare pieces from other players | A top kit sooner, bounded by what players found and by Level. A lucky kit is at most about one tier ahead |
| Premium's 2 hours | About 20% more a day |
| Bought remains | The top third of a full flame, the smallest part of the luck |
| Bought town work | Trade XP sooner. No combat power |

**How ranked things are protected:**
- Ladders rank inside Level bands of 20, so bought hours move you into the next band sooner, never up your own.
- No board ranks raw Level or XP.
- The even arena reads nothing bought.
- The gear arena matches inside gear bands.
- The world boss pays by share against your band, counted per week, never per hunted hour.
- Castle labor counts the work day only.

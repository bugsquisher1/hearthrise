BRIEF 01: THE MUDLARK, the Slake's one new creature (plan line A4a, about 40 generations)
Art director, 2026-11-07. Run the SAMPLE first (step 1 and the south facing of step 2), stop, and hand me the strip.

WHAT IT IS (docs/planning/PRIORITY_BOARD.md, "The proof: The Slake", row Mudlark): a river scavenger who picked the
Banded Company's dead on the mudflat below Hask's Ford and drowned among them. Undead like every Barrow March body, but
small, hunched and quick: a skirmisher that catches a kiter and hits often and light. Its loot is a hook, so it holds one.
Sprite id `mudlark`, set `slake` (staged; see "where it goes").

STYLE (style 1, docs/design/PIXELLAB_STYLE1_KIT.md §3; never mix styles):
  view     high top-down, the one angle of every world sprite; never side-on, never isometric
  outline  single color black outline
  shading  basic shading, light from the upper left; no glow, no shadow under the feet (the engine draws shadows)
  palette  natural and muted: mud browns and greys, the bone-grey of drowned skin; ONE signature accent, verdigris
           (copper-green), on at most 15% of its pixels (ART_DIRECTION §4). No bright colours, no purple.
  size     48x48 canvas. Its class is `vermin` (src/data/world/art-budget.js: 48 frame, body 28-40 px, measured on the
           larger side). Crouched, its body should measure 30-36 px tall: a head shorter than the hero (36-40).
  style image  art/briefs/gen-2026-11-07/refs/style-glade-poacher-s-48.png (the Glade poacher, the style every Barrow
           March body was made from). Size and stance check: refs/style-goblin-sluicer-s-48.png (a crouched 37 px body).
           Upload with get_upload_url and pass upload:<id> (a pasted base64 is truncated, KIT §5).

STEP 1, the rotations: create_object_pro_flash
  n_directions 8, size 48x48, view "high top-down", outline "single color black outline", shading "basic shading",
  style_image = the poacher crop, with "match palette, outline, shading".
  prompt: "hunched undead river scavenger crouching low, mud-caked torn rags, grey-green waterlogged skin, a lumpy sack of
           scavenged scraps tied on its back, a short iron boathook in one hand, green copper stains on the rags and the
           hook, muted browns and greys, game enemy sprite"
  name: "S1 Mudlark"
  Reject and retry when: it stands upright like a man (it must crouch), the sack is missing in any facing, the hook
  changes hands between facings, it reads as a goblin (no pointed ears, no big nose), or a facing comes out side-on.

STEP 2, the clips: animate_object (v3) on the object from step 1, one call per facing.
  idle    8 facings, generate 8 frames, keep the first frame the still: "breathing slowly while crouched, the sack rises
          and falls, feet planted, stays in place, nothing else appears in the frame"
  walk    8 facings, 6 frames (v3 keeps the still as an extra first frame: drop it, so 7 come back for 6):
          "quick crouched scuttle, both feet lift in turn, the sack bounces, nothing else appears in the frame"
  attack  8 facings, 6 frames: "lunges forward and hooks with the boathook, then pulls back, nothing else appears in the
          frame". The blow lands on frame 3 (pass --hit 3 at import).
  death   south, east, north, west only, 6 frames: "collapses face down into the mud, the sack spills, lies still"
  Muster lesson (lane/b-muster-barrows-art): diagonal facings paint glints and sparkles despite the words. Retry those in a
  NEW animation group (a group inherits its description), worded "nothing else appears in the frame".
  Reject and retry when: gear appears or vanishes mid-clip, the feet do not lift in turn, a frame drifts off the anchor
  by more than 3 px, or any effect (spark, slash arc, splash) is painted in.

WHERE IT GOES: download the object ZIP. Save it under art/world-src/gen-2026-11-07/mudlark/: rotations as
<facing>.png (south, east, north, west, south-east, south-west, north-east, north-west), clips as
walk/<facing>/<i>.png, attack/<facing>/<i>.png, death/<facing>/<i>.png, idle/<facing>/frame_NNN.png, and a note.json
with the object id and every animation group id. Do not import, wire or build: the art director reads it first.
Import, after my yes (an art lane, on the Slake's art branch, never main while the Slake is unshipped):
  node tools/world-art/port-pixellab.mjs unit --set slake --id mudlark --dir art/world-src/gen-2026-11-07/mudlark
       --class vermin --object <OBJ_ID> --name "S1 Mudlark" --hit 3
  node tools/world-art/port-pixellab.mjs idle --set slake --id mudlark --clip art/world-src/gen-2026-11-07/mudlark/idle
  node tools/world-art/build.mjs --set slake;  node tests/world-assets.mjs

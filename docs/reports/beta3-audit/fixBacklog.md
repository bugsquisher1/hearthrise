### Recommended design for C1, and why

Three candidates were on the table:

- **Per-kill loot credit RPC (client claims an item map).** Rejected. It puts a client-supplied quantity of a tradeable item on the wire — the one thing CLAUDE.md's server-authority rule forbids ("never trust a client-supplied value that crosses to another player"). It also opens a self-only over-credit surface on commons that the market can then launder.
- **Span-sim fidelity (feed the settle the attended inputs so it reproduces the fight).** Rejected as the primary fix. It cannot converge: the settle seed is `hr_seed(user, slot, 'accrue:'+accrued_to)` mixing a 256-bit secret with RLS on (`supabase/functions/hr-accrue/index.ts:500-506`) precisely so the client cannot predict rolls, while the client rolls its own table with its own RNG (`src/core/combat-sim.js:130-137`). Making them agree means deleting the seed secret. Fidelity work is still worth doing for the *unattended* path (C1-4, and the auto-eat column), just not as the loot answer.
- **RECOMMENDED — server rolls loot per CREDITED kill, plus a `loot_accrued_to` watermark.** `hr_credit_kills` already accepts an observed count, clamped by `hr_bounty_kill_cap` and journalled. Deriving loot from the *credited* count means every die stays server-side, no client quantity crosses the wire, and the watermark makes double-payment structurally impossible (the identical shape the combat-XP split already ships and `tests/combat-xp-settle-split.mjs` already guards).

**Tradeoff to state plainly to the designer before the lane starts:** the player will get the right COUNT of drops but not the same IDS the combat log showed ("the log said goblin_ear, the bag has bones"). Two ways to close that: keep client rolls display-only and add a retirement receipt ("4 predicted drops did not land"), or make the credit response authoritative for the log. The receipt half (C1 increment 3) is shippable on its own TODAY and converts silent data loss into a stated loss, which is the property CLAUDE.md rule 3 actually asks for — ship it first if the design ruling takes time.

**Gold:** rule explicitly. Either include gold in the same watermark or record the decision that gold stays server-dice; today `accrual.js:1419-1429` pays gold for the full window with no split, so it is the next thing to drift.

# Hearthrise itemization roadmap, Levels 1 to 400

Game designer (Fable), 2026-11-18. Companion to `docs/design/itemization-system.md` (the six rules). Everything here is **proposed and unmeasured** unless a row says built or ruled. It is a plan to build grounds against, not a catalogue. Hours past Level 200 are arithmetic from the 10-25 pace ruling, not a run. Region 4 names are working names, not checked free.

## One-page summary

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How long is a region past 110? | About 55 Levels, six grounds in faction pairs, one call-out boss in the middle, one keyed boss at the end |
| How many regions to 400? | Five after the Barrow March: the Morrow Hills, then four more, with the Unmarked Catacombs as a bridge at 165 to 185 |
| What replaces gear tiers past tier 7? | Two "bands" a region. A band is a power budget for named uniques, not a set of standard pieces |
| What drops hourly once standard gear ends? | Crafting stock and gem chips. Uniques stay Rares |
| How does crafting stay alive with a 99 cap? | 99 is the license. After it, recipes come from patterns, materials from the region's own grounds, and a maker's mark per region |
| What does each region add to crafted slots? | One metal, one hide or cloth, one main gem, two new perks |
| Chest gear late? | One key tier per band. A piece sits at about the next band's budget. Two-attribute pieces start at the Level 200 key |
| Tempering late? | One small ladder per band, same 1% a rung, capped low |
| What must a new ground ship with? | The template in section 9: about 18 to 20 item rows |

**How big this is, in hunting hours**

| Levels | Hours in the band | Hours an average Level |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 110 | about 710 | 3 to 11 |
| 110 to 165 | about 590 (the board's figure) | 10.8 |
| 165 to 200 | about 380 | 10.8 |
| 200 to 300 | 1,440 | 14.4 |
| 300 to 400 | about 1,800 | 18 |

Levels 110 to 400 are about 4,200 hunting hours, roughly 350 days at 12 hours a day. Six grounds a region at about 150 hours each fits that.

| Levels | Region | Grounds | Gear | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 50 | Greenhaven | Six | Tiers 1 to 3, standard | Built |
| 45 to 110 | The Barrow March | Five, Krell's Hall | Tiers 4 to 6 standard, tier 7 from Krell | Built |
| 110 to 165 | The Morrow Hills | Six, the Roost Mother (call), Father Gault (hall) | Finishes tier 7 at 110 to 128. Bands 8 and 9 | Designed 11-14, one ground staged |
| 165 to 185 | The Unmarked Catacombs | One ground under Havora, plus the descent at any Level | Its own uniques, band 10 | Ground staged with no gear. Descent designed |
| 185 to 240 | Region 4 | Six, a call boss, a hall boss | Bands 11 and 12 | Structure only |
| 240 to 295 | Region 5 | Same | Bands 13 and 14 | Structure only |
| 295 to 350 | Region 6 | Same | Bands 15 and 16 | Structure only |
| 350 to 400+ | Region 7 | Same | Bands 17 and 18 | Structure only |

**Tiers and bands**

| | Levels 1 to 110 | Past 110 |
|---|---|---|
| Step | A tier every 15 Levels (1, 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 88) | A band about every 27 Levels, two a region |
| What a step is | A full standard set in three weights and seven weapon families | A power budget. Every piece is named and has its own stat mix |
| How power rises | The tier's numbers | The band's budget line, tied to the Level the piece is worn from (ruled 11-16: about Dawnsteel times Level over 110, no tier 8) |
| Hours a step | 60 to 110 | 290 at 110 to 200, about 390 at 200 to 300, about 490 at 300 to 400 |

A band lasts three to five times longer than a tier did. That works only because a ground gives a hero a new unique every few hours (section 9).

**Rules every region follows** (already ruled): equal counts of First Flame and Last Ember grounds (11-14); a region's last boss is a keyed hall with a daily personal roll, bosses before it are call series (11-15); no ground is best at everything; no bound gear, gear worn by Level only (10-23, 10-24); villains are NPCs both factions fight (11-14).

## 2. What each band adds, by slot

### Dropped slots (rule 1)

| Slot | Tiers 1 to 7 | Each band past 7 |
|---|---|---|
| Helmet, body, legs, boots | Standard, three weights, about hourly | Named uniques as Rares. Across a region's six grounds every slot and weight appears at least once. Boss pieces on top |
| Gloves | Standard, Rares | Uniques, plus the Catacombs glove wing |
| Weapon | Standard, seven families (eight with fists) | Two unique weapons a ground. Across a region every family appears twice |
| Shield, spellbook | Standard | One or two a region, plus the boss's |

**What a unique is** (ruled 11-14, 11-16): inside the band's budget, with its own stat mix and one property. It wins somewhere (a ground or a build) and gives something up. None is the best in its slot everywhere. No set bonuses.

### Crafted slots (rule 2)

| What a region adds | Count | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| A metal for rings, earrings and buckles | One | Ore from kills and from nodes on that region's grounds |
| A hide or cloth for cloaks and belts, and a chain for necklaces | One or two | Common drops |
| A main gem | One | The region's hall boss, on the daily roll |
| Lesser gems | Two or three | Ground Rares and the call boss |
| Gem chips | One row a ground | The hourly drop. A set number of chips make a gem at the forge |
| New perks | Two, one on an attack pool and one on a guard pool | The engine |
| Plain pieces | Six: ring, earrings, necklace, cloak, belt (three weights counted as one piece) | Recipes unlocked by the region's patterns |

**Perk sizes do not grow past tier 7.** A region brings new perks, not bigger copies. The whole worn set stays worth less than one weapon step, so a Level 110 crafter's work isn't worthless at 200 and power doesn't compound over 400 Levels.

### Used up (rule 3)

| Supply | Past tier 7 |
|---|---|
| Arrows, bolts, whetstones | One new rung a region, from the region's metal |
| Runes | Five tiers is the ruling. No new rune rung |
| Food and drink | One or two new dishes a region from the region's meat, fish or crop |

## 3. Crafting across 400 Levels: the rule

**99 is the license. After 99, a crafter grows by what they've found and what they've made.**

| Layer | How it works | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Skill level, 1 to 99 | Unlocks recipes through tier 7. Raises roll odds and the good batch chance, both topping out at 99 | Ruled: gathering and crafting cap at 99 |
| Patterns | Every recipe past tier 7 is a pattern, a Rare from that region's grounds. Using one unlocks the recipe for the account | Hunting feeds the bench. A pattern book to fill, region by region |
| Region materials | A region's metal, hide and gems come only from that region's kills and nodes | A crafter needs a hunter at that Level, or the market |
| Maker's mark | Make a set number of a region's pieces and the account earns that region's mark: a small capped boost to the Fine chance for that region's pieces | Something to work toward after 99 without raising the cap |
| Reforging | Carries a Hallmarked piece into the next band at the fee difference | Old work keeps its value |

Nodes for new ores sit on hunting grounds (as the Night Shift's Dawnstone Veins are designed) and need the top gathering level, so mining a new region's ore needs a hero who can stand on that ground.

## 4. Chest gear, later game

Tyler, 11-18: chest items stay rare, so there is no hard ceiling. They may beat the next tier's drop, and that's about as far as they should go. The deeper the content, the rarer and stronger, some with two perks later.

| Dial | Levels 15 to 110 (the four keys today) | 110 to 200 | 200 to 300 | 300 to 400 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Key tiers | Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum at landings 10, 40, 70, 100 | One per band (the board names a key at 130) | One per band | One per band |
| Power of a piece | At about the next tier's drop | At about the next band's budget | Same | Same |
| Attributes | One | One, larger | One; a paid piece has about a 1 in 5 chance to be its two-attribute version | Two on about half of paid pieces |
| Base chance a key pays a piece | 16 / 8 / 4.5 / 2% (ruled) | About 2% | About 1.5% | About 1% |
| Bad luck | The Hearth's warmth, stokes, reforging | Same | Same | Same |

- "Next band and no further" keeps one rule the whole way: a chest piece is one step ahead of what the ground drops, never two.
- Two-attribute pieces start at the Level 200 key, so Level 200 is a big deal and the second half of the game gets a new chase.
- Each two-attribute piece is its own named, hand-authored row: a sample first, then small batches.
- Chest reforging (uses one of the 6 weekly opens) is how a won piece follows a hero up a band.
- The Catacombs chest follows the same table with helmets, boots and gloves: a vault door every 10 rooms, one key tier per band.

## 5. Tempering past tier 6

| Ladder | Levels | Cap a track | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tiers 2 to 6 | 15 to 88 | 5% | Built |
| Dawnsteel | 88 to 110 | Small, sized on the simulator | Ruled 11-16, not built |
| One ladder per band past 7 | 110 and up | About 2 to 3% | Ruled 11-15 in principle |

1% a rung, nothing rolls or fails, the ladder sits on the hero, each band starts at rung 0 so power can't compound. Gold per ladder about 40 to 50% of what a hero banks in the band. Each band's salt is made from that region's ore and a common drop. A unique needs a `band` number in its data so the smith knows which ladder it's on.

## 6. Linings by region

Strengths stay 20 / 25 / 30% with the 40% cap (the 11-13 design). A region decides which element is the wall on each ground and supplies the part; it doesn't make linings stronger.

| Region | Wall elements | The part |
|---|---|---|
| Barrow March | Fire at the Pyre Field and the Hold | Each ground's own monster part |
| Morrow Hills | Ice on the Ox Road and the Old Guard, poison on the Night Shift, fire at the Bellows | Same. Carter's Rest's Coal Tongs are named for the fire lining |
| Catacombs | Poison (Lime Sack), ice | Lime Mortar, Leech Jaw |
| Regions 4 to 7 | Each ground names one wall element; a region uses at least two; no ground's wall matches its faction pair's | One part a monster |

A fourth element (Air exists as a hidden rune family) is the one new thing a later region can add to this system.

## 9. The template: what a new ground must ship with

For a ground past tier 7: five monsters, the fifth an elite. Batches stay at 20 rows or fewer, sampled and played first.

| Rows | How many | Band | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coin | Every monster | | Income |
| Bones or a Hearth fuel | One a monster (existing rows where they fit) | Common | Inner Flame |
| Crafting stock: the region's ore, hide or cloth | Two or three rows across the ground (at least one ore and one hide or cloth in a region's first three grounds) | Common | Rings, belts, cloaks, salts |
| The monster's own part | One a monster, five | Uncommon | Linings |
| Gem chips | One row, on the elite | About hourly | A gem without waiting on one Rare |
| Unique armor | Three: one per weight, three different slots | Rare | The chase |
| Unique weapons | Two, from the families the ground favors | Rare | The chase |
| A wildcard unique | One: shield, spellbook or gloves | Rare | Fills the region's grid |
| A pattern | One | Rare | Unlocks one of the region's crafted recipes |
| A lesser gem | One, on every second ground | Rare | Hallmarking |
| A named Hearth fuel | One a ground or boss | Rare | Ruled 10-25 |
| A trophy | Boss grounds only | 100 to 150 h | Ruled |

Total: about 18 to 20 new item rows a ground.

**Around the ground:** the region's ore or wood nodes on at least two grounds a region; one named wall element with its lining part on this ground; the ground pays one faction (built in pairs); one Tamsin task row; the band's salt recipe names this region's ore; wiki monster stat cards from data.

**A region's bosses**

| Boss | Ships with |
|---|---|
| Call-out boss, mid-region | Three boss pieces for different slots, one call-only piece, a lesser gem |
| Hall boss, region end | A key on the last ground's elite. Four boss pieces, no two in one slot. The region's main gem. A hide for the region's top cloak. A trophy. One pattern. A daily personal roll |

**A region's crafted set** (one batch, once the grounds exist): ring, earrings, necklace, cloak, three belts, a buckle, a salt, an ammo rung, two perks. Their graded rows are generated.

**The check a lane runs before it reports a ground:** every unique sits inside the band's budget; every slot and weight cell for the region is still coverable by the grounds left; every part has a lining or recipe that uses it; a hero in the previous band's gear can hunt it on Normal.

**What a hero sees on such a ground:** with seven or eight uniques as Rares at 10 to 20 hours each, a unique drops about every two hours, and one that fits their style and weight roughly every six to ten. Over 150 hours on a ground that's 15 to 25 pieces they can use or sell (arithmetic, not measured).

# Visual audit, 2026-10-15 (Art Director, phase 2)

**Scope.** The world-shell prototype (`prototypes/world-shell/`, branch `design/world-shell` at 13bfafe8, which boots the shipped b585 World runtime and atlases) and the b585 game (`index.html` on the same tree, test harness, signed out). Captured fresh at DPR 1 with Playwright: the prototype at its one density ×2, desktop 1440×900 and phone 922×423. Every screenshot below was read. Shots: `prototypes/world-shell/shots/phase2/` (named in the Evidence column). Measured values come from scripts run on the committed atlases and the live DOM; how each was measured is named once. Nothing here is scored (an independent review does that).

**Severity:** P0 a player cannot play · P1 a player is misled or cannot read the game · P2 it looks unfinished · P3 polish. **Effort:** S under a day · M a few days · L a week or more.

## Top five
1. **Monsters vanish into the Mill Cellar floor (P1).** The rat's body edge reads at 2.36:1 against the floor's median and its black outline at 1.17:1, so the outline does nothing on near-black earth. Slime 2.59:1. The rule is ≥ 3:1 (ART_DIRECTION §4).
2. **Loot is invisible (P1).** The loot bag is a 12×14 dark sack at 1.06:1, and it lands on the corpse's own tile (`src/world/choreo.js:166 bags.push({ at })`), so it hides inside the body.
3. **The hero slides on diagonals (P1).** The pather walks 8 directions (`src/world/core/path.js` DIRS has 8) but the hero has 4 facings (`art-budget.js` DIRS `s,e,n,w`), and the south walk lifts only the left foot.
4. **Nothing in the world moves but the hero (P2).** Every monster idle, both NPCs, the three peers, the torches, the brazier and the waterwheel are one still frame (`fps: 0` in all four atlas manifests). An idle game is watched; a still world reads as a screenshot.
5. **The phone HUD is not the ruled HUD (P1 for B1).** The prototype's phone bottom HUD measures 90 px (21.3% of 423) with 10 slots and the vitals on the bar; the 10-14 ruling is 68 px (16%), 6 slots + a page toggle, vitals top-left. B1 must build the ruling, not the prototype.

## What is good and must be kept
- The pixel world itself: one palette, one light direction, one perspective, measured scale classes. The town's roofs, walls and trees and the cellar's props are a real game's art (`town-desktop.png`).
- The one density: the prototype's world, icons and gear figure share ×2 exactly.
- The shell's layout: the world edge to edge, windows docked, an analyzer instead of a dashboard.

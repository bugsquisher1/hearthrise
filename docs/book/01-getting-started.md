# 1. Getting started and the first hour

How a brand-new player meets Hearthrise, from the first screen to the first time they come back after a full day away.

Tags used in this book: **[New in 0.3]** is a new design shipping in v0.3.0 "The Roads". **[Changed]** is an old system reworked or cut. **[Unchanged]** works as it does today. **[Later patch]** is designed but ships after 0.3.

---

## 1.1 What kind of game this is
**[Unchanged]**

- You lead a company of up to four heroes in a shared pixel world.
- You never swing the sword yourself. You decide who fights, how, with what, where and how much risk they take.
- The server plays every hunt, whether the game is open or closed. Hunts keep going after you close the browser.
- You come back to a report that says how deep your plan held, what broke first and what dropped.
- No classes. A hero is whatever they fight with. Level never stops.

## 1.2 Making your first hero
**[Unchanged]**

You make one hero and pick how they fight from six style cards. The pick puts the whole practice set on the hero.

| Style card | What they wear |
|---|---|
| Tank | Practice Sword and Practice Shield |
| Dual Axes | Practice Hatchets |
| Club | Practice Club |
| Mage | Practice Wand and Practice Spellbook |
| Ranger | Practice Bow and Practice Crossbow |
| Healer | Practice Staff |

The other practice weapons sit in the bag, so trying another style later costs nothing.

## 1.4 The first hunt: the Mill Cellar
**[Changed]**

- One press from town sends you to the Mill Cellar, under the mill in Havora.
- Giant Rats, Slimes and the odd Hive Wasp live there. Levels 1 to 8.
- **New:** the hunt screen reads "Depth 1 of 20". The Cellar is on the Danger scale like every ground (chapter 5). There is no tier picker anymore.
- The risk setting plays Steady here and isn't shown as a choice yet. You meet it at your first party ground.
- Your hero eats by itself from the bar.
- The Cellar ends itself at Level 5. Your hero has outgrown it.

Not final: the Cellar's place on the Danger scale is built only for Muster Barrows today. Every other ground, the Cellar included, moves onto the scale in the R10 lane.

## 1.5 Your first Level: a Roads point
**[New in 0.3]**

- At Level 2 your hero earns their first Roads point. One point comes with every Level after that, forever.
- The card says "A Roads point. Spent on {move}'s road."
- "Spend as suggested" spends it for you. It can be a standing rule, so you never press it again.
- Chapter 3 explains the Roads.

## 1.6 Back in town after the Cellar
**[New in 0.3]** for the flame and first-cook cards, **[Unchanged]** for the rest

At about Level 4 you come back to town. The end card shows:
- What your hunt earned.
- **New:** the bones and remains your company burned, and how tall your Inner Flame stands (chapter 7).
- A card about the Hearth: "The Hearth. Your company's luck."
- Tamsin Reeve, the tally-keeper, has gear for you (chapter 12).
- **New:** your first cook. "Cook 10 Shrimp" uses the 10 raw Shrimp from your kit.

## 1.7 Level 5: your first crossroads and your company
**[New in 0.3]**

Two big things happen at Level 5.

**Your first crossroads.** A move's road splits three ways. You pick one, and it changes how the move plays. The suggested one is already pressed.

**Your company arrives.** "{Name} has outgrown the Mill Cellar. Three recruits are ready." One press brings three recruits:
- They arrive at Level 5, the same Level as your first hero.
- They take jobs your first hero doesn't play, from Tank, Healer, Ranger and Club.
- Their Roads points are already spent as suggested for their job.

The old party finder is gone (chapter 12).

## 1.8 Your first party hunt: the Sewer and the Risk pick
**[New in 0.3]**

- The Sewer runs under town and wants a full party. Levels 5 to 25.
- Drain Rats, Culvert Bats, Sump Slimes, Sewer Kobolds and Cistern Spiders. The Rat King lives at the bottom.
- **The Risk pick shows up here for the first time.** Careful, Steady or Bold. Steady is pressed. Each one has a line under it. Chapter 5 has the details.
- Your party climbs deeper by itself and falls back when it gets into trouble.
- **Your first fall back is explained.** The report says something like "Fell back: Rook at 24% to a Sewer Kobold" and "Held depth 6 of 20".

## 1.9 Ten and fifteen minutes into the party hunt
**[Unchanged]** (new trades behind it)

- **At 10 minutes,** the game shows your rest plan. That is what your company works on in town once its hunting day is over (chapter 9).
- **At 15 minutes,** it tells you plainly: "It is safe to close the game. Your party hunts for about 10 more hours, then goes to work."

Not final: the rest-plan card is switched off today. It reads a company settings file that doesn't exist yet. The first-hour rewrite (lane H1) adds it.

## 1.10 Hours two and three
**[Changed]**

- Depth climbs on the Sewer as your heroes grow.
- Second crossroads at about Level 9.
- Wickstead, a burned farm, opens at Level 8 for a lone hero.
- The old "try Dangerous" nudges are gone, because there are no tiers to try.

## 1.11 Your first return
**[New in 0.3]** for Hunting blocks, **[Unchanged]** for the return card

After the 10-hour hunting day your heroes walk home and go to work. When you come back:
- One return card for the whole company. Totals first.
- What the trades made, what the flame burned.
- At most three things that need you.
- A **Hunting block** is offered: 2 more hours of hunting, bought with gold, at most 2 a day (chapter 10). This replaces the old 4-hour stamina refill.
- "Carry on" starts the next day with one press.

## 1.12 Later on
**[New in 0.3]**

- **Level 30:** your first vow slot (chapter 3).
- **Level 55:** your first Trait, at Muster Barrows, rank I (chapter 6).

Nothing in the first three hours needs a Trait, a Signature, the market or a party finder. You meet all of them later, on purpose.

## 1.14 Choices you make once
**[New in 0.3]**

The game's first rule is that you never make the same choice twice by hand.

| Choice | Made once, where | Remembered by |
|---|---|---|
| Spending Roads points | "Spend as suggested" | A standing rule. Recruits get it too |
| A crossroads | The card at that Level | Your plan. Another hero can copy the build |
| Risk | The Hunts card at your first party ground | Your plan, for every ground |
| Burn or keep remains | The default stoke rule | Your plan |
| What the company works | The rest plan | Your plan |
| Buying a Hunting block | The return card | A standing "buy up to N" rule |

## 1.15 Returning players
**[New in 0.3]**

- There's no hero conversion. Everyone starts fresh in 0.3.
- A "What's new" card shows once on your first sign-in to 0.3, with a "you start fresh" line for returning players.

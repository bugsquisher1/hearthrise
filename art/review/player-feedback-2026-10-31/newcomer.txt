HEARTHRISE — first two evenings, from a total newcomer
Posted by: Pip's player (came over from the stream Discord)
Date: 2026-10-31 · desktop browser, 1440x900, mouse + keyboard

About me: phone games and a bit of Stardew. Never played an MMO, Tibia, Huntera or an idle game.
I don't know what DPS, tank, aggro or "stamina" mean in game-speak. I gave it two evenings.

How I played (so you can weigh this): the real game on a local test server, not the live site.
"Waiting" was done with a time-skip button instead of real hours. A few odd things are probably
caused by that skip, and I've put those in their own section at the bottom so you don't chase them.
Screenshots are in newcomer/ next to this file (01..15).


================================================================
EVENING ONE (sign-up and about an hour)
================================================================

1. SIGNING UP
   Doing: made an account. I typo'd the second password on purpose.
   Expected: the usual.
   Happened: a clear "The two passwords do not match." Fixed it, then "Check your email for a
   link". Back to sign in, and I was in within a blink (about 0.2 s).
   Felt: fine, painless. Nothing tells me what kind of game this IS before I sign up, though.
   Screenshot: (not kept)

2. "YOUR CHARACTERS"
   Doing: first screen after logging in.
   Happened: "Up to 4 can play at once." but I only see 2 slots. Huh? (Later I learnt a 3rd slot
   costs 15,000 gold.) The idea of more than one hero at once didn't mean anything to me yet.
   Felt: a bit confused, but OK.

3. HERO CREATOR  <- LOVED IT
   Doing: named my hero Pip, picked yellow hair, a green shirt, brown trousers.
   Happened: a cute chunky pixel preview updates as you click colours. The "How you fight"
   text is short and readable ("There's no class to pick. The weapon you hold is how you fight").
   Felt: smiled. Felt like my own little guy.
   Screenshot: 01-hero-creator.png

4. ...AND THEN MY COLOURS WERE GONE
   Doing: pressed Enter town.
   Expected: my green-shirt blondie in town.
   Happened: a grey armoured knight with a red plume walked out. None of my colours anywhere.
   The Outfit tab later lists "Plate Harness", "Grove Robe" etc, but never my colours. Same thing
   happened with my second hero.
   Felt: honestly a little deflated. The first thing I made in your game didn't stick.
   Screenshot: 02-town-first-look.png

5. FIRST LOOK AT TOWN: TOO MANY BUTTONS
   Doing: just looking around.
   Happened: the town art is gorgeous (barn, cobbles, a big round stone square). But:
   - 15 small icons across the top with no words. Hovering shows a name, but the first one I
     hovered ("Inv...") was hidden UNDER the quest box.
   - Bottom bar: 20 numbered boxes, HP/MP ring, an hourglass "12:00h", "Food x20", "Charm",
     "Setup Preset 1", "Target: Nearest", "Offens... Balanc... Defen...", "Chase", "Distance - 1 +".
     I didn't know what any of the right-hand half meant and was scared to touch it.
   - The quest box was great though: "Rats in the mill. Open Hunts and enter the Mill Cellar."
     with the right icon lit up in gold. I just did what it said.
   Felt: overwhelmed for a second, then rescued by the quest box.
   Screenshot: 02-town-first-look.png

6. THE HUNTS WINDOW
   Happened: lovely monster portraits (rat, slime, wasp) and "Fits your level" in green told me
   where to go. The details below were gibberish to me: "Each pull: 2 packs of 2 to 3 · back in
   32 s", "As built + / -", "Up to 8 a pull when you hunt alone", "Gold: 500 gp pays the store as
   you use it" (what store? I'm in a cellar!).
   Felt: liked the top half, skipped the bottom half.
   Screenshot: 03-hunts-window.png

7. FIRST FIGHT  <- LOVED IT
   Doing: pressed Enter.
   Happened: instantly in the Mill Cellar (well under a second). The cellar is SO cosy: sacks,
   jars, a millstone, a little brazier. A tip said "You don't click to attack. Your hero walks to
   the rats, swings, and picks the next one." That's exactly the sentence I needed.
   "Power Strike" and "+15 XP" pop up over Pip. Level 2 in about 80 seconds.
   Smooth as butter (I checked: a steady 60 fps).
   Small thing: top right says "Next level in: after 10 min" while the ring says I'm 84% there.
   It reads like the next level comes in 10 minutes.
   Felt: this is the "ohh, I get it" moment. Very satisfying.
   Screenshot: 04-first-fight.png

8. FARMING WHILE I HUNT  <- LOVED IT
   Doing: a tip said "Plant your land", so I opened Farm.
   Happened: 2 plots, plant turnips, press Water ("grows twice as fast for 2 hours"). Tiny turnip
   drawings with "2 h left". Pip kept hunting the whole time.
   Felt: my Stardew brain lit up. Doing two things at once is the hook for me.
   Small thing: hovering the Turnip Seed in my bag says "Used for: Sell it at the shop." I just
   planted them!
   Screenshot: 05-farm-while-hunting.png

9. THE "ACTION BAR" TIP HID ITSELF
   Happened: after farming, a tip appeared already folded up into a tiny strip that just said
   "Your action bar". I nearly missed it. Opened, it said the bar "uses the first one that's ready
   and whose conditions are all true". Conditions?
   Felt: shrug.

10. "EAT ON TIME" — THIS FELT LIKE HOMEWORK
    Doing: after skipping 10 minutes, a tip told me to change when Pip eats.
    Happened: "A Cooked Shrimp heals 96, and at 60% of your 170 HP you're missing 68, so 28 of it
    is wasted. Press slot 3, press - until it reads 50% (only 11 is wasted there...)".
    The slot opens a screen that looks like programming: "when [my HP] [is below] [60] %",
    "+ Add a condition", and a big table with "g per HP, cheapest first". I did what it said.
    Then straight away ANOTHER tip: "slot 4 still buys Salted Shrimp below 60%... make it 50%".
    Then another one about Flame Strike and runes ("press slot 6, then Runes, Flame Strike, Plain
    Fire Rune, then Save").
    Felt: like a maths lesson stacked on a coding lesson. I did them because I'm obedient, not
    because I understood why. I never once saw Pip in danger, so "wasted 28 HP" meant nothing.
    Screenshot: 06-eat-on-time-config.png

11. THE MOST IMPORTANT SENTENCE CAME 15 MINUTES IN
    Happened: a tip: "You can close the game: your hero keeps hunting until its stamina runs out,
    and what it earns waits for you."
    Felt: WAIT, WHAT. That changes everything about how I'd play. This should be on the very
    first screen, not 15 minutes in. Also I still didn't know what stamina meant here.

12. I MISSED MY LEVEL-UPS
    Happened: Level 5 happened while I had a window open. Next time I looked, the number had just
    changed. I did catch a floating "Level 3" and "Level 6" over Pip once each, which was nice,
    but there's nothing that stays to tell me "you levelled up, here's what you got".
    Felt: robbed of a little party.

13. LEAVING THE HUNT TAKES A WHILE
    Happened: pressed Leave hunt; it says "Leaving in 24s" and counts down, about 20 seconds in
    total. Nothing explains why I wait.
    Felt: a bit impatient, the only slow thing all night.

14. HUNT SUMMARY  <- LIKED IT
    Happened: clean card: time 17 min, 142 monsters, 547 gp. A big gold button "Next: Wear what you
    found" -> Equip upgrades -> done in one click (a Bronze Helm).
    Felt: easy and rewarding. That gold "Next:" button is the best thing in the UI.
    Screenshot: 07-hunt-summary.png

15. FEEDING THE HEARTH: NOTHING HAPPENED?
    Doing: tip said press Bones, then Fuel all.
    Happened: Pip WALKED across town (over a river, past a watermill, really pretty) to a big stone
    hearth... and stood there. No fire, no sparkle, no number. Status went back to "idle".
    The hearth even looks unlit. I don't know if it worked or what "Inner Flame" is.
    Felt: the walk was charming, the payoff was nothing.
    Screenshot: 08-great-hearth-nothing-happens.png

16. CLICKING A PERSON  <- LOVED THE IDEA
    Doing: I clicked the lady by the notice board, just to see.
    Happened: Pip walked over and "Tamsin's tally" opened: kill tasks for gold and armour. Clicking
    a person in the world WORKS. Great.
    But: every "Take" button was greyed out with no reason. I finally worked out I had to pick
    Heavy / Medium / Light "armor she pays you in" (which sits ABOVE the board) first. There's
    also a list "1. Head Earlier, 2. Body Earlier..." — what does "Earlier" mean?
    Took "200 Gray Wolves" in Meadowbrook Thicket.
    Felt: delight, then a frustrating minute.
    Screenshot: 09-tamsin-take-greyed-out.png

17. SELLING
    Happened: Shop -> Sell tab -> "Sell all loot · 129 gp" -> "Sell 6 stacks?" -> done. It kept my
    wheat and shrimp on purpose. Easy.

18. MEADOWBROOK THICKET + THE HOUSE
    Happened: a grassy path with bushes, wolves and lots of "miss" popping up. A little "Tally:
    Gray Wolves 1/200" box on screen, nice. I peeked at House: Wanderer's Camp -> Homestead ->
    Farmstead -> Manor -> Keep -> Hearthrise Castle, with a Garden, Kitchen, Forge, Library...
    Felt: THAT is a goal I understand. I want the castle. (Needs 20 Copper Ore and 30 logs, shown in
    red; no idea yet where those come from.)

19. CHAT
    Happened: typed "hi! anyone here from the stream?" -> shows up with a timestamp and my level.
    Nobody else around ("1 online"). The Combat tab says things like "Pip hits Someone for 1.",
    "Pip defeats Someone." — most monsters are called "Someone" (only Giant Rat had a name).
    Felt: lonely, and "Someone" made me laugh.
    Screenshot: 10-combat-log-someone.png

20. SECOND HERO (BRAMBLE)
    Doing: tip said I could run more heroes, so I made Bramble and picked red/yellow colours.
    Happened: grey knight again (colours ignored again). I wanted an archer, so I equipped the
    Practice Bow from the bag. Bramble turned into a blonde archer and the bottom bar rearranged
    itself by magic. Cool! Sent Bramble to the Mill Cellar. The hero tabs top-left (Pip / Bramble)
    and the "All characters" window are neat.
    Odd: the bow's tooltip lists "Melee accuracy +5, Melee damage +3".
    Odd: "2 online" — for a second I thought someone joined, but it's just my two heroes.
    Odd: money. "Bag 645 gp" / "Bag 26 gp" in one window, "Your gold 1,794 gp" in another, "shared
    purse" in the House. Where does my money live?
    Closed the browser for the night: Pip hunting wolves, Bramble hunting rats, turnips growing.


================================================================
EVENING TWO (came back "the next day", about an hour)
================================================================

21. COMING BACK  <- NICE
    Happened: signed in (fast). Characters screen: Pip Level 9 (was 6)! Bramble... Level 1?
    In town Pip and Bramble stand side by side, which is adorable. Tally says "Gray Wolves 200/200 ·
    Done. Visit Tamsin to be paid." A toast: "Bramble's hunt in Mill Cellar has ended."
    But I didn't get a "while you were away" story for Pip: why did the hunt stop, what did I earn
    overnight? I had to guess. (Could be my time-skip, see the bottom.)
    Screenshot: 11-evening2-return.png

22. BRAMBLE: 4 HOURS, ZERO KILLS, 4,524 GOLD GONE  <- I NEARLY QUIT HERE
    Doing: clicked Bramble's summary.
    Happened: "Your hunt was stopped because the server could not accept its last few minutes;
    nothing from them was counted." Time 4:00:18. Monsters 0. Level XP 0. Balance -4,524 gp.
    Supplies used: all 20 of my own Cooked Shrimp, 286 Salted Shrimp bought from the store, 52
    Turnip Juice bought from the store. The suggested "Next:" was "Defense isn't training" ->
    buy Leather Gloves.
    So my archer stood in a cellar for four hours being nibbled by rats, eating shrimp I paid for
    out of the money my OTHER hero earned, and never fired a single arrow.
    Why (I had to hover every slot to find it): slot 3 Precision Shot said "Needs ammo for each
    cast: you carry none your weapon shoots, and no ammo slot buys any. Put arrows or bolts on the
    bar (Ammo) to cast it." Slot 2 Flame Volley needed Fire runes I don't have. Meanwhile 50 Bronze
    Arrows were sitting in Bramble's bag the whole time. Equipping the bow built the bar for me but
    didn't put the arrows on it, and nothing warned me when I pressed Enter.
    Felt: gutted, and a bit cheated. The summary blamed "the server" and suggested gloves, which
    sent me the wrong way. A newcomer who doesn't hover every slot would just think the game is
    broken and leave.
    Screenshots: 12-archer-4h-zero-kills.png, 13-archer-dead-bar.png

23. FIXING IT MYSELF
    Doing: empty slot 7 -> Ammo -> Bronze Arrows -> Save. Back to the cellar.
    Happened: the hunt screen now says "Ammo: 50 Bronze Arrows of yours", and Bramble got 5 kills in
    30 seconds and was Level 5 after 23 minutes. The "Spells need runes" tip only showed up NOW,
    after the disaster.
    Felt: relieved, and a little proud I figured it out. But I shouldn't have had to.

24. TAMSIN PAID ME... NO ARMOUR
    Happened: "Tamsin paid Pip 480 gp and 5 Raw Wolf Meat for Gray Wolves." Both the tip and Tamsin
    herself promised "480 gp and a piece of armor you can wear". I'd picked Heavy. No armour.
    Felt: like I'd been short-changed.
    Screenshot: 14-tips-and-toasts-over-hero.png (the toast on the left)

25. WICKSTEAD  <- LOOT HIGH
    Happened: "A burned farm on the Thicket road. Rats in the granary, turncoats in the house." —
    best blurb in the game (though I mostly saw grass and a ploughed field, not a burned farm).
    20 minutes: 214 kills, and an Iron Platebody, a Studded Leather Body, an Adept Sash dropped!
    The Iron Platebody's tooltip was perfect: compares to what I wear, then "Needs Level 15 —
    you're 9". "Wear what you found" put on three Bronze pieces in one click.
    But: the "What next" tip box and the Tamsin toast sat on top of my hero for the whole hunt.
    The Tamsin toast never went away on its own: it was still there an hour later.
    Screenshot: 14-tips-and-toasts-over-hero.png

26. PARTY
    Doing: tip said right-click Bramble's tab -> Invite to party, "your own heroes join at once".
    Happened: "Bramble is hunting. Invite them once that hunt ends." Clear enough, but the tip made
    it sound instant. I gave up on parties.

27. COOKING AND FISHING  <- LOVED IT
    Happened: Cooking: Pip WALKED to a spit-roast campfire in town and the panel said "3 cooked so
    far, 1 burnt, 1 more on the fire". Fishing: Pip walked down to the river bank and stood fishing; "It
    keeps going while you are away".
    Felt: this is the cosy bit I came for. Best moment of the two nights.
    Small thing: the fishing panel said "2 caught so far · 0 in your bag" while my bag had 5 raw
    shrimp.
    Screenshot: 15-cooking-campfire.png

28. TIPS IN THE WRONG ORDER
    Happened: on evening 2 I got "Every weapon is a style" (a long box explaining sword/bow/wand
    etc. — it covered Pip completely) — AFTER my archer disaster. Then "Before the wolves: Out past
    the Cellar you can die... costs 2% of your Level XP... Blessings are free until Level 30" —
    AFTER I'd already done the wolves AND Wickstead. Blessings themselves were one click each in
    Shop > Services. Easy, I just wish I'd known on day one.

29. THE HELP PAGE IS THE BEST THING YOU'VE WRITTEN
    Happened: found Settings > Help by accident on evening 2. "Your hero fights by itself", "It keeps
    hunting with the game closed", "Stamina is hunting time", "Your weapon is how you fight".
    This is everything I needed on day one, in plain words. It's buried behind a gear icon.
    Also it says "A free hero holds up to 8 hours" of stamina, but my bar and the Shop said 12 h.


================================================================
SPEED / LAG
================================================================
Everything felt quick. Rough numbers from my session (local test server, so the live site will
add some network time):
- Page load about 1 s; sign in 0.2-0.3 s; Enter town 0.45-0.7 s; Enter a hunt 0.2-0.6 s;
  page reload back into a running hunt about 1.3-1.4 s.
- Hunts ran at a steady 60 fps; I never saw a stutter or freeze. No error messages at all.
- The only thing that felt slow was the ~20 s "Leaving in..." countdown, and the Mill Cellar
  sometimes left Pip standing alone for 10 seconds waiting for rats to come back.
(Fonts looked plain in my browser; I'm told that's my test setup, so I'm ignoring it.)


================================================================

  summary." and showed my PREVIOUS hunt's numbers while I was clearly still hunting (the XP ring

- Both heroes' overnight hunts ended with "the server could not accept its last few minutes;

- My turnips never ripened overnight; the skip doesn't move farm clocks.
- Pip's summary on evening 2 showed the old Mill Cellar run instead of the overnight wolf hunt.


================================================================
THE QUESTIONS
================================================================

Would I come back tomorrow, and why?
  Yes, probably. Pip is level 9 with an Iron Platebody waiting at 15, there's a castle to build,
  turnips to check, and the cooking/fishing bits are cosy. And I like that my heroes keep going
  while I'm away. But if I hadn't worked out the archer's arrows myself, I'd have written the game
  off as broken on evening two.

The one thing I'd tell the devs:
  Never let a hero start (or keep running) a hunt it can't fight in. If the bar can't attack (no
  arrows on it, no runes), say so in big letters when I press Enter, and stop the hunt after a
  minute of zero kills instead of quietly burning 4,500 gold for four hours.

What did I never understand?
  - The right side of the bottom bar: Setup/Preset 1, Target (Nearest / Lowest HP / Highest % life
    / Strongest / Follow the leader), Offensive/Balanced/Defensive, Chase vs Distance, Charm.
  - Action bar "conditions" and "tried after slot 2" order. Why I'd ever change them.
  - "Each pull", "packs", "As built", "DPS and HPS", "Costs are personal".
  - Inner Flame, Ward, Absorb, "Hit chance 62% against no armor". What the Great Hearth did.
  - "Earlier" next to each armour slot on Tamsin's board.
  - Where my gold lives (bag gold vs "Your gold" vs shared purse) and why one hero can spend the
    other's money.
  - Why my chosen colours vanished.
  - Stamina: 8 h or 12 h? What happens at 0?

When was I most tempted to quit?
  Reading Bramble's summary: 4 hours, 0 monsters, -4,524 gp, "nothing from them was counted",
  and the game's advice was to buy gloves. Runner-up: the "eat on time" maths-tip chain on night
  one.

Best moment?
  Pip walking over to the spit-roast in town and the panel saying "3 cooked so far, 1 burnt,
  1 more on the fire". Close second: the first fight in the Mill Cellar with "+15 XP" popping and
  "you don't click to attack" finally making it click.

Scores
  Fun:     7/10  (gorgeous, cosy, plays itself nicely; the archer disaster cost points)
  Clarity: 4/10  (great one-line quest box and Help page; everything else is jargon or late tips)
  Speed:   9/10  (snappy everywhere, steady 60 fps; only the leave countdown drags)

4. OPINIONS (mine, as the newcomer)
========================================================================
OPINION 1. The sign-in screen has a logo and two fields but no one-line pitch. A friend's link gives me no idea what I am about to play until I am inside.
OPINION 2. The character list's first line ("2 hero slots... up to 4 heroes can play at once... the third is 15,000") is account-management text before I have a hero. I would not read it.
OPINION 3. The first town is a zoomed-in empty cobbled square with "1 online". It does not show me a town. I have to trust the coach and open Hunts. The map I liked came later (the gate, the forge, the home).
OPINION 4. I never felt danger. From Lv 1 to 15 across five grounds my main hero (heavy armor, sword and shield) never showed an HP reading below about 85% (I sampled HP at about 20 moments, not continuously), and I never saw a near-death. The warning text on harder grounds is honest but I never saw it matter. A newcomer could learn nothing about blessings, food or armor from play.
OPINION 5. Gold piles up with little to spend on. At Lv 15 I had about 39,000 gp. The things to buy that I found were stamina (1,600 to 2,300 gp per 4 h), the House steps (2,500 gp next), the hero slot (15,000), and a few cheap accessories (120-360 gp). The House chain and stamina are real sinks; the rest of the shop felt like an afterthought for someone with this much gold.
OPINION 6. Armor never shows on the hero, so equipping upgrades is a stat change only. Fine in a text game, flat in a game that has a pretty pixel world.
OPINION 7. "Level XP" numbers are very large (173,424 after 7.5 h; "480 / 13,573 XP to Level 7"). I stopped reading them and watched the bar and the "Next level in" timer.
OPINION 8. Terms the game never introduces before using them: "Inner Flame" (it appeared as a skill after burning bones), "armor weight" (Heavy/Medium/Light), "Level XP" vs skill XP, "Dispatch loot", "Preset 1", "Tally".
OPINION 9. The first coach cards are written as steps and they work. The long ones (Tamsin, Before the wolves) read like documentation.
OPINION 10. The icon-only top bar (17 icons) depends on hover titles; the coach rings carry the first 10 minutes, but after that I had to hover to find Farm, House, Hunt prep.

8. VERDICT: WOULD I COME BACK TOMORROW?
========================================================================
Honest answer as this player: probably yes, once, to collect my hero's result, and then it depends on whether anything pulls me past the numbers.
Why yes: I closed the tab for 1 hour and for 8 hours and returned to exact, readable reports and real progress (Lv 4 to 6, 6,009 logs, ripe crops, Tamsin's payment). That is the best moment in the game and it works every time. The hunt view is alive and the first minute pays off ("Your first Level" at 43 s). Selling, equipping and the House ladder each told me what to press next.
Why it might not stick: the first ten minutes before the hunt are the weakest part (no pitch, a creation screen where my colours vanish and the button is below the fold, an empty zoomed town, "1 online").

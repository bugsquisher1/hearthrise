Hask's Ford, the walk grid (art lane b-hasks-ford-art, 2026-10-28; v2 organic, replaces the rejected rectangle grid).
The painted ground art/world/march/hasks_ford_ground (PixelLab map ee5f44c4) matches these rows cell for cell. The designer owns the final
rows (src/data/world/maps/); if they move, the paint moves with them.

Legend: # blocks a step and a shot (alder thicket wall, reed beds, barrow mounds)  . bank ground  ~ deep river (blocks)  : the ford (walkable)
Lint (scratch tool, same rules as KIT 4b): 886 open tiles, every one in an open 3x3, all reachable from the road in at (0,29-31).

The river comes in from the west at y11-16, bends down through the ford and leaves east at y12-15. ONE crossing: the Ford, x22-36 y18-24,
shallow water with two gravel bars (x25-29 y20-21, x31-33 y22-23), mud landings north (x27-31 y14-17) and south (x25-31 y25-28).
Pockets (irregular clearings):
  P0 the Pike Files  x3-18 y25-37   the road in from Wickstead enters at the west edge, y29-31
  P1 the Toll Stone  x19-37 y25-35  the south landing of the Ford
  P2 the Low Barrows x40-56 y23-37  barrow mounds split it (x45-47 y31-33, x52-54 y32-35) and a barrow bank on its north
  P3 the Company Stores x22-38 y3-15  the north landing of the Ford
  P4 the Muster Line x40-57 y3-11
  P5 the High Barrows x6-20 y4-11  ringed by barrow banks, a mound in the middle (x10-12 y4-6)
Lanes are 3 wide and wander; a 2-wide trampled mud track runs the route (road in > P0 > P1 > Ford > P3 > P5, P3 > P4, P1 > P2).

BRIEF 04: THE STAKE NETS (the Slake's second signature piece), the barge's spill, and the river kit's missing floor
pieces: one prop sheet; then the copper silt floor (plan lines A4c 12 and A4d 30 generations)
Art director, 2026-11-07.

PART 1, THE SHEET: create_image_pro_flash, 256x256, a 4x4 grid of 64 px cells on a transparent background, one prop
per cell, centred, nothing touching a cell edge. Two calls; I pick cells across both.
  view high top-down; single black outline; basic shading lit from the upper left; no shadows, no ground around props
  style image: refs/style-hf_pike_stakes.png (a cell of the March's own prop sheet, image 7bfee120: same hand, same
  wood). Upload with get_upload_url.
  palette muted: wet wood, rope tan, iron grey, silt grey-brown; verdigris (copper-green) only where named below.
  scale: the March sheet's props came out 34-58 px in 64 px cells; keep that. A stake stands 40-46 px tall.
  prompt: "a 4x4 grid of pixel art game props for a river mudflat where an army's dead lie, high top-down view, one prop
  per cell, transparent background: 1 two wooden fishing stakes with a long rotted net sagging between them and a green
  copper-crusted harness strap caught in the net, 2 the same stakes with the net torn and trailing, 3 the same net with a
  dented helmet and a pike head snagged in it, 4 one lone stake with net rags, 5 a heap of spilled coal and a split
  sack, 6 a broken wooden spar with rope, 7 a split barrel leaking black pitch, 8 loose broken hull planks, 9 a mooring
  post with a frayed rope, 10 a wicker eel trap on its side, 11 a patch of green copper stain on wet grey mud, 12 a
  smaller green copper stain with a line of half-buried rivets, 13 ripple marks in wet mud, 14 three rusted pike heads
  standing out of the mud, 15 a heap of green copper-crusted leather harness and buckles, 16 a round banded shield gone
  green, half sunk in mud"
  Reject a cell when: it is drawn corner-on (isometric), it has a painted shadow or a ground patch under it, a stake is
  under 36 px tall, or a decal (11-13) has a hard rectangular edge.

PART 2, THE COPPER SILT FLOOR: create_topdown_tileset, mode pro, 32 px tiles, view high top-down, transition 0.25.
The Slake's theme is its floor: "the Meadowbrook drops its copper here". Two chained pairs. Put the side that must not
drift on the LOWER side and check its fill tile's hash against the existing set (Muster lesson).
  set 1  lower = the March mud, lower_base_tile_id 2fdf9b47-aff6-4510-a1cf-8461af777554 (with the mud fill,
         refs/floor-ford-mud-64.png, as the reference image)
         upper = "wet river silt flat seen from above, smooth grey-brown mud with soft patches of green copper staining
         and faint ripple marks, muted, calm low contrast"
  set 2  lower = the March shallows, 40e321d1-9a47-474b-a38b-e5a96c8a9560 (refs/floor-ford-shallows-64.png)
         upper = set 1's new silt terrain id
  Reject when: the silt is brighter than the mud (props and actors must stay a value step above the floor, §4), the
  green covers more than about a fifth of a tile, or the mud or shallows fill comes back changed.
  Where it goes: art/world-src/gen-2026-11-07/silt/<tileset id>.json (the GET /v2/tilesets/<id> response, the input of
  port-pixellab.mjs paint). The ids join region-kits.js barrow_march.families.march after my yes.

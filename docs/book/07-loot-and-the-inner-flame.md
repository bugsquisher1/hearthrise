# 7. Loot and the Inner Flame

How drops work, how rare is rare, personal loot, remains, and the Inner Flame: your company's luck.

---

## 7.1 How drops work
**[Unchanged]**

- Every monster has its own loot list, with a chance for each item.
- Every drop has a home. The bestiary shows a monster's list, then the chances, as its page fills.
- The server rolls every drop. The browser never does.

## 7.2 Loot bands
**[Unchanged]**

Every drop on a ground sits in a band. The band is a promise about how often you see it.

| Band | How often | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 15% to 100% a kill | Hides, bones, ore, food |
| Uncommon | 5% to 15% a kill | Monster parts |
| Scarce | About one piece an hour at the floor | Mostly that ground's plain armor |
| Rare | 10 to 20 hunted hours per item | Gets a reveal |
| Key | 1.5 to 3 hunted hours | Hall keys and the like |
| Trophy | 100 to 150 hunted hours | Bosses only. Gets a reveal |

**Lucky finds.** A few monsters carry a lucky find as their last row: a piece the server announces itself when it drops, about 4 to 30 hunted hours apart.

**Field salvage.** Some monsters can drop a helmet, boots, gloves or a belt of their own tier, revealed as a Rare.

## 7.3 The reveal
**[Unchanged]**

- Every Rare drop gets the big reveal (Tyler kept it on every Rare).
- A Rare drop is also posted to the System chat for everyone, up to 3 a hero a day.
- The alert offers "Open your inventory" and "Share".

## 7.4 Hearthfinds
**[Unchanged]**

Once in a very long while the realm stops to look at what you found.

| Hearthfind | Comes from |
|---|---|
| Emberheart | The Elk King, the Grim Reaper, the Green Dragon |
| Worldroot Seed | Normal trees, Yew trees, Duskwood trees |
| Deepvein Lodestar | Copper rocks, Mithril rocks, Dawnstone rocks |
| Tidecaller's Pearl | Shrimp spots, Shark spots, Moonlit Pools |

- 100 to 400 hours of hunting or gathering at its source.
- Bound to you, worth nothing at the vendor. A moment, not money.
- Your first find unlocks the Hearth Plinth, a display in your house where your finds stand. All four earns the title **Wonderkeeper** ("The Four Hearthfinds").

Not final: the three Emberheart monsters aren't on any open ground, so it can't drop today.

## 7.5 Personal loot
**[New in 0.3]**

- Every hero rolls its own drops on every kill it was at.
- No rare roll and no waiting a turn. A Rare goes to the hero who rolled it, and the party feed says so.
- A party never drops more than its heroes would apart. Each hero rolls at its share of the kill, so the market isn't flooded.
- Each hero's Rare rolls read its own company's flame. A party of two accounts each chases on its own fire.
- Coin is still split evenly.
- **Pity:** a hero who keeps missing a Rare gets a slowly rising chance until it hits, so nobody's luck stays bad for long.
- A hired hero's loot goes to its owner.

Today's party loot (split by value, a dice roll for each Rare) goes away.

Not final: per-hero drop rates. Lane P1.

## 7.6 Your bag and loot rules
**[Unchanged]**

- Each hero has a bag. Storage holds the rest (chapter 13).
- **Loot rules** per item: keep, sell or burn. Set once in the plan.
- Plain gear you've outgrown sells to the store by rule.
- Every Trait piece is kept by default and shown on the return card.

**What the store pays for gear** (a share of its book value):

| Gear tier | Store pays |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | 50% |
| 3 | 14% |
| 4 | 7.5% |
| 5 | 3.3% |
| 6 | 1.5% |
| 7 | 0.7% |

Raw materials sell for 20% of their value.

# The Inner Flame

## 7.8 What it is
**[New in 0.3]**

Tyler's idea (2026-11-21): "Could we move luck to 'inner flame' and inner flame is explained as luck?" Approved.

**How it plays:**
- Your company has **one flame** at the Great Hearth. Every hero's Rare rolls read it.
- Remains from your hunts go on the fire by your plan's rule.
- **The taller the flame, the better your Rare odds.** Each bit of fuel adds less than the last, and the fire only holds so much.
- **The flame burns down only while your heroes hunt.** In town or away it holds its height. It never fades by the clock.
- **Everything it burns down raises your Flame Level.** A higher Level holds a taller fire that burns slower.
- Set the **stoke rule** once: keep it full, keep it at two thirds, or leave it out and sell your remains. "Tonight we chase drops" is a plan, not a chore.

## 7.9 The two numbers
**[New in 0.3]**

| | Flame height | Flame Level |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Fuel on the fire right now | A permanent record of fuel burned down |
| Goes up when | You burn remains | Height burns down while you hunt |
| Goes down when | Your heroes hunt | Never |
| What it gives | Rare chance | Room: a taller, slower fire |
| Top | The room your Level allows | 99 |

## 7.10 What it's worth
**[New in 0.3]**

At the starting values:

| Flame Level | Burns per hunted hero-hour | Rare chance on your own remains | Rare chance kept full |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8% of its height | +20% | +26% |
| 50 | 6% | +24% | +30% |
| 99 | 4% | +30% | +36% |

- A company that burns everything it drops sits at about two thirds of a full flame, which is most of the luck.
- Keeping it full (by buying remains, later) adds the last 6 points of chance, the smallest part.
- **Rank:** the flame also raises how often a Trait rolls at the highest rank the depth allows. Under depth 8 only rank I is open, so the flame does nothing for rank there.

**Flame Level pace.** A four hunting 8 hours a day burns about 32 points a day on its own drops, so Level 99 takes about 190 days. Kept full on a long Premium day with both Hunting blocks, about 63 days. Level 99 gives your house's fire a special color, nothing else.

**What it costs.** Burning all your own remains gives up about 5% of your coin. For gold alone the flame loses. It pays for a company that wants a Trait to wear. That's the choice.

Not final: every number above is a starting value for the Balance Lab.

## 7.11 What luck never touches
**[New in 0.3]**

- Which Trait rolls.
- Coin.
- Keys and chest prizes.
- Hall and world boss prizes.
- Signatures.
- A death never takes anything from the flame.
- It gives no regeneration and no combat power.

## 7.12 Named fuel bursts
**[New in 0.3]**

- A named fuel adds a burst to your flame and can stand over its room by that much. It burns down like any height.
- It still colors the town's fire for everyone.
- Named fuels trade, so gold can buy a burst. The burst is still bounded by your room.

## 7.13 Buying luck
**[Later patch]** (with the market)

- Tyler ruled that bought remains burn too (RD-Q1 b). Remains bought at the Weighhouse go on the fire like your own.
- Two limits keep it fair. Gold can keep a flame full but never taller than its Level allows. And Flame Level grows only while your company hunts, so bought fuel still needs hunted hours.
- Every drop chaser becomes a buyer of remains. Every leveler has remains to sell.
- Crowns sold for gold can reach drop luck this way. It's flagged for the pre-launch Security review.

0.3 has no market, so in 0.3 your flame burns your own remains only.

## 7.15 Today's Great Hearth
**[Changed]**

What exists today and changes in 0.3:
- Burning fuel at the Great Hearth trains the **Inner Flame** skill, which sets each hero's health and mana regeneration. That link is cut.
- Up to 20 named-fuel burns a day.
- **Hearth shows (gold sinks):** colored powder for the fire (Cinnabar red, Quicklime white, Borax green) at 2,000 to 10,000 gold, up to 5 a day. A round of sparks at 5,000 / 10,000 / 20,000 gold, up to 3 a day. Pure show, no power. **[Unchanged]**

## 7.16 Hearthrise, the town's fire
**[Unchanged]**

- A world event every 28 days, for 72 hours.
- Fuel burned at the Great Hearth grows the town's fire through five marks.
- When the Hearthrise lights, everyone earns more Level XP on every hunt until it ends, from +10% up to +25% with a full fire.

Not final: in 0.3, remains go to each company's own flame. Whether burning for your own luck still grows the Hearthrise fire isn't written down. See the contradictions list.

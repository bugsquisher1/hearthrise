# 3. Levels, skills and the Roads

How heroes grow: Level, the four combat skills, death and Blessings, training, and the Roads (the talent map).

---

## 3.1 Level
**[Changed]**

- **Level never caps.** Level 200 is the big one.
- Level XP comes from monsters your hero helps kill in hunts.
- **How a kill pays (new in 0.3).** A kill pays a share of what your hero's own Level takes, so the pace follows the Level table below at the ground's normal depth. Holding deeper pays more, and the bonus grows the deeper you hold (chapter 5 explains depth).
- A Level gives more health, more mana, a Roads point, and sometimes a new ground or move.

**The Level table** (hunted hours to reach a Level, for a mid-geared party of four):

| Level | About how many hunted hours |
|---|---|
| 5 | 0.25 |
| 10 | 6 |
| 25 | 36 |
| 50 | 168 |
| 100 | 600 |
| 200 | 1,680 |
| 300 | 3,120 |
| 500 | 6,720 |
| 750 | 13,440 |
| 1,000 | 23,520 |

At 10 hunted hours a day, Level 200 is about 168 days of play.

Not final: the redesign re-times every Level on the new scale. These are today's targets, and the first-hour Levels are estimates until they're measured on a fresh account.

## 3.2 The four combat skills
**[Unchanged]**

Each hero trains these by using them. They are the hero's own, never shared with the company.

| Skill | How it trains |
|---|---|
| **Melee** | Dealing damage with a sword, hatchets, a hammer or a club |
| **Ranged** | Dealing damage with a bow or crossbow |
| **Magic** | Spending mana on spells. Healers and mages earn Magic on every cast |
| **Defense** | Taking blows in armor. A shield block counts double |

- A skill makes that style of fighting stronger for that hero.
- Many moves unlock at a skill level, for example Frost Slam at Melee 12 and Spring Tide at Magic 20.
- Combat skills have no cap. Trades cap at 99 (chapter 9).

## 3.3 Inner Flame as a skill
**[Changed]**

- Today Inner Flame is a fifth combat skill. It sets how fast a hero gets health and mana back, and it trains by burning fuel at the Great Hearth.
- **In 0.3 it stops being a hero skill.** The flame becomes your company's **luck**, fed by the remains your hunts leave behind. It gives no regeneration and no combat power. Chapter 7 has the whole thing.

## 3.4 Death
**[Changed]**

**When a hero can die (new in 0.3).**
- On **Careful** and **Steady**, the party falls back before anyone dies. A death there is treated as a bug.
- Only **Bold** can die. A death is the price of a risk you chose.

**What a death costs.**
- 6% of the hero's total Level XP and 6% of each combat skill's progress.
- It can take a Level, but never below Level 1.
- The dead hero goes back to town. The rest of the party drops back about ten depths and plays one Risk setting safer for the rest of that hunt (Bold drops to Steady).
- If a death takes a Level, the hero's last Roads spends come off first. A "Spend as suggested" rule puts them back.
- A death never takes anything from your Inner Flame.

**In the Tower and the Catacombs** a death costs nothing (chapter 11).

## 3.5 Blessings
**[Unchanged]** (price rework planned)

A Blessing cuts what your next death costs. They are bought with gold before a hunt.

| Blessing | Cuts the death cost by | Price |
|---|---|---|
| First | 25% | 100 gold + 10 a Level |
| Second | 50% | 150 gold + 15 a Level |
| Third | 75% | 200 gold + 20 a Level |

- Free until Level 30.
- You need the one before to buy the next.
- A death uses them up.

Not final: the redesign wants one simple Blessing price that grows with Level, priced in hours of the wage (chapter 10). Lane SN prices it. Since only Bold can die now, Blessings matter less than before, on purpose.

## 3.6 Training
**[Unchanged]**

Training raises one combat skill without hunting. No Level XP, no coin, no drops.

| Place | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **The Training Yard** (opens at Level 10) | 50 gold + 6 a Level, each hour, paid at the start of the hour | Up to 12 hours a session. Uses no stamina |
| **Your house's dummy** (from the Homestead) | Bought once per skill: 150,000 / 250,000 / 400,000 / 600,000 gold | No hourly fee |

- One fixed rate for every skill and Level: 30,000 skill XP an hour.
- A training item doubles that rate while its hours last. Training items from the store can't be traded.
- The dummy never falls, and a hero never drops under half health against it.

Not final: the redesign folds training into town work as "one more thing a resting hero can do". The 0.3 plan doesn't say when.

# The Roads

## 3.7 What the Roads are
**[New in 0.3]**

The window is called **Talents**. Inside it is **the Roads**: a map of the country around Havora. The Great Hearth sits in the middle of town and roads lead out of it.

- Every Level from 2 gives **one point**.
- You spend a point on any spot (a "node") that touches one you already own.
- Moves get stronger as you walk their road, and at forks they **change how they play**.
- Two heroes with the same weapon end up different.

## 3.8 The map
**[New in 0.3]**

- **The Great Hearth** is where every hero starts, for free. It holds Mend's road and the Havora crossing.
- **Seven countries**, one per weapon way: Shield, Sword, Club, Dual Axe, Ranged, Destruction and Restoration.
- **The Hearth**, the support's country, sits beside the Great Hearth. It ships with the wraps (chapter 4). It may slip to 0.3.1.
- **Shared moves sit where their weapons meet.** Turning Cut lives in Club. Flame Strike and Power Strike live in Sword. A Shield hero reaches them through Havora.

## 3.9 The kinds of node
**[New in 0.3]**

| Node | Looks like | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rank stone | Small square stone | +1 rank of the move whose road it's on | 1 point |
| Crossroads | Diamond | Pick 1 of 3. Changes how the move plays | 1 point |
| Waystone | Ringed dot off the road | A small rule | 1 point |
| Border town | Gabled house on a border road | Use one move from the next country with any weapon | 1 point |
| Hybrid crossroads | Diamond with a diamond inside | Pick 1 of 3 that mixes two countries. Needs a crossroads in each | 1 point |
| Vow | Standing stone | A strong rule with a cost. Takes a vow slot | 1 point |
| Open road | Three dots | +1 rank of that move per point, forever | 1 point each |
| Final form | Diamond at the end of a road | Level 200. Pick 1 of 3 on one move | 1 point |
| Milepost | Stone with a number | Level 250, 300, 350 and on. Pick 1 of 3 | 1 point |

## 3.10 How a road is laid out
**[New in 0.3]**

Each move's road goes:
1. Three rank stones.
2. **Crossroads I** (pick 1 of 3).
3. A waystone off to the side.
4. Two more rank stones.
5. **Crossroads II** (pick 1 of 3).
6. Another waystone.
7. The open road, forever.

The next move's road leaves from crossroads I.

## 3.11 Vows
**[New in 0.3]**

A vow is a big gain on a condition and a cost that always bites.

- **Vow slots:** 1 at Level 30, 2 at Level 50, 3 at Level 200.
- Each vow sits beside its country's first crossroads. Each country has 4 (the Hearth has 5).
- Every vow is good somewhere and bad somewhere else. Across all grounds it comes out about even.
- "Spend as suggested" **never** takes a vow. It walks past and offers the vow on your return card as one of your three choices.
- A vow's cost never lowers max health.

Examples:
- **Hold Fast** (Shield): enemies on you can't switch targets · −3% damage dealt.
- **Lifeline** (Restoration): ally near fall-back, your next heal is instant and free · 10 s per ally · −3% healing.
- **Open Wounds** (Dual Axe): Bleed stacks up to 10 · −3% damage dealt.
- **Cold Focus** (Destruction): +20% frost spell damage · +2 s Chill · can't cast fire spells.

Every vow is listed in chapter 15.

## 3.12 Borders and borrowing
**[New in 0.3]**

- **Border towns** let a hero use one move from a neighboring country with any weapon.
- **The Havora crossing** offers any move from any country, but with a harsher penalty.
- **Two borrowed moves at most**, counting the crossing.
- A borrowed move costs power by how strong it is. A weak move borrows with no penalty. A strong one comes in at −30% or −50%. The strongest can't be borrowed at Havora at all.
- A borrowed move opens at a Level set by how strong it is, from Level 20 up.

| Move | Penalty at a border town | Opens at Level |
|---|---|---|
| Challenge | None | 20 |
| Hunter's Mark | None | 30 |
| Frost Lance | None | 60 |
| Fireball | −50% | 80 |
| Shelter | −30% | 120 |
| Hoarfrost | −50% (not at Havora) | 120 |
| Protective Ward | −50% (not at Havora) | 120 |
| Greater Mend | Waiting on a test | 80 to 150 |

**Rules for a borrowed move:**
- It hits like the native weapon's normal blow at your Level, never your own weapon's. A club is never the best healer.
- A borrowed heal shares one 6-second clock with Mend.
- It uses its own rune or arrows.
- A borrowed spell trains Magic at Mend's rate.

**Nine borders exist.** Club and Dual Axe, Dual Axe and Ranged, Ranged and Destruction, Destruction and Shield, Shield and Restoration, Restoration and Sword, Sword and Club, Destruction and Restoration, Sword and Shield. The Hearth adds two more (Hearth and Shield, Hearth and Restoration). Every other pair is Havora only. Chapter 15 lists each border's options.

Not final: whether a borrowed Greater Mend ships in 0.3. If its test fails, Greater Mend can't be borrowed in 0.3.

## 3.13 Numbers grow with Level
**[New in 0.3]**

- Every number on a talent node is shown at full size in the design. A hero gets a share of it by Level: **Level ÷ 200**, full at Level 200.
- So at Level 30 a node gives 15% of its full number. At Level 100, half. At 200 and on, all of it.
- Rules (who it hits, what it unlocks) are whole from the start.
- The card always shows your hero's own number.
- **Level 200 is the day your build is full size.** It also opens the third vow slot and one final form.

This growth applies only to what a hero grows into (talents, vows, borrowed moves, consumables). It never applies to gear, Traits or Signatures, so a lucky drop counts in full at any Level.

## 3.14 How much a build can matter
**[New in 0.3]**

- All the talent picks on a hero at once can add at most **about 10% to your pace**. Past that, the game clamps it.
- So two vows on the same condition hit the cap. A third vow buys coverage of another condition, not more size.
- The best build for a ground beats the suggested one by 5 to 10%, never more.
- No build is meant to be best everywhere. Every build is meant to be top three somewhere.

## 3.16 Changing your mind
**[New in 0.3]**

- **Drafting is free.** Plan changes as long as you like.
- **Applying a draft is a reset**, and a reset costs gold.
- The price is 0.5 hours of the wage at your hero's Level, doubled for each reset that hero already made that server day (0.5, then 1, then 2 hours of wage).
- A new hero's first reset is free.
- **A release that adds nodes gives every hero one free reset**, good until the next release.

Not final: the reset price. Lane SN sets it.

## 3.17 Saved builds
**[New in 0.3]**

- Every hero keeps its current build and **one saved build** for free.
- The plan can name a saved build per ground. At the Start, the plan applies it if needed, inside a price limit you set, and the return card says what it paid.
- A build can be copied from another hero or a ladder entry, for free, cut to the hero's points.
- A build never holds more points than the hero has.
- **Share codes** let you paste a build from another player.

**[Later patch]** Premium adds a second saved build per hero and one free reset per hero per day. More slots cost Crowns (chapter 14).

## 3.18 Theorycrafting tools
**[New in 0.3]** where noted

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Build planner | Draft a whole build past your Level and save it. Plan a route to a far node |
| Share codes | Copy and paste a build. Ladder entries get "Copy build" |
| Test on the dummy | Run a draft on the training dummy and read the hunt analyzer. No XP or loot |
| Builds like yours | Depth held, XP an hour, healing, mana and supplies for the nearest known build on each ground. Every number marked as an estimate |
| Details | Exact numbers on tooltips |

Not final: the build planner and the "Builds like yours" table are display tools on the backlog. 0.3 ships share codes. The others may follow.

## 3.19 Past Level 200
**[New in 0.3]**

- A **Milepost** every 50 Levels (250, 300, 350 and on). Pick 1 of 3: a new waystone or a third crossroads on a move you use, from the latest release.
- Points keep flowing into open roads, which have no top.

## 3.22 Choices you never repeat
**[New in 0.3]**

| Where you could repeat yourself | What remembers it |
|---|---|
| Spending a point every Level | "Spend as suggested", or a saved plan's order |
| Taking a vow | Asked once on the return card |
| The same build on a second hero | Copy build, or a share code |
| Changing builds for a ground | A saved build per ground in the plan |
| A death takes a Level | The last spend comes off and the rule puts it back |
| New content | The rule follows the suggested road. One free reset per release |

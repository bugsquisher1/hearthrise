# Balance program 30: the plan for the whole game

Phase 3, the synthesis (2026-11-20). It reads the eight dives (`21` to `28`), the model (`01`), the real-player baseline (`10`), the first audit and Tyler's rulings of record up to today's "FOUR DIVES APPROVED". Nothing in the game was changed to write it. Every decision in it is also a row of `30-decisions.json`, which feeds the ruling page.

Labels, as in every program file: **REAL**, **MEASURED**, **CITED** (a dive's number, with its file), **RULED**, **COMPUTED** (arithmetic here on cited numbers, not played), **ASSUMED**, **PROPOSED**. Nothing in this file was measured by me. Where a total is COMPUTED the balance guard of section E is what measures it for real.

## A. One screen

### What the game is after these changes

You start alone. Your first hour is a Level every few minutes, and the end of every hunt sells your junk and tells you what to buy next. Levels 7 to 10 no longer take four hours each. A second clock, the Bestiary, pays Luck every time you finish a creature's stage, so there is always something about to land.

You come back twice a day for a few minutes. One press, **Carry on**, sends every hero back to what it was doing. The card only stops you for things that need a decision: better gear, a new ground, a full bag.

Your first big gold goal is a second hero (400,000 gold, about Level 33). A pair, then three, then your own four by about week 11. Friends skip that wait: four people are a party on day one, and their heroes can hunt with yours while they are offline.

Deadly is a real fight. A party at the right Level holds it with a bigger food bill. A party ten Levels under dies. Gold has things to want at every Level: refills that cost more each time, linings, the House, Tempering, the Tower's chest pieces you carry up with keys.

Pace of record (COMPUTED, ASSUMED pair at 1.2 and three at 2.0 times the lone table): alone to Level 33 in about 68 hunted hours, Level 50 at about 140, Level 110 at about 390, Level 185 at about 740. That is day 43, day 119 and day 226 for the Own four. The Heavy four (8.4 hours a day) reaches Level 110 on about day 46. So **the Morrow Hills must be open about six weeks after a wipe**, or the first players run out of game.

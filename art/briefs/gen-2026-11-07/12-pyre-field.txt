THE FLOOR DECIDES EVERYTHING ELSE, SO IT IS MADE FIRST, AND IT IS PALE
 The ash floor does not exist yet. Its value is the one choice that makes burnt-black bodies readable or not, so P1's
 first pair is the first call of the brief, and every body is checked against it. Measured
 (tools/world-art/ring-contrast.mjs maths, palette values; sheets in art/review/brief-12-2026-11-08/):
   ring median             on ash stone #8a867d (L 0.239)   on stone #6a6865 (0.139)   on the March mud (0.051)
   wight 0.027                        3.74                        2.44                        1.31
   barrow_knight 0.051                2.87                        1.87                        1.00
   provost re-pour 0.022              4.03                        2.63                        1.40
   black outline 0.003                5.44                        3.56                        1.91
 On a dark floor a black body vanishes, which is why ART_DIRECTION §4 measures the ring inside the outline. On a pale
 floor the dark body and its black outline are the silhouette, so burnt-black bodies pass by being darker. Real wood ash
 is pale grey, so this is also the truer floor. It makes the Pyre Field the one pale ground of the March: black figures
 on grey ash, recognisable from a screenshot (CLAUDE.md §10).
 THE RULE, for this ground:
   ash ('.', where every pocket fights)  fill median on stone #8a867d or lighter, never darker than #6a6865 in any pixel
                                         run wider than 2 px. Warm grey, low contrast, no white (#aaa49a only in
                                         specks).
   scorched earth (':', the burn lines)  one value step darker, median on #6a6865 (L 0.14), warm brown-grey.
   charred wood ('#') and cinder pits ('o') block a step, so nobody stands on them: they may go near black.
   Every body (new, re-poured, or placed here later) passes ring-contrast.mjs at 3:1 against refs/floor-pyre-ash-64.png
   (a 64x64 crop of P1 pair 1's ash fill, saved by the generator), with its ring median at or below 0.045 (the palette's
   earth #3d2a1d, iron #2c2e33 or the shadow ramp). Mid-brown (earth #553a26 and up) fails by design.
 Heroes: on the ash a steel knight's ring is about 1.1:1, but its black outline is 5.4:1, so it reads by its outline.
 FLAGGED to the designer: P5's pull tile (30,24) sits on the burn line (x29-30 are ':'), where a black body reads 2.6:1.
 Moving it to (32,24) puts the whole box on ash (rows 23-25, x31-33 are '.'). Not changed here.

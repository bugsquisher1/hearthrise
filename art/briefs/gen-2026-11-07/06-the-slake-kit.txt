WHAT THE KIT COVERS FOR THE SLAKE (0 generations):
  the floor's river, shallows, gravel, mud and reeds (13 March pro tilesets; port-pixellab.mjs paint from the merged
    art lane; 0 gens). Deep water never meets mud in the kit (no river/mud pair): where the Slake's silt bars meet a
    channel, paint a one-cell shallows lip (mud/shallows and river/shallows pairs exist). Paint the edges organic:
    the walk grid's rectangles (the shallows crossings) must not show as rectangles in the paint (the Ford's gravel bars
    do, read 11-07).
  props: hf_pikes_fallen, hf_banded_shield, hf_helm_heap, hf_pike_stakes, hf_rowboat_sunk, mu_dead_hawthorn,
    mu_shields_leaning, mu_crates, mb_reeds, westwood_ash, and the clutter. Not `oak` (read 11-07: its light round
    canopy is Greenhaven's beside the March's alder; trees.png).

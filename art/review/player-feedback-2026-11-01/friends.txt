What grouping does, as we read it
- Level XP and kill count were identical for every member at every read. Gold was close, loot differed person to person.
- A Level 1 got the same XP as Level 7s: 2,034 XP and Level 1 to 5 in about 10 min.
- Who pays: "Costs are personal." In the 8-per-pack three, at 3:52: Bram 48 gp, Wren 1,030 gp, Sol 2,165 gp. Sol's supplies hit 1,172/h in the first half hour. The healer's runes keep the tank alive and the healer alone pays.

Does the game say beforehand what grouping does to earnings?
- No line we could find says it. The Hunts window showed only: drop chance "in a party of 3" (Mill Cellar helmets 1% vs 0.65% alone; Queen's Comb 0.11% vs 0.072%); "Bigger pulls pay more once your heroes can hold them"; "Costs are personal" (in the hunt).
- Reading the whole Hunts window, as leader and alone, found no line about sharing or splitting XP or gold.
- The only comparison is after the fact: "Best alone" and "Best in a party" under "Your best here", once you have tried both.

4. HOW SOMEONE WITH A NAME GETS INTO A PARTY (presses)
  Name in chat:     right-click, Invite to party = 2. Receiver: Join = 1. Total 3.
  Friend:           N, Invite to party = 2. Receiver: Join = 1. Total 3.
  Typed name:       Y, Invites tab, type, Invite = 3 + typing. Receiver: Join = 1. Total 4 + typing. On a first open Y lands on Find a party, where there is no name box.
  Then starting:    Y, Pick a hunt for the party, ground, Start with party = 4. If the leader already has the Hunts window, 3.
  Party of 3 from nothing, by chat name: 3 + 3 + 4 = 10 presses.
  Trap, invites crossing: anyone who has sent an invite already owns a party. +2 or 3 presses (Y, tab, Leave party, Join again).
  Regrouping from solo hunts: both must Leave hunt first. Each waits out a countdown; once we still read "Hunting" 16 s after the press. Invite buttons stay lit and answer "Not while a hunt is running."
  Name typed: case-insensitive. A typo says "No character goes by that name."

6. WEIGHHOUSE AND GIVING TO A FRIEND
HARNESS NOTE: on this local world the Weighhouse first said "isn't trading right now." I set hr_market_config.enabled = true in the local database so we could reach it. Not a game bug.
- Level gate: "The Weighhouse trades with heroes of Level 10 or higher." The first friends got there around 7:49, helped by hunting well above their level. The wording suggests any hero on the account counts ("Reach it on any hero"); Sol bought as Solace, so not tested from Sorrel.
- Trade worked: Wren listed an Adept Robe Top at 143, fee 2 gold. Sol bought it mid-hunt into her bag. Wren received 137. History on both sides reads correctly.
- Wren was not told when it sold. Nothing popped up; her gold moved and History had a line.
- Giving: there is no way we could find. The name menu has Inspect gear, Message, Add friend, Invite to party, Block.
- Listing cheap is refused: "The shop pays 137 for one, and nothing lists below that... ask 143 or more." The listing shows in Browse for everyone, so a Level 10 stranger could presumably buy it first (not tested). A friend's "gift" costs the buyer 143, the seller 2 gold and the 4% cut. The Sell tab says "gifts" can't be sold, but we never saw a way to send one.

Not bugs, but they hurt (OPINION)
- The leader's pick drags everyone. Only the leader sees the red warning. Wren picked the Glade, Sorrel was Level 9, was never asked, and died in seconds at a cost of 3,127 Level XP.
- "Leave if anyone leaves the hunt or dies" is on by default. One person going to dinner sends the group home. Only your own box is shown, and it is small.
- The Glade at Level 7 in starter and bronze gear paid 35,296 XP/h for 4 h 21 min with no deaths. The Thicket "in your range" paid 13,839. The pull setting nearly doubled it again (35,296 as a four to 64,346 as a three at 8 per pack). The "above your level" warning did not stop us and hunting above level was simply better while a healer was with us.
- A Level 1 second hero earns the full share. Sol's account earned double, and the seat cost Bram and Wren about 13% XP in the Thicket.

- A first friends-list invite right after leaving a hunt produced no invite and I did not capture the message; probably the leave countdown. A retry worked. No "Invite sent" line was visible there 1 s after pressing, unlike the typed path.


9. PERFORMANCE WITH SEVERAL TABS
Three to four separate browser contexts in one headless Chromium, on a PC also running other work.
- Frame rate read 48 to 61 fps on all pages during hunts. One dip to 48, once.
- JS heap 15 to 32 MB per page; 830 to 1,237 elements (one sample, late in the session).
Headless has no GPU work to speak of and the local database is one backend, so this says nothing about 15 real players on a real server. Unreached in any honest sense.


10. TOP 5 TO FIX, RANKED
1. Say what grouping does to earnings before people find out. In the Mill Cellar at the default pull, the same three people earned 35 to 48% less XP and money per person together than apart, and no line warns them. At 8 per pack in the Glade the gap closes and the healer is better off. The default and the pull setting decide everything, and the pull setting sits deep in the Hunts window.
2. Stop panels landing off-screen below about 1600 px wide. At 1280x720 or a 1366 laptop, nobody can see their friends' HP or their own XP/h.
3. Role and outfit frozen at join. Equip-after-invite is exactly what new friends do, and the first party in the game shows three identical knights labeled the same.
4. Group flow traps: crossing invites ("already in a party"), silent refusal, silent removal, no inviting mid-hunt, no switching ground without everyone stopping, default "one leaves, all go home", and a friend's stamina reported as "left the hunt" with no warning first.
5. The away card: counts the whole hunt instead of the absence, stays on "Still hunting" after the hunt ends, and blocks the Chat tabs and the Party panel until dismissed.


11. VERDICT
Probably yes for next week, but out of friendship more than because the game rewards it. The first ten minutes were lovely: we could see each other in town, three presses got someone into a party, the hunt is lively, and returning to a running hunt after a closed browser felt like the promise of the genre. Then the numbers bite. "Best alone 7,255, Best in a party 3,902" is the line that would stop a voice call, and the healer can see she is paying for everyone's survival. The Glade with a healer was safe and fast, but we found that by ignoring a warning and pressing a + deep in a window, and a laptop player could not even see the numbers that told us. If grouping is explained up front, panels stay on screen and the small silences (refused, removed, stamina out) are fixed, these three keep playing together. As it stands, one of them leaves for solo by the weekend and the others follow when the healer's bill comes due. This whole paragraph is OPINION; we did not play a real week.

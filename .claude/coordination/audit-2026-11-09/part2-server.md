## Done well

- **One gold path.** Every gold move goes through `hr_purse_move` under the account purse row lock, with a fixed lock order. The `hr_ledger_stamp_purse` trigger refuses a ledger row whose stated purse is not the wallet's balance, so the ledger equals the purse by construction (2026-12-15-account-gold.sql R1-R7).
- **The money RPC pattern is followed.** Stamina refill, scaled hunts, the Crier, titles and setup slots price from a server catalogue and refuse `quote_changed`. Refills, scaled hunts, the Crier and Town Works also clamp per day under the account lock (titles and slots are bounded ladders). Each journals one aggregate row, and the town sinks count Gold Given inside the same transaction. Scaled-hunt hours are spent only by the server's own count of world swings.
- **One combat engine.** `simulateHuntInstance` (`src/core/combat-sim.js`) is the only loop. The edge settle and the browser's live view import the same function, seeds are HMAC-keyed per swing and domain, an unseeded swing throws, and there is no away mode.
- **The edge cannot write.** `hr_engine` holds no table privilege. The settle reads no client value beyond `slot`, and `hr_hunt_apply_instance` re-proves every member's window, the kill and value bounds, and store prices in one subtransaction.
- **Grant hygiene is executable and nightly.** `hr_assert_grant_hygiene` pins the engine allowlist by `regprocedure`, catches procedures and ungated definer RPCs, and runs from cron. It is clean in the replay.
- **The GM wall is default-deny.** `hr_access_check` refuses a walled account anything not on `hr_gm_wall_allow`, grants take no account argument, walled heroes are put in `hr_lb_hidden`, and 19 shared tables (market, parties, Town Works, town shows, Gold Given, friends, world finds) carry wall triggers.
- **Journals stay aggregate.** One ledger row per purchase or settle, 90-day retention with roll-ups, immutable inside the window. The `game_events` lesson is applied everywhere new.
- **The chain replays.** 616 files applied cleanly with their §4 self-checks.

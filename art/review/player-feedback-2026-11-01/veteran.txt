NUMBERS THAT ADD UP (I checked; this is good and a veteran notices)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Loot lines sum to the loot value (26 lines = 11,664). Balance = gold + loot - supplies. Per-hour
figures match totals over time. Supplies = count x price (87 x 14 + 10 = 1,228). Gold after the
hunt = before + earned - supplies (1,219 + 16,211 - 1,368 = 16,062). Worth drops by the bid of
what you wear (35 + 90 + 65 + 25 = 215). Roll of Honor XP = sum of my hunts (7,546 + 255,743 =
263,289). Death: 5,394 + 161 + 63 = 5,618 on the Deaths tab. Set bonus 4.6% x 6 = 28%. Analyzer and
summary agree to within the seconds between reading them. The item tooltip is excellent.

7. Absorb 49.5% at L11 and 45.3% at L14 with the same four pieces on. Nothing says why. The tooltip

did not test a healer, a mage, a bigger pull or the Warrens, and those may change it. The flow
itself is clean: right-click, Invite, Join, Start with party. Leader leaves, everyone goes home,
and the summary says why.

----------------------------------------------------------------
DANGER
----------------------------------------------------------------
I did not die once by playing. L10 with no armor survived "above your level, expect losses" and
earned 54k XP/h. The Sewer ("A party of 2 to 4") was soloable at L10 with no armor.
To get a death I needed: under-leveled, no armor, a bow with no arrows so no attacks, and ALL food
slots emptied. Then I died within minutes.
The death itself is good: clear modal, killed by what, cost itemized, back in town at full health.
5,394 Level XP looks like 2% of lifetime XP (about 270k). XP 27% to 20%, level kept.
Opinion: the only risk in the game I saw is the shop bill. A Tibia player will find that strange.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PACING AND THE CHASE
----------------------------------------------------------------
XP per level: L1 127, L5 6,564, L10 72,045, L11 72,549, L14 80,109. It climbs 11x in five levels,
then goes nearly flat. Hours per level at my best rate: L5-L6 about 17 min, L10-L14 about 1.4 h in
the Glade.
Stamina caps real play at 12 h per day plus refills. 20 hunting hours is about two real days, not
two weeks.

HOUR 5: Strong. Thicket pays 3x the Cellar, armor pieces drop, the hero looks like he's winning.
HOUR 12: Weak. The Weighhouse opens at L10, which is a real moment. But I hit the stamina wall,
  levels slow to 3 h each at Thicket rates, and the only signpost is a label telling me not to go
  to the ground that would double my XP. I found that by ignoring the warning.
HOUR 20: Thin in what I played. L15-ish, 27k gold before the slot. Visible goals: L32 for the
  Warrens (about 30 more hours at Glade rates, my estimate), 40,000 for slot 4, Melee 30 for a
  better whetstone, the bestiary (13 of 30 at hour 13), rare drops at 0.07-0.3%. Nothing changes what my hero does
  hour to hour. If the middle game is meant to be trade skills, nothing pulled me there; the one
  nudge I saw was "Plant your land".

that I can see, but I didn't test deleting and remaking.

BUGS (repro, then what I saw)
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Fuel keeps burning into the next hunt, and the loot still counts it.
   Press Bones > Fuel all, let it run partway, then enter a hunt. 11 h later: every Bone from the
   hunt gone, Inner Flame up 11 levels, summary still says Bones x1,954 in "unsold" loot value. No
   word anywhere that this happened. The burn stopped after that hunt: the next hunt kept 72 Bones.
   Inferred from before/after, I didn't watch the job run. Inner Flame +24% regen is a nice gift;
   the unasked spend and the overstated balance are the bug.
2. Sewer's rule text is wrong. It reads "A party of 2 to 4." A lone L10 walks in and it's the best
   money on the list. Compare Warrens: "A party of 2 to 4, or alone from Level 45", and it does
   gate me.
3. Bow with no arrows can still Enter. Prep warns clearly, then nothing on the hunt screen. Sorrel
   lost 10 min of stamina and 784 gp to 56 shrimp before "You went 10 minutes without landing a
   blow, so you headed home." The safety stop is good; a warning on the hunt screen is missing.
4. No weapon at all: prep says nothing, the style reads "Melee", and he fights with fists.
5. Tamsin task not on the chosen ground gives no warning. I ran 11 h at 0/120 Giant Rats, shown in
   the Tally box the whole time, because the list had changed its pick.
6. Spell detail text: "Requires: Bow or Crossbow or Bow or crossbow."
7. Tamsin's armor slots each read "Earlier", which looks like a status but seems to be a reorder
   button.
8. Find a party's second filter showed "Needs a shield" before I touched it, though "Any party" is
   in the list.
9. Hunts "+" for pull size: pressed 8 times in the Glade, the label stayed "As built" and "6 packs of
   2 to 3". Can't tell if it did anything. Not sure this is a bug.
10. "3 online" with 2 people signed in. It appears to count heroes.
11. HP stayed 250/296 after 8 h in town, before and after a reload, following a max HP rise from
    armor. It filled once a hunt began. Might be intended; looks wrong.

----------------------------------------------------------------
TOP 5 TO FIX, RANKED
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Level bands don't mean anything. "Expect losses" is the best XP on the list and it's safe, even
   unarmored. Make the warning true with real danger, or say what it really costs.
2. Parties earn less per head than solo: -27% XP, -13% coin on the same ground. Fix this before a
   multiplayer playtest, or tell players which composition pays.
3. Hero power doesn't move income. Kills/h is set by the ground, across level, weapon, skill and
   armor. A min-maxer has nothing to optimize but ground choice and the food bill. Verify with a
   real weapon upgrade first; I didn't test one.
4. Fuel: stop the job when a hunt starts (or ask), and take burned Bones out of "unsold" loot value.
5. The hour 12 to 20 chase. Gold has one real sink and drops are vendor stacks. Give a mid-game
   target that changes what the hero does, and point at it on screen. Trade skills may be the
   answer; I never felt pulled to them, and I didn't play them.

VERDICT (opinion): The arithmetic is honest and the screens explain themselves, better than most
idle games I've read. But the first 20 hours have no danger, parties pay less than solo, and hero
power barely shows in the numbers. I'd keep reading, and I'd stop trusting the labels by hour 12.

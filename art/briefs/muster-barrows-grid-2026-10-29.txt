The Muster Barrows, the walk grid (art lane b-muster-barrows-art, 2026-10-29; organic, the Barrow March's second ground, 55-75 party).
The painted ground art/world/muster/muster_barrows_ground matches these rows cell for cell (tools/world-art/port-pixellab.mjs paint
reads the PAINT rows below). The designer owns the final rows (src/data/world/maps/); if they move, repaint from this file.

Legend: # blocks a step and a shot (alder thicket, barrow mounds, reed beds)  . open ground (withered grass, the mud track)
Lint (scratch tool, the KIT 4b rules): 1070 open tiles, 1070 of them in an open 3x3, 1070 reachable from the road in at (0,8-11).

The road comes in from Hask's Ford on the west edge (y8-11), from the Ford's Muster Line. No river: a field of burial mounds in
the alder wood behind the ford, where the Banded Company buried its dead and never burned them. Larger pockets than the Ford (a party
ground), joined by 3-5 wide lanes; a 2-wide trampled mud track runs two routes (road in > P0 > P1 > P4, and P0 > north lane > P2 > P4)
and a spur P1 > P5. Islands of thicket break every clearing; no pocket is a rectangle and no two are the same shape.
Pockets (irregular clearings):
  P0 the Muster Field       x1-19 y4-16   the open field where the company mustered; an old barrow in its north-west corner
  P1 the Long Barrows      x19-37 y13-29 two long mounds lying north-west to south-east, the track in the sunken lane between them
  P2 the Sergeants' Row    x34-58 y3-15  four round barrows along the north edge, the mounted sergeants' (Barrow Knights)
  P3 the Wet Hollow        x4-18 y24-38  a reed bed in the middle; reached down a lane from P0
  P4 the Captain's Barrow  x37-57 y21-38 the great mound (x45-55 y31-36) and its forecourt, ringed by standing stones: Captain Hask
  P5 the Biers             x19-33 y29-38 the company's dead laid out for a pyre nobody lit; a wet patch

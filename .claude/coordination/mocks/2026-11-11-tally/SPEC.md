# Tamsin's tally: the window, rebuilt as her ledger (mocks, 2026-11-11)
Tyler 2026-11-11: "Can we revamp the Tamsin's Tally board? It seems plain / confusing and boring", then "I'm okay with the rewards, but the menu / ui itself sucks ass." So this lane changes only the window. What she pays and the task rules stay exactly as they are.
Captured on set/v0.2.121 with `tools/qa/local-world.mjs`: a fresh hero takes the rats, hunts the Mill Cellar, gets paid in town, then takes three tasks. Shots are at 1440x900 and 922x423 (touch). Files: `before/` (real screens, plus `before/dom/*.txt` with the window's text), `after/` (the mock body injected into the real window on the same live page; `*-win.png` is the window alone), and `compare-desktop.png` / `compare-phone.png` (today and the mock side by side). `mock.css` is the proposed `.ht-*` block and uses tokens only. `tools/` holds the three scripts that made all of this (edit ROOT to re-run).

**Problems, worst first (evidence: before/).**
1. **Your own task is not what you see first.** Opening the window shows 5 lines of intro, then the gold, then the board. The text is 282 words on a fresh open (`dom/d-01-fresh.txt`). On a phone, no task row is in view when it opens (`m-01-fresh`). While hunting, the held task starts below her intro (`d-05`, `m-05`). Progress is a grey 8 px bar that is barely visible at 9 of 25. After a third Take the window scrolls down to her board, the held tasks go out of view, and the status box covers two rows (`d-09`, `m-09`).
2. **Every row looks the same, and the useful part is at the end of a grey sentence.** Every row has a name, then "Meadowbrook Thicket (Level 5 ground) · 480 gp + a Bronze Helm" wrapping onto 2 or 3 lines. The window has no icons at all. "Take" is bold text with no button shape, and it looks the same as "Held" and "Full". A task you already hold shows twice (in Your tasks, and again as a "Held" row). "a Bronze Helm" sits on three rows at once (`dom/d-03-took-rats.txt`). Her "she gives it once" note is in the code but never appears. **Client bug:** `sharedPieces` in `src/shell/windows/tally.js` reads `r.pick.pick`, but `tally-intents.js` parses it into `r.pick.kind`. The ground is plain text, so nothing takes you there.
3. **No character, and no answers to the obvious questions.** Tamsin says the same rules sentence in every state. Her story is not on screen: thirty years keeping the mill's rats down, then the Rat King (`src/data/tally-tasks.js`). "Paid today: 0 of 3" never says when the day resets. The armor section is 80 words of rules plus six "Move up" buttons (`d-02`). Before the first Take her board shows materials ("25 gp + 4 Slime Gel"). Right after, the same rows show armor ("a Bronze Helm") (`d-01` then `d-03`), so the reward changes under the player.

**What the mock changes (after/).**
- **Her line on top.** Her name in the display face, the day count with the reset ("Paid today 1 of 3 · new day in 6 h"), and one thing she says about where you stand. Her line sits on a gold rule and replaces the greeting, the title line and "Your gold".
- **Your tallies first.** Each held task is one ledger entry: the creature, the exact count ("112 / 200"), and her tally marks (ten strokes in two fives, each a tenth, cut ones in gold). The piece she pays shows at 32 px in the bag's pocket well. Then "You'll get 480 gp and a Bronze Platebody.", the ground as a take-me-there link (`goto.js`), and Keep and Give up. A done entry uses the Hunts list's picked row (gold bar, lifted) and says what happens next.
- **Her board below.** Each row is two lines: "200 Gray Wolves" over the ground link and "Level 5". On the right are the piece in its pocket, the gold over the piece's name, and a sunk Take button (the same button as the stations mock's Make). Held tasks leave the board. One gold line under the head says why every Take is off, either while hunting or with three held.
- **Her pick folded to one line at the foot.** "She pays you in heavy armor, head first, then body and legs. [Change]" Change opens the weights as one segmented control, a sentence for the chosen weight only, and the slot order as six chips. Tap a chip to have it first, which sends the same `hr_tally_set { order }`. Last comes the one rule about worn slots and materials.
- **Phone (the sheet).** Same order and markup. Her line drops to the small size, and the pay column narrows so a long piece name wraps. Her board's first row is in view on open (`m-01`). The held task and its marks fit with the board head below it (`m-03`, `m-04`).
- Measured on the mock: 0 text under 12 px and 0 clipped labels in all 13 shots. Desktop shows 6 board rows on a fresh open (today: 3 and a half). Phone shows 3 (today: 0).

**Not changed.** The frame, title band, width (460), place, fonts and colors. The 32 px item art. What she pays, the counts, the cap and every rule and intent (Take, Give up, Keep, weight, order, the collect on open). The HUD tally widget (separate; it is clipped at the analyzer's foot while hunting, `d-05`, and gets its own fix).

**Size for the build lane.** Client only. One lane B, about half a day. It rewrites the skeleton and patch in `src/shell/windows/tally.js` (stays near 250 lines), swaps the `.ht-*` block in `src/styles/windows.css` for mock.css, moves her lines into a new `src/data/text/tally.js`, and updates selectors in `tests/tally-window.mjs` plus one happy-path test (state picks the line, marks match the server count). The reset line is display only, worked out from the UTC day the server already uses, and nothing can spend it (§6). **Optional server field (lane C, read-only RPC body):** `hr_tally_view` would take the suggested weight, so the board shows armor before the first Take instead of materials. Without it the fresh board shows materials as it does today. **Separate lane A now:** the `pick.kind` bug in problem 2 (one line plus a regression test).

Talents: the Roads (layout sketch)

Rough layout for docs/design/redesign/17-talent-tree.md. One panel component: a window on desktop, a sheet on a landscape phone. Roads leave the Great Hearth in Havora, one country per weapon way. Square stones are milestones, diamonds are crossroads (pick one of the options beside it), red standing stones are vows, three dots are an open road. Gold is a sample Shield hero at Level 34. Shapes and positions are rough. The art lane owns the final look.

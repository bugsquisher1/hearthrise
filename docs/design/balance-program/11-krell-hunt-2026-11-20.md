**What the gaps say, plainly.**

1. **Krell's Hold pays about 12% too much Level XP, and two separate readings agree.** The real four reads 2.85 against a cap of 2.5, which is 12% over. The gate's lone hero reads 1.13 against a target of 1.0, which is 12% over. The plan found the same thing at Hask's Ford and booked it (S1, `30-plan.md` line 127). Krell needs the same line.
2. **The v0.2.142 slowdown did not reach this player.** His hour fell 4.5%. He gained three Levels and tank spells, and his four now kills 15% faster than the simulator's four at the same Level.
3. **"Struggle or pay" does not pay through the store for a four with a healer.** The plan sizes the harder tiers by a store bill 25 to 33% higher (`21-combat.md` line 108). This four heals for about 0.07 gold a health point through the healer. Store food costs 1.5. So more monster damage costs this four more drinks for the healer, about 400 gold a head an hour, and more cooking in town. It does not cost thousands.
4. **The simulator's four is not this four.** It buys Graven runes at 44 gold where he buys Carved and Etched. It puts 97% of the damage on the tank where the live game puts 28% on the healer. Its bill is 2.6 times his and it loses 7,500 gold a head an hour where he clears 9,700. Any guard that grades "Deadly never loses gold" on that four is red on the day it ships.
5. **The gold model is close on income and low on the bill.** Coin and loot are within 14%. The store bill is 67% over the model's 6,370, and that is all of the 19% miss on free gold.
6. **The journal and the watched share are both about twice the platform plan's.** The heavy player leaves the hunt on screen all day.

## 9. Repeated decisions seen (the first principle)

- **19 Hall Keys and no Hall fight.** Each key is one fight started by hand. A four that farms keys for ten hours has nineteen starts waiting for it.
- **The loot went out in one press** (Dispatch loot sold the gear and banked the materials). That is the pattern working.
- **The hunt ended by hand at 9.7 of a 12 hour limit**, and three heroes went back to their town jobs without a press. That is the pattern working too.

## 10. Limits

- One person, one day, 39 hero-hours. Every number here is a direction until a second day agrees.

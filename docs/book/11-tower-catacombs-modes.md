# The Burning Tower

## 11.2 What it is
**[Unchanged]**

Tyler (2026-11-14): "it's kind of its own challenge where the floors get incrementally harder as the player or group climbs ... dying has no penalty BUT the level xp isn't all that great".

**How it plays:**
- A climb is a run of floors. Each floor is one room of **5 monsters** at the floor's Level.
- Clear the room and the stair to the next floor opens about 4 seconds later.
- **Every 10th floor is a landing.** An old Keeper stands there with four more.
- **You have 3 minutes** to clear a floor, **10 minutes** for a landing. Run out and the climb ends.
- A death costs nothing.
- One hero or a full party of four. The rooms are sized the same for both.

## 11.3 Rules
**[Unchanged]**

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Opens at | Level 10, for every hero in the party |
| Monsters a floor | 5 |
| Landings | Every 10 floors |
| Checkpoints | Every landing. A climb starts on the floor after any landing up to your best (a party's: the highest landing every member has reached) |
| Highest floor with its own stats | 300. Floors above it play floor 300's rows |
| Party XP | One hero 100%, a four shares 130% between them. Pairs and threes sit between |
| Heat | Past floor 100, each floor's blows grow 1.2% more, compounding |

## 11.4 The Hearth Chest and its keys
**[Unchanged]**

The chest sits on the ledge of the Great Hearth. Tower landings drop keys. A key opens the chest.

| Key | From landing | Always holds | Weapon chance | A weapon gives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bronze Key** | 10 | 50 Hearth Cinders, 15 salts, 20 Cooked Shrimp | 16% | Iron-tier stats, +1 combat skill |
| **Silver Key** | 40 | 150 Cinders, 25 salts, 15 Cooked Lobster | 8% | Mithril-tier, +2 |
| **Gold Key** | 70 | 300 Cinders, 30 salts, 20 Cooked Lobster | 4.5% | Emberforged-tier, +3 |
| **Platinum Key** | 100 | 600 Cinders, 40 salts, 25 Cooked Lobster | 2% | Dawnsteel-tier, +4 |

- **6 opens a week** per hero for Tower keys. Keys keep dropping with no weekly earn cap.
- **Up to 30 held.** Past that, a new key doesn't drop.
- Keys move between your own heroes through Storage. They can't be sold or traded.
- **28 Tower weapons**, one per key per weapon type. For example the Bronze Key's are the Palisade Sword, Millwright's Mallet, Fowling Bow, Bonesetter's Staff, Lamplighter's Wand, Westwood Hatchets and Orchard Keeper's Crossbow. Chapter 15 lists them all.
- **Key Warden outfit:** a small chance in any key, higher with better keys.
- **Signatures in 0.3:** Lamplighter's Robe (Bronze lock), Passing Bell and Lychgate Leggings (Gold lock).

## 11.5 Stoking the chest
**[Unchanged]**

- **Warmth.** Every key that finds no weapon warms the hearth for that hero's next key, by 4% of the key's base chance. It tops out at three times the base after 50 dry keys. A weapon cools it back to nothing.
- **Stoking.** "Stoke the fire for good luck." Before a key you can stoke once, twice or three times, paid in Hearth Cinders worth 3, 7 or 12 keys. That multiplies the chance by 1.7, 2.5 or 3.3. No gold.
- **Your own weapon first.** Half of all weapons found are the kind your hero has equipped. The rest spread over the other kinds.
- "Use all" opens every key in one press.
- The menu shows the exact chance before you open, so nothing is hidden.

Not final: the redesign says luck never moves chest prizes. That means the Inner Flame doesn't. The chest keeps its own warmth. See the contradictions list.

## 11.6 Hearth Cinders
**[Unchanged]**

Cinders come from every chest. They pay for stokes, and they burn at the Great Hearth as fuel.

# The Unmarked Catacombs

## 11.7 What they are
**[Later patch]** (staged, opens when its art lands)

"The dead Havora couldn't afford to burn were put down here to wait. They're still waiting."

The Catacombs sit under Havora, for Levels 165 to 185. They come two ways.

**As two Danger-scale grounds:**

| Wing | Monsters | Traits it rolls (up to rank III) |
|---|---|---|
| **Bone Wing** | Shrouded Pauper, Bone Wall, Night Carter | Snuffed, Keeper's Carry, Full Wick, Pauper's Share, Dunn's Warning, Stair Bearer |
| **Flooded Wing** | Shrouded Pauper, Night Carter, Vault Leech, Bone Wall, Keeper Dunn | Slack Water, Floodmark, Numbed, Lime Pit, Sour Vault |

**As two climbs,** like the Tower, with Keeper Dunn on the landings and the same rules: rooms of 5, landings every 10, checkpoints, no death cost.

## 11.8 Catacomb keys and armor
**[Later patch]**

- Two key families: **Waterlogged Keys** (Flooded Wing) and **Bone Keys** (Bone Wing), each in Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum, from landings 10, 40, 70 and 100.
- They open the same Hearth Chest, with their own 6 opens a week and 30 held.
- Instead of weapons, they find **Catacomb armor**: helmets and boots in all three weights. 24 pieces, from the Tinker's Kettle and the Midwife's Hat up to the Vault Cutter's Helm and Dunn's Slippers.
- Catacomb helmets add combat skill levels while worn (+2 to +3 Melee on a plate helmet, Ranged on leather, Magic on cloth).
- The Bone Wing leans to helmets, the Flooded Wing to boots.

## 11.9 Catacomb Signatures
**[Later patch]**

Ossuary Helm (Bone Wall), Floodwater Hatchets (Flooded Wing), The Wood Book and The Cold Candle (Keeper Dunn), Catacomb Shroud (Shrouded Pauper), Vault Leech Ring (Vault Leech), Night Carter's Boots (Night Carter).

## 11.13 The world boss
**[Later patch]**

- One shard-wide fight a week. Every company adds to it and is paid by what it added, against what a company of its band is expected to add. So one company's edge never shrinks another's pay.
- Attempts are counted a week, never by hunting hours.
- The first is **Mossback**, who comes to the Mill Pond every 28 days for 72 hours. His event row exists. His monster and art don't yet (Tyler: wait on art).

## 11.14 Arenas
**[Later patch]**

- Your opponent is another company's saved plan, so nobody needs to be online.
- The engine work for heroes fighting heroes is built now ("the side seam"), so the arenas can arrive later.

### Core game modules (server-authoritative, unit-tested; start as soon as the spec's architecture section is approved)
4. **Movement:**
   - heroes pass through each other; monsters collide;
   - a leash that is a per-hunt value with a small margin, and resets on exit;
   - follow-the-leader until a new target.
5. **Targeting + role AI:**
   - tank holds; ranged staggered by their distance setting;
   - distance measured from the hero's own target;
   - kiting = chase off and distance > 1; kiters pull from range and close in when leashed monsters can't reach;
   - monster target switching + the tank's auto-Challenge.
6. **Status effects:** bleed is generic; stacks max 5 for 6 s; scales slowly with Melee skill × Life-rune tier; physical damage.
7. **Consumables:** food = HP, drink = mana, no potions; 50% instant + 50% over 6 s; own stock free, store-bought when out; never stop for lack of stock.
8. **XP:** per hero = base share × that hero's multipliers (bestiary-ready); grouped "+XP" events (1 s).
9. **Server work (lane C; applies on a passing §4 self-check; Security review at the reopen gate):**
   - per-hero hunt settings (action set, target, stance, distance, chase);
   - the own/store supply ledger;
   - server-side analyzer session stats;
   - party (up to 4, limit is a config value, any role mix, even loot split by value, party-finder filter by weapon type).

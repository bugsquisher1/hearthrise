BRIEF 03: THE GROUNDED BARGE, the Slake's first signature piece (plan line A4b, about 40 generations)
Art director, 2026-11-07. Three candidates first (about 30 gens), stop, hand me all three; fixes after my pick.

WHAT IT IS: the Banded Company's supply barge. Hask sold the crossing, so it never got back upstream; it ran aground on
the Slake's middle bar and broke its back there (the board's lore line). It is the ground's centrepiece and its
landmark: the pocket "the Grounded Barge" (P5) is fought around it, and the Color Sergeant holds it. On the map it
blocks a step and a shot: the hull fills tiles x27-36, y18-22 of the Slake's rows (10 wide, 5 deep, narrower at the
bow and stern rows). Sprite id `sl_grounded_barge`, set `slake`.

FORM: a long, flat-bottomed river barge lying east to west, seen from high above (the one world angle): the deck is
what we see most, with the near (south) side of the hull showing a little. Broken-backed: the deck planks split and
sagging in the middle, one end lower than the other, sunk to the waterline in grey-brown silt. A stub of a broken mast
near the middle. An open cargo hatch with coal heaped inside. Rotted green weed along the hull where the water stood.
Three or four of the company's round banded shields still hung along the gunwale, greened by copper. No people, no
bodies, no lights.

STYLE (never mix styles; docs/design/PIXELLAB_STYLE1_KIT.md §3):
  view high top-down; single black outline; basic shading lit from the upper left; no baked shadow, no glow, no water
  or ground around it (transparent background: the floor is the painted ground under it)
  palette natural and muted: dark wet wood browns, iron grey fittings, coal black, the one accent verdigris
  (copper-green) on the shields and the weed only, under 15% of its pixels
  plank scale: planks 4 to 6 px wide, the same scale as the March's sunk rowboat (refs/style-hf_rowboat_sunk.png):
  this is that boat's big sister, made of the same wood
  style images (upload each, get_upload_url): refs/style-hf_rowboat_sunk.png and refs/style-hf_wagon_tipped.png

THE CALL: create_image_pro (Pro Flash caps at 256 px; the Great Hearth was drawn this way at 352, lane/b-hearth-art)
  size 320x176, transparent background, the two style images, and the description above as the prompt, starting
  "a pixel art game prop, high top-down view, a long wrecked river barge run aground, broken in the middle ..."
  Three calls, three candidates.
  Fixes after my pick: inpaint_image on the region named (a seam, a stray light, a wrong-angle end), 32 to 64 px windows.
  Reject when: the angle is side-on or isometric (the deck must be seen from above, no corner pointing at us), the hull
  is shorter than 280 px or taller than 170 px of art, water or ground is painted around it, or the planks are
  smaller than 3 px (it will read as a model, not a wreck in this world).

WHERE IT GOES: art/world-src/gen-2026-11-07/grounded-barge/candidate-<n>.png + note.json (image ids). After my pick,
the import lane (class `building`: art-budget.js body 80-160 px tall; a 160 px tall hull fits):
  node tools/world-art/port-pixellab.mjs object --set slake --id sl_grounded_barge --class building --material wood
       --footprint 10,5 --file <picked png> --object <IMAGE_ID> --name "S1 Grounded Barge"
  node tools/world-art/build.mjs --set slake;  node tests/world-assets.mjs

WHAT I CHECK: locked (the wood grade dulls warm browns: judge it after build.mjs, never raw), on the Slake's silt
beside the hero and a Bargeman at x2 in tools/qa/region-kit.html; its edges where it meets the silt (no hard square
cut); it reads from across the ground as the place to fight round; the hero is about a fifth of its length.

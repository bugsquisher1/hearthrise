Mirror of `all.json`. 155 calls, one row each, sorted by id. Written 2026-11-21 on `lane/d-book-integrate`. No game code, data or migration was changed.

The three decider files held 157 rows. MM-7 and GR-24 were each decided twice and are one row here. The review's 31 fixes are applied. A fixed row shows the fix numbers, and its first wording is kept in `was` in the JSON.

**Status words.** DECIDED is Fable's decision of 2026-11-21. DECIDED (reversal, Tyler may veto) changes one of Tyler's rulings and stands unless he vetoes it. TYLER CONFIRMS is a money call or a grant with one recommendation. WITHDRAWN was refused by the review.

**Counts.** 114 decided, 24 decided as reversals (23 lines on Tyler's list, because CE-8 and GR-82 are one line), 16 for Tyler to confirm, 1 withdrawn. 40 rows were changed by a fix.

In a reason, "I" is the decider. Only a ruling id or date cites Tyler.

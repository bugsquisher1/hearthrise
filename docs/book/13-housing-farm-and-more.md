# 13. Housing, the farm and everything else

Your house, its rooms and storage, the farm, outfits, mounts, and the small systems that didn't fit anywhere else.

---

## 13.1 Your house
**[Unchanged]**, with the redesign's "hall" direction noted

Your company has one home. It belongs to the account. Each property has its own map, and you can buy the next one up.

| Property | Price | Farm plots | Rooms it opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wanderer's Camp | Where you start | 2 | None |
| Hearthside Homestead | 400 gold, 20 Copper Ore, 30 Normal Logs | 4 | Kitchen, Garden |
| Fieldworth Farmstead | 25,000 gold, 10 Cooked Shrimp, 25 Copper Ore, 40 Oak Logs, 4 Wolf Pelts | 6 | Workshop, Cellar, Forge |
| Stonecross Manor | 150,000 gold, Iron Fittings, Iron Ore, Silk Thread, Timber Beams, Willow Planks | 8 | Library |
| Ironvale Keep | 600,000 gold, Bear Pelts, Big Bones, Iron Fittings, Maple Planks, Steel Bars, Timber Beams | 10 | Shrine, Trophy |
| Hearthrise Castle | 2,500,000 gold, Emberforged Bars, Gatekeeper's Chains, Iron Fittings, Runewood Planks, Timber Beams | 12 | None more |

- A property bought stays bought.
- The Hearthrise Castle opens your home to visitors.

**[Changed] direction:** the redesign calls the home the company's **hall**: storage, the trade benches, farm plots, a trophy wall and a door other players can walk through. It gives no combat power. Your flame's fire, your records, your Hearthfinds and your faction banner show here.

## 13.2 Rooms
**[Unchanged]** today, **[Changed]** by the redesign

Each room has five rungs, bought with gold and materials. Rungs 1 to 3 run from 600 gold (the Garden) up to 160,000. Rung 4 up to 240,000. Rung 5 from 600,000 to 900,000.

| Room | Its bonus, rung 1 to rung 5 |
|---|---|
| Kitchen | Cooking 2% to 10% faster. Less burnt food |
| Forge | Smithing 2% to 10% faster |
| Workshop | Crafting 2% to 10% faster, saves 1% to 8% of materials |
| Library | Runecrafting 2% to 10% faster |
| Garden | 1 to 5 more farm plots |
| Cellar | Food buffs last 20% to 100% longer |
| Shrine | Inner Flame training 2% to 10% faster |
| Trophy | 2 to 6 trophy mounts |

- Owning a room at rung 1 or more removes the bench tax for its trade (chapter 9).
- **The Shrine and the Trophy room are off sale today.** The Trophy room comes back later as a place for mounts from real trophy drops and firsts.

Not final: Tyler approved cutting the house speed rungs and the Cellar's food rungs (2026-11-21), and the redesign drops small percent bonuses and the Shrine's flame speed (the flame isn't a skill anymore). The data still has them. See the contradictions list.

## 13.4 The training dummy
**[Unchanged]**

From the Homestead up, buy a dummy per combat skill and train with no hourly fee: 150,000 / 250,000 / 400,000 / 600,000 gold (chapter 3).

## 13.5 The farm
**[Unchanged]**

- Plots: 2 at the start, more with each property and the Garden.
- Nine crops from Turnip (Level 1, 4 hours) to Moonbloom (Level 88, 22 hours). Tomato and Emberfruit regrow. Chapter 9 has the table.
- Farming is a company trade. It feeds Cooking today and Alchemy later.

## 13.7 Mounts
**[Later patch]**

- Mounts are looks. Nothing to time. A hunt starts on its ground and ends in town with no travel, so a mount never touches the hunting day.
- Your company earns its **first mount with its first depth record**.
- More are **tamed from rare drops**, with a bestiary line, and each takes a crafted saddle.
- Crown mounts are looks only and bound.
- A mount is seen riding through town, out at the Start, home at the return and on the return card.
- A faction side cloth for your mount comes with faction standing.

## 13.11 Where you play
**[Unchanged]**

## 13.12 Accounts
**[Unchanged]**

- Passwords need at least 10 characters.
- A returning player in 0.3 lands on hero creation. Nothing carries over (chapter 1).

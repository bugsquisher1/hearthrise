# 2. Your company, heroes and recruits

Everything about who is in your company and what belongs to whom.

---

## 2.1 One account, one company
**[Changed]**

- One account per player. Your account is your **company**.
- The company holds up to **four heroes**. All four slots are free. Nobody buys hero slots anymore.
- You start with one hero. Three recruits join in one press at Level 5 (chapter 1).
- Every hero you own can be playing at the same time. Only the hero you're looking at streams live to your screen. The others keep playing on the server.

**What belongs to the company, shared by every hero:**

| Thing | Chapter |
|---|---|
| Trade Levels (Mining, Cooking, Smithing and the rest) | 9 |

| Ladders and records | 12 |

**What belongs to each hero:**

## 2.2 Heroes
**[Unchanged]**

- **No classes.** A hero is what they fight with. Pick up a bow and you train Ranged.
- Each hero has a **Level**, which never caps, and four combat skills: **Melee, Ranged, Magic and Defense**. Chapter 3 covers both.
- **Health:** 150 at Level 1, plus 10 a Level. Heavy armor adds health.
- **Mana:** 100 at Level 1, plus 10 a Level. Light armor adds mana.
- A hero can wear any gear their Level allows (chapter 6).

## 2.3 Recruits
**[New in 0.3]**

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| When | Your first hero reaches Level 5 |
| How many | 3, in one press |
| Their Level | The same as your first hero (no gap) |
| Highest Level they arrive at | 10, however late you press |
| Their jobs | Picked from Tank, Healer, Ranger and Club, skipping the job your first hero plays |
| Their gear | The full practice set for their job |
| Their Roads points | Spent as suggested for their job |

A recruit who arrives with banked points has the suggested path one press away.

## 2.4 The company plan
**[New in 0.3]**

The plan is one saved object on the server. It is the heart of the "decide once" rule.

**What the plan holds:**
- Who hunts, and in which row (front or back).
- The ground and the Risk setting.
- Each hero's action bar.
- A saved Roads build per ground, if you want one.
- Supply rules (what to restock, from Storage first, then the store).
- Loot rules (what to keep, sell or burn).
- The stoke rule for your Inner Flame.
- Temper and imbuement rules ("Re-seat Temper", "Keep imbued").
- What each hero works on in town after the hunting day.
- How many Hunting blocks to buy without asking.

**How it plays:**
- **One Start** runs the plan for the whole company.
- Plans are **free** to save and name. The old paid setup slots (15,000 and 40,000 gold) are gone.
- A plan can be copied from another of your heroes. Copying between players by share code comes in 0.3 too.
- No hunt ever starts by itself. You press Start, or Carry on.

## 2.5 Partying your own heroes
**[Changed]**

- Up to **four heroes** in a hunt.
- Your own heroes join with no invite.
- **Two rows.** Front and back, set in the plan. Monsters pick which row to hit by their role (chapter 5).
- Any mix of styles is allowed. Four of one style is allowed too, just weaker.
- Party-wide effects from the same source never stack. Four different heroes beat four of the same.

## 2.6 Parties with other players
**[Unchanged]**

- Heroes from different accounts can fill one party of four on the same rules.
- A shared supply bill is agreed once and remembered.
- Heroes of either faction side can hunt together.

## 2.7 How a party shares
**[Changed]**

| What | How it's shared |
|---|---|
| Level XP | Split by the heroes alive at each kill. A lower hero earns faster until close (catch-up) |
| Coin | Split evenly, any leftover coin rotates |
| Loot | **Personal.** Each hero rolls its own drops (chapter 7) |

**Catch-up.** A hero below the party's top Level earns faster until it is close. A recruit or a retrained hero is never a second grind. A carried hero is paid its own Level's rate, never more.

**Why a four earns more.** A good four holds a much deeper depth than its heroes apart, and depth pays (chapter 5).

Not final: how party XP is shared is still in two minds. Today's data has a table by party make-up and a +15% for a lone hero. Tyler ruled "by party size alone" for the first build. The redesign says "no party table at all, one pay rule". See the contradictions list.

## 2.8 Hunting alone
**[Unchanged]**

- A lone hero is allowed and fair. It is slower than a party. No number is set for how much slower (Tyler, 2026-11-22).
- Some things suit a lone hero: a target drop, a hero at a different Level, a cheap gold run.

## 2.9 Hired heroes and going on call
**[Later patch]**

Hiring a hero from another player is the party finder of the future. It ships after 0.3.

- A resting hero can go **on call** once every 24 hours, for 12 hours, then goes back to its chosen town work.
- An on-call hero has its own **hire stamina**, separate from its own hunting day. The hirer sees it and can spend all of it.
- The owner trades the hero's town time for a small cut of the hunt's pay. The cut comes out of the hunt's own pay, never new gold.
- The owner risks nothing. A hired hero takes no loss on death.
- A hired hero's personal loot goes to its owner and rolls on its owner's flame.

Not final: the cut, the size of the hire stamina pool and the hire board screen.

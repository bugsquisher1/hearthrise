0. HARNESS NOTES (read these before trusting any number)
======================================================================
- Local world via tools/qa/local-world.mjs openWorld(); accounts through world.play({account}), which signed each account up and in. I did not drive the sign-up screen myself.
- hr_market_config.enabled was false in the local DB; I set it true with one UPDATE (as instructed). Later I also set min_level from 10 to 1 so level-5 heroes could use the Weighhouse (harness change, see section 6).
- I used six accounts: the cast (bram, wren, sol) and three "twin" accounts (Brin sword, Wynn bow, Sola staff) that hunted SOLO in the same ground, started minutes before the party, as the "playing alone" baseline. Twins were fresh Level 1 heroes like the cast.
- The PC was shared with other lanes and the local world is one Node process with an in-memory database. As hunts piled up (6 heroes in real time + two time skips) the world slowed: screenshots of hunting pages timed out after 8-25 s, a 14-minute skip took 115 s, and new browser pages could not load play.html at all (page.goto timed out at 180 s, twice) after about 01:00 UTC. The game itself showed a "Slow connection. Still trying..." line on Sol's page at that point. I treat all of that as harness load, not as a game bug, but I did not isolate it.
- First "Enter town" glitch on the create-hero screen: see bug P1-1, which could be load-related.
- Two other agents were writing to the same scratchpad directory; I had to rename my files. No effect on the game.
- Nothing was written to production; the local world refuses it by construction.

Derived (my arithmetic):
- Party Level XP/h per hero vs solo: sword +14% (8,281 / 7,277), bow +11%, staff +37%.
- Level XP per kill per hero: party about 8.9, solo sword 15.4. A party kills about 15.6 per minute (5.2 per hero) vs solo 7.9 per minute. So the hero gets about 57% of the XP per kill but the pack dies about twice as fast.
- Gold + loot per hour per hero: party about 1,880 vs solo sword 1,776 (+6%), vs solo bow 2,557 (-26%), vs solo staff 1,632 (+15%). Per kill per hero: party about 2.0 gp, solo sword 3.8 gp.
- Supplies: "Costs are personal." Each hero pays their own. Over 43 minutes Bram 0 gp, Wren 80 gp (Thin Turnip Juice x8, a mana drink for the bow), Sol not read. Solo bow 87 gp/h, party bow 110-125 gp/h.
- The party's per-hour rate stayed flat across the session (8,164 / 8,135 / 8,281), so leveling from 1 to 5 did not distort it here.

What the screen promised vs what I measured:
- Promised: "3 of 4 classes: 18% more Level XP in Mill Cellar."  Measured per hero: +11% to +14% against the sword and bow solos. No panel line separates the class bonus: "Level XP per hour" and "Level XP per hour before bonuses" are the SAME number (8,281 and 8,281). So the screen gives no way for a player to see the 18%. I cannot tell from the screen whether the bonus was applied; the arithmetic above is also mixed with the pull difference (below).
- Promised: bigger packs in a party ("2 packs of 5" vs "2 packs of 2 to 3") and better drop chances ("in a party of 3": e.g. Bones 18% vs 12% alone, Wheat 15% vs 9.9%, Slime Gel 14% vs 8.8%). Measured: gold + loot per hour per hero is about the same as a solo sword hero, not higher.
- Confound I could not remove: the party fought packs of 5; the solos fought packs of 2-3 (both are the default). So "grouping" and "bigger default pull" are not separated. I did not get to the pressed-up pull test.

7. OPINION (marked)
======================================================================
OPINION: Party UI is the best part of this build for friends. The invite flow, refusal messages and crossing invites are all clear and truthful; the class labels (Melee / Ranger / Healer) appear as soon as a weapon is equipped, and the bonus line tells you immediately why a mixed party is wanted.
OPINION: The promise and the payoff are too quiet. +18% Level XP is a number a friend group will quote in the call; if the hunt panel then shows no "party bonus" line, it will feel invented even if it is real.
OPINION: In party the single hero's numbers hardly move (+11 to +14% Level XP, same gold), but the pack dies twice as fast and the healer's solo hunt is weak (6,045 vs 7,277), so the real appeal for the healer is large. A friend who plays staff will always want to join the sword and the bow.
OPINION: The "Remove" button and the "Leave if anyone leaves" checkbox only exist once the hunt has started, which makes sense but is hard to discover from town.

11. VERDICT
======================================================================
Would these friends still be playing together next week? PROBABLY YES for the first week, with a wobble.
Why yes: the party flow is easy and honest (invite by name, plain refusals, the party follows the leader into the hunt, Leave/closing the browser behave exactly as the screen says, and nobody lost anything). It costs nothing to group: per hero Level XP/h was 11-14% above a solo sword/bow and the gold was the same, and the healer gains the most (+37% over a solo staff, who also hunts slowly). The Weighhouse worked first time and told the truth about fees.
Why the wobble: (1) the promise is "18% more Level XP" in a number they will quote on the call, but the panel never shows a party bonus and its two Level XP figures are identical, so the first friend who checks will say it is fake; (2) nothing in a party hunt asks the three of them to do anything together beyond pressing Start with party, and the hunt panel for the leader says "Your hero fights alone"; (3) I could not test harder grounds or a death, which is where parties either bond or fall apart. If the bonus is real and shown, and if the next ground makes the healer feel needed, they stay. If not, they drift back to separate characters.
What the game told truthfully: invite results, the "already in a party" refusal, the leave-hunt countdown and "Sol left the hunt, so Bram came home too", "Hunt again is off. Your party leader picks...", Weighhouse fee/tax/total, supplies "Costs are personal." What it did not tell: the size of the party bonus, and that a party of 3 fights packs of 5 by default while a solo fights 2 to 3.

4. BUGS (facts unless marked; each with steps)
----------------------------------------------
Bug 1. The return screen doesn't report skilling, and reports an old hunt instead.
  Steps: Hunt the Thicket, press Leave hunt. Start Mining, Iron Rock. Close the page for 8 hours. Sign in, press Play.
  Saw: "While you were away (8 h 3 min), Tess hunted Meadowbrook Thicket. The hunt has ended 8 h 13 min ago. You left the hunt and walked home."
  Nothing about 4,303 Iron Ore or Mining 26 to 47. That hunt ended before I left.
  Same after a 50 min bench job: "While you were away (51 min), Tess hunted The Sewer..." with no word on 564 Oak Planks.
  With a hero who has never hunted (T+1:30, 392 planks made): no recap at all.
  Only the skill window later says "Mining the Iron Rock: 4,303 in your bag."

Bug 2. Tools tell you to sell them, and quietly stop working if you put them away.
  Steps: Forge a Bronze Axe. Hover it in the bag.
  Saw: "Used for: Sell it at the shop." Pressing it offers only Sell. No Equip. Nothing says it speeds chopping. The row time just changes (4.8 s to 4.6 s).
  Steps: With an Iron Axe and a Bronze Axe in the bag, read Woodcutting: Normal Log 4.3 s. Move both to Storage. Read it again: 4.8 s. No message.
  (Opinion: a tidy player who stores or sells these loses speed and never learns why. The Shop lists them under Tools with no benefit either.)

Bug 3. Where a monster material comes from is hidden when you need it.
  Steps: Open House, read "Wolf Pelt 4 (none in your bag or Storage)". Hover it, press it: nothing. Open Crafting, read "No Silk Thread in your bag or Storage": hover, press: nothing.
  Open Hunts, read each ground's Drops and Rare drops: they list gear only. Wolf Pelt, Silk Thread, Bone Chips, Bat Wing, Wheat, Slime Gel never appear.
  The bag tooltip does say "Comes from" and "Used for", and it's very good, but only for a stack you already hold.

Bug 4. Sell all loot sold a material I needed.
  Steps: Crafting 21, Slime Gel ×32 in the bag. Shop, Sell. The row has no "Sell all loot keeps it" tag. Press Sell all loot.
  Saw: gel gone. Timber Beam needs 2 Slime Gel and House deed III needs 6 Timber Beams, both visible in game at that moment.
  At Crafting 31 the same row reads "Sell all loot keeps it", so the keep list seems to follow recipes I've unlocked, not recipes that exist.

Bug 5. I can spend hours making things that can't be sold anywhere, and nothing warns me.
  Steps: Bind Chalked Earth Runes (9,180 made), Bind Chalked Fire Runes (3,195), Fletch Bronze Arrows (2,350 made).
  Bag tooltip: "The shop has not priced this yet." They aren't on the Shop's Sell tab.
  Weighhouse, Sell: "11 stacks here can't be sold to other players: starter kit, gifts and trophies." None of these rows appear. Neither do the 330 Cooked Shrimp I cooked myself.
  Chalked Runes aren't in any starter kit I saw. Wolf Meat, Iron Arrows, Blank Runes and Iron Whetstones do list.
  (Opinion: "has not priced this yet" reads as an unfinished game.)

Bug 6. Whetstone tooltip is wrong.
  Steps: Hover Coarse Whetstone or Iron Whetstone in the bag.
  Saw: "Melee damage +2" then "Used for: Fired from a bow, one per shot." Bronze Arrows carry the same line, correctly.

Bug 7. Items labeled "Monster loot" that I made or gathered.
  Hover Copper Ore, Oak Log, Iron Bar, Steel Bar, Timber Beam: header says "Monster loot". Bronze Bar, Copper Bar say "Material".

Bug 8. Character list shows a job that's already over.
  Steps: Start a job that will finish while you're away, close the page, return.
  Saw: "Level 8 · In town, working on Oak Plank" with all logs already planked. Press Play and the tab says "In town, idle". Twice.

Bug 9. Ranger prep screen looks wrong.
  Steps: Equip Practice Crossbow, open Hunts, a ground. Saw "Ammo: 249 Iron Arrows of yours, loaded. Runes: 3,195 Fire runes of yours".
  A crossbow loading arrows, and a ranger with a rune line. Bow reads the same. I didn't fight with either, so this may be wording only.

Bug 10. My own drink and spare food aren't used by the hunt.
  Coach tip during a hunt: "Your own turnips cook into juice that costs nothing."
  Steps: Cook Turnip Juice (78) and Cooked Wolf Meat (112). Open Hunts, a ground, read Before you go.
  Saw: "Food: 34 Cooked Shrimp of yours, then Salted Shrimp from the store, 14 gp each" and "Drink: Thin Turnip Juice from the store, 10 gp each". Neither my juice nor my wolf meat is named.
  My Turnip Juice stack was still exactly 78 after a 160 min Sewer hunt. I didn't try assigning them to a bar slot by hand, so that may be the way, but nothing told me to.

Not sure these are bugs, but they look it:
- Food order: Cooked Shrimp (Cooking 1) heals 96. Cooked Wolf Meat (Cooking 5) heals 72. Roasted Carrot (Cooking 5) heals 60. Then Wheat Bread (Cooking 20) heals 216. The level 5 recipes are worse than the level 1.
- Skill card says "Next at level 12: Blank Rune (material)" while I've been making Blank Runes since level 1. Different recipe, same name.
- Flavor text: Copper Ore "smelted with tin", Bronze Bar "Copper and tin". There's no tin; the recipe is 2 Copper Ore.

Opinions (design, not bugs):
- Combat Level 10 gate on the Weighhouse. At Mining 47, Smithing 27, Crafting 31 and owning a Manor, I was told I wasn't qualified. Getting there took 5 h 45 of hunting in total.
- Gear I can make, I can't wear: Iron Hatchets, Warhammer, Shield need Level 15, and I was 7-10. Iron Whetstone needs Melee 30 (I have 11). A skiller never tests his own goods.
- No bench queue. A job ends and the hero stands idle for the rest of the absence (35 of my 60 minutes at T+0:30). Gathering never stops. So at bedtime I always pick gathering.
- Sell… on the bag sells the whole stack with no amount. The workaround: right-click into Storage, sell the rest. The Shop's big-stack button is capped at 1,000 a press.
- No coach step I saw mentions gathering or crafting. "Rats in the mill" sat pinned at the top for my first 6 hours of skilling.
- A second hero is simply +100% resources for 0 gp, and the third slot at 15,000 gp is about 15 minutes of bar-making. 500 starting gp arrives with each new hero too.
- Bronze chain is dominated: Bronze Bar costs more ore and pays less than Copper Bar.
- Room upgrades look weak for the price: Forge 2 is "smithing 4% faster" for 3,000 gp. Soil and Garden are the ones that felt good.

Things I liked, so you keep them:
- Bag tooltips ("Comes from", "Used for", value). Hunts prep ("Before you go") naming my own food and runes first. Market Sell warning: "the shop pays you more than a sale would. Ask 125 or more to match it."
- Food link: the Sewer ate about 290 of my shrimp, and it felt like my cooking mattered.
- Farm: harvest during a hunt works, Harvest and replant is one press, watering is a small nice touch.
- Weighhouse loop end to end: fee and 4% cut shown up front, History clear, pick where the goods land. Clean.


5. CHAINS: WHO WANTS THE RESULT, AND WAS IT WORTH IT
----------------------------------------------------
Woodcutting: found in G, one press. Logs → planks → House, arrows, tools. Worth it; planks are top money. Tools help.
Mining: same. Ore → bars → House, tools, fittings. Essence → runes. The 8-hour bedtime skill. Worth it.
Fishing: Raw fish → Cooking → eaten on hunts, sold cheap (Cooked Shrimp 9, Raw Herring 2). Fine, low value. No tool that I saw.
Cooking: 41 min to 22. Food really gets eaten. Bread at 120 gp is great money for rat-drop wheat. Worth it.
Crafting: planks yes, Timber Beam yes (300 gp plus House). Clothing and weapons: Oak Wand 57, Oak Crossbow 65, sold below materials and also drops free. Arrows sell for nothing.
Smithing: bars and whetstones yes. Weapons and armor lose money. Drops gave me 7 Iron Swords, 9 Bronze Platebodies, 4 Iron Helms for free meanwhile. Forged gear is a dead end for anything except XP.
Runecrafting: slowest XP, runes only for your own mage. Earth and Fire go to a wand or spellbook, Water and Poison to a staff (prep screen). Store sells Plain at 1 gp a cast. I made 12,375 for no gold.
Farming: seeds from Shop, crops need Cooking. Best XP per press. Worth it. Seeds don't return on harvest, so Harvest and replant needs stock.
Dead ends I can name: Bronze Arrows and Chalked Runes (for gold), forged and sewn gear (for gold), Burnt Food (1 gp, says so), Coarse Whetstone (no price; I didn't test it in a fight).
Junk loot that says "Sell it at the shop": Rat Tail, Small Fang, Venom Sac, Gray Dewclaw and so on. Fine, it's honest about it.


6. HARNESS ODDITIES (not game bugs)
-----------------------------------
- Weighhouse reads "isn't trading right now" in this world because hr_market_config.enabled is false in the replayed chain. I set it true in my local database only, to test the market. Everything after that is real presses.
- After a time skip the open page takes up to about 15 s to show the new numbers. That's the poll catching up with a jump no player makes.
- "While you were away (10 min) ... +70,920 Level XP" mismatched its own clock: I skipped time with the tab open, and the tool's header warns about this.
- Slow presses with two accounts hunting are the single-backend database.
- Screenshot numbers restart after each page close, so they aren't in one sequence.
- Skips can't cross a UTC day, so per-day counts didn't reset.


7. TOP 5 TO FIX (ranked, by what a skiller feels)
-------------------------------------------------
1. The return moment ignores skilling. Coming back after 8 h to a message about an old hunt, with no word on 4,303 ore and 21 levels, wastes the game's best moment. Say what the gathering or bench did, and don't name a hunt that ended before I left. (Bug 1)
2. Crafting pays 15 times hunting, and refined bars beat finished goods. 70,900 gp/h from Iron Bars against 4,800 gp/h from the Sewer, while a forged weapon sells for a third of its metal. So nobody forges (opinion: and nobody hunts for money). Rebalance bar prices down, and give forged gear a reason to exist. (Section 3)
3. Say where monster materials come from: in the House and recipe rows, and in Hunts' Drops. My House II and III both stalled on things nothing on screen located. (Bug 3, and Bug 4, which sold one of them)
4. Explain tools. Tooltip should say what it does and that it must stay in the bag, not "Sell it at the shop". Moving it to Storage should warn. (Bug 2)
5. Don't let me craft unsellable stock in silence. Give Chalked Runes, Bronze Arrows and player-cooked shrimp a price or a market row, or say so on the recipe, and replace "The shop has not priced this yet". Fix the whetstone text with it. (Bugs 5, 6)


8. VERDICT
----------
I would keep playing for the skills, at least for a good while, and that surprised me. Pressing a skill and getting a real, clear number (seconds, XP, level) is the Runescape feeling I came for, and away-time gathering makes every bedtime feel earned; the House gave me a true goal, deed by deed, and the moment the Sewer's Silk Thread let me finish the Manor was the best in the session. What would make me stop is that it's too generous and too flat at the bottom: Level 47 Mining happened while I slept, money stopped mattering by hour 17, and no skill ever asked me to choose anything harder than "the next tree". Nothing I forged was worth forging, and the game never once told me what my hero did while I was gone. Fix the return screen and give crafted gear a reason, and I'd play this for months.

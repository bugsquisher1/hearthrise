HEARTHRISE — a phone player's review
Who I am: I play idle games on an Android phone, landscape, thumbs only. Five to fifteen minutes
at a time: on the bus, on the sofa. My signal drops in the tunnel every morning.
How I played: touch phone at 922x423 (and 667x375 for a stretch), over about three in-game days of
short sessions, with "away" time skipped between them. Screenshots are in phone/ (names below).
I took ~140; only these 15 are included (downscaled to phone size) to keep it light:
  07-create-hero, 10-town-first, 13-mill-cellar-picked, 18-hunt-5s, 35-helm-expand,
  47-hearth-30s, 54-offline-25s, 57-evening-hunt-667, 88-day2-summary, 96-all-characters,
  100-offline-reload, 103-lunch2-in-667, 120-wren-left, 129-day3-wren, 137-day3-commute-kestrel.
Other [names] below were taken but left out; the text says what they showed.
(The fonts in my screenshots are the sandbox's fallbacks; I'm not counting that.)

TOP FIVE, in priority order (details in the diary below):
 1. A new archer can't attack out of the box: no ammo slot on her bar, arrows sit in the bag. She
    "hunted" 7.5 h for 0 kills while the store bought 8,746 gp of food. Nothing warned or stopped it.
 2. "Hunt summary", "Last hunt: ..." and "hunt ended" alerts are stuck on each hero's FIRST hunt;
    later hunts never get a summary, and opening the game mid-hunt often shows that old hunt frozen.
 3. Phone controls you can't reach: hero tabs squashed to 12 px; at 667 px the icon bar covers
    All characters / Characters / Sign out, hotbar slots 5-20 hide under the stance panel, and the
    chat box starts below the screen. Party invites need a right-click/long-press on that hidden tab.
 4. Hover-only meaning on a touch screen: 15 unlabeled top icons, hotbar "why it won't fire"
    reasons, keyboard-shortcut chips (H, B, Escape, Enter) on every sheet; grey buttons with no reason.
 5. Waiting with no word: 46 s blank cold start on slow signal, 20-33 s Leave, "Loading the world..."
    forever offline, and stacked see-through tip/alert cards covering the hero and each other.

[01-signin] Opened the game.
  Expected: a login I can get through one-handed.
  Got: a calm dark-and-gold sign-in, crest on the left, form on the right. Loaded in about a
  second. Fits a landscape phone with no scrolling.
  Felt: nice. Looks like a real game, not a web form.
  Small: "Create an account" and "Forgot your password?" are text links only 24 px tall. I hit
  them, but it's a thumb-stretch target, not a button.
  Small: the email box grabs focus on open. On a real phone that throws the keyboard up straight
  away and hides half the screen before I've even looked at it.

[02-create-account, 03/04-keyboard-*] Made an account with the keyboard up.
  Expected: the keyboard to cover everything (it usually does in landscape).
  Got: the page scrolls so the box I'm typing in and the "Create account" button stay visible
  above the keyboard. Good.
  Small: on the create screen the panel's bottom border runs off the screen at 423 px tall.

[05-account-made] "Check your email for a link." Then "Back to sign in"...
  ...and the sign-in form was EMPTY. I had to type my whole email address again on a phone
  keyboard, seconds after typing it. Carry the email across. (Password managers help some people,
  but this is the first minute of the game.)
  After the keyboard closed the page stayed scrolled down, so the panel's title was cut off.

[06-characters] Signed in, quick (a quarter of a second here).
  "Your characters": two empty slots, "Create hero". Big rows, easy to hit. "Sign out" is another
  24 px link but I don't need it often, so fine.

[07-create-hero] Create your hero.

  The name tag "Kestrel" was green in town, which made me smile: my shirt colour shows nowhere
  because I'm in armour, but fine.

SUMMARY
======================================================================

WHAT I LOVED
- My hero keeps hunting with the phone in my pocket, and the server is honest about it: kills,
  XP, loot and gold really were there when I came back, every time, even after tunnels.
- The look. Chunky, readable pixel art: rats, wolves, crows, poachers, a knight with a red plume,
  a blonde archer. 60 fps even on an emulated mid-range phone. Not one hitch I could feel.
- The first five minutes: create, tap Hunts, tap Enter, and a knight is fighting rats on his own.
  Level 2 in under two minutes. The ring filling up is a great hook.
- Sheets that slide in from the right with the world still visible on the left. The pinned gold
  Enter button. The "All characters" sheet. The full-screen hotbar slot editor. These feel made
  for a phone.
- Small honest touches: "No connection. Nothing was changed.", "Leaving in 20s", "This hunt is
  above your level, expect losses", "Every kill counts, even while you're away", the stamina
  popover's plain prices, colour-coded gear stats on the doll, "Next at level 5: Cooked Wolf Meat".
- Recovering from bad signal without me doing anything.

WAITS AND HITCHES I NOTED
- First open on slow signal with a cold cache: 46 s of blank before the sign-in form (750 kbps).
  On 3G with a warm cache, 30 s to my characters. On good wifi: ~1 s. Then Play: 0.3 s (wifi),
  ~10 s (canteen), 4.6 s (mid-range phone on 4G).
- Leave: 20-33 s every time, on any signal. Sometimes with a "Leaving in 20s" countdown, sometimes
  just "Leaving..." or a vanished button.
- Switching heroes: ~7.5 s on 3G.
- Everything else (enter hunt, equip, sell, plant, cook, buy stamina) answered in under a second.

WOULD I KEEP THIS ON MY PHONE?
  Yes, but on probation. The core is exactly what I want from a commute idle game: a living little
  fight I can glance at, real progress while I'm away, and two-minute decisions (new ground, new
  gear, a task, a refill). But two things nearly made me uninstall: an archer that silently did
  nothing for seven hours while spending 8,700 of my gold, and a summary/"last hunt" that has been
  wrong since my first hour. The first one breaks trust; the second one breaks the "what did my
  hero do while I was away?" moment, which is the whole reason to open an idle game.

BEST TWO-MINUTE SESSION
  Open -> "All characters" -> see both heroes and what they're doing -> tap the one that stopped ->
  Hunts -> the ground that says "Fits your level" -> Enter. Bonus round on the sofa: Shop -> Sell
  -> Sell all loot, then tap a doll slot and wear whatever is green. My day-2 lunch did a full
  leave / take a task / re-enter in 2 min 37 s, and half of that was the Leave wait.

WHAT I COULD NOT DO ON A PHONE
- Find out what most of the 15 top icons are without opening each one (names are hover-only).
- Switch heroes or sign out on a 667 px phone (the icon bar covers those links completely).
- Use the hero tabs at all on any size (squashed to 12 px).
- Invite my own hero to a party ("right-click / long-press the tab": the tab can't be hit).
- Read why a hotbar skill isn't working (the reason lives in hover text; I only see a red slash).
- See hotbar slots 5-10 and 15-20 on a 667 px phone (they're drawn under the stance panel).
- Type in chat on a 667 px phone without first dragging the sheet to find the box.
- Pick a hero colour reliably (16 px swatches).
- See an item's stats from the bag grid (only from the doll's slot list).

THE ONE THING I'D TELL THE DEVS
  Make "while you were away" true and loud. When I open the game, show me what each hero did since
  I last looked (where, how long, kills, XP, levels, gold, drops, and WHY it stopped), and if a
  hero has done nothing for minutes ("can't attack: no arrows on the bar", "out of gold"), stop
  it and tell me in red, on the character list, before it costs me a day.

SCORES (out of 10)
  Usability: 5  — sheets, big Enter and All characters are right; tiny/hidden top-left, hover-only
                  labels, cut-off controls at 667 px and the silent archer pull it down.
  Fun:       7  — the fights look alive, levels and new grounds come quickly, gear and tasks give me
                  real two-minute decisions. Would be 8 with working summaries and parties.
  Speed:     7  — 60 fps everywhere, sub-second actions on wifi; docked for the 46 s cold start on
                  slow signal and the 20-33 s Leave.

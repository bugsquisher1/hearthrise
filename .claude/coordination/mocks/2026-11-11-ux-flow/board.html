UX flow audit, 2026-11-11

Before is today's game on the local world (the v0.2.121 set), played as Wren from a new account to Level 8. After is the same captured screen with only the flow changed: the same window frame, fonts, tokens and 32 px item art. Read AUDIT.md for the evidence and SPEC.md for the build. Screens open alone at their real size in screens/.

A. One way in, one way back

Before. A monster's facts open a second window on the left. With Hunts on the right, the world is gone (1440 x 900).

After. The monster is a page inside Hunts. The band says where you are and every crumb goes back. The icon row is in four groups: going out, your hero, the town, people.

Before, phone. The ground's page, 5 screens tall.

After, phone. One back press that names where it goes.

B. The Inventory

Before. Three gold buttons, the picked item at the foot, the grid squeezed to two rows by a sale's question.

After. The bag, what you wear, and a counter for the picked item. A press picks one item and shows it. Ctrl or Shift picks more, and the bar at the foot acts on them all.

After, Storage. Storage is the counter's second tab, so the bag and the chest are on screen together at 1440. Today opening Storage closes the bag.

Before, phone. The doll and the grid share 400 px. The picked line is under the fold.

After, phone. Worn is a tab, search is a press, the picked item rises from the foot.

C. Back in town

Before. After 4 hours the summary's gold button is the first coach card ("Every weapon is a style"). 4,587 gp of loot and 4 upgrades are not on it.

After. The summary leads the trip home: wear, sell, put away, bless, each one press, then the hunt again.

Before, phone.

After, phone. Hunt again stays pinned to the sheet's foot.

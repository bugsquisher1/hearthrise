# Art read of FB-5's first-hour screens

**Read by the art director** 2026-11-21 on `lane/r-art-first-hour`, from `set/v0.2.144` at 7842bc626 (the set's later commits up to 3dd3057d5 touch no `src/` file). Read only: no game code, data or test is changed. Build plan rule `13-build-plan.md` section 2.5; the design is D-1 in `05-designer-rulings.md` section 1.

**How.** `node tools/qa/local-world.mjs serve` played in the browser pane (a new account from the sign-in screen, the replayed chain, no production), plus the same world driven by its Playwright API for full-size shots. One new account a size: create a wand hero, enter town, Hunts, the Mill Cellar, time skips until the Cellar ends at Level 5, the end card, Build your party, three heroes, the Characters screen. A second account a size for "One at a time" and a hunt with Chat open. Sizes: 1280x720 and 1920x1080 desktop, 922x423 phone (touch), plus one measure at 1366x768. Every shot below was read. Judged against `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md` (sections 9, 10, 12), CLAUDE.md section 7 and D-1 section 1.3.

Chat sits at its home bottom left, [21, 241, 400, 280] and [21, 601, 400, 280], so they do not touch. The Town menu and the tally window open right of Chat. Hunts opens clear of Chat at 1920x1080. At 1280x720 its 955 px window has no room to, and it draws above Chat, as rule 4 allows. The picked way is a gold inset on a sunk well, which is the button system's choice look.

HEARTHRISE, PLAYED ON A PHONE AND AN OLD LAPTOP
Build v0.2.78 (main 621c27c0d), local world, played blind, 2026-10-31.
Who I am: I play in landscape on the couch with my thumbs, and at the kitchen table on an old laptop.
My opinions are marked as opinions. Everything else is something I saw or measured.

VERDICT
It works with a thumb, and nothing I did needed a mouse. No crash, no console error, no leak in 30 minutes.
The trouble is room. On a small phone the coach cards and side sheets leave so little space that the thing
you are told to press is hidden. At 6x CPU a town with nothing moving dropped to 44 fps, lower than a packed
Sewer's 49 in the same session.
"image N" below means my own screenshots, kept in my scratch folder and not committed.


1. WHAT A HEADLESS BROWSER CAN'T TELL YOU
- Real GPU: this was Playwright's headless Chromium on a strong desktop PC, drawing in software. Frame numbers
  are about the main thread, not about a real phone's GPU. A real phone may be better or worse.
- Battery and heat: not measured at all. Main-thread CPU % and requests per minute are only stand-ins.
- Real network: everything came from 127.0.0.1. The "4G" rows are Chrome's simulation, not a radio.
- On-screen keyboard: headless shows none. On a real phone in landscape it takes roughly half the height.
  Every form below is likely worse than I report. Not verified.
- Real thumbs: tap sizes are measured. Whether I'd actually miss is judgement.
- Pull-to-refresh, notch and safe areas, app switching and tab sleep: not testable here.
- CPU throttle is Chrome's multiplier on a fast PC, not a real old laptop (no slow disk, no thermal limit).
- 4x and 6x numbers are noisy. Other lanes shared the machine (13 other headless Chromes at one check, CPU 34%).
  Some 6x runs beat 4x runs. Read ranges, not single values.

SUSPECTED HARNESS, NOT THE GAME
- H1 Reload times are too high. The local server sends Cache-Control: no-store and no gzip, over HTTP/1.1,
  and there was no service worker on 127.0.0.1. So every reload re-fetched all 379 scripts. Live should be faster.
- H2 Server calls are slow here. One PGlite backend answered hr_load in 0.6 to 2.3 s. Worst-case reloads
  (18 s, 34 s, 22 s) probably include that, but I did not prove it.
- H3 The first cold load took 26.5 s because the server read files from disk for the first time. I threw it out.
- H4 "While you were away (11 min), Couch hunted Mill Cellar: +30 Level XP, +4 gold, 2 kills" appeared at 844x390
  after a 90-minute hunt I had never left the page for. Time skips moved the clocks, so I suspect the skip. Worth one check on live.
- H5 Hunt length and levels came from time skips, and the Weighhouse minimum was lowered from 10 to 2 in this
  world's memory so I could trade. No results were faked; only waiting was compressed.
- H6 My own errors, not bugs: a CDP scroll gesture did nothing where real touch events scrolled fine; a tap at (0,0)
  from a hidden button opened All characters; Ctrl+A with no field focused fired a hotkey. I also thought Leave
  took over 80 s at 844 and 922. It didn't: the button goes to zero width while counting, so my wait ended early,
  and the Shop and Weighhouse shots at those sizes were taken with the hunt still counting down.


2. PHONE PROBLEMS
Viewports 667x375, 844x390, 922x423, touch on. Worst first within each severity.

SERIOUS

P1. Coach cards eat the sheet, and hide what they tell you to press.
  667x375. The sheet is 320x275 and the coach card sits outside the part that scrolls.
  - Hunts, first time: coach says "Press the Mill Cellar". The list gets 58 px and one row is 87 px. The words
    "Mill Cellar" sit under the hint line (the title is covered by .hw-hunts-now). You see "Level 1-8" and no name.
  - Pick the ground: only "Back to the list" and Enter show. None of the ground's details are visible.
  - Shop: coach "Before the wolves" is 156 px, leaving 79 px of scroll.
    Press Got it and the next card appears at once, "Sell what only pays coin", leaving 40 px (image 152 shows
    "0 in / the" and nothing else). Cards chain, so Got it doesn't get you your screen back. Only the X does.
  - Slot setup sheet: "Your hero just ate" lands on top and the food list shrinks to about 25 px.
  - In town, the "Every weapon is a style" card clips its own text above Got it. "Your first Level" clips too.
  844x390 and 922x423: much better. Hunts list 93 px and 126 px; ground details 81 px and 114 px. Tight, usable.

P2. Tapping an item covers its own button.
  667x375, bag. Coach: "Press the Bones, then Fuel all." I tapped Bones.
  - A desktop-style tooltip about 480x185 opened right over the row holding Fuel all. Still there 3 s later.
  - Fuel all was underneath. My next tap on that spot only closed the tooltip; the count stayed 89.
  - Third tap worked. So three taps, one of them blind.
  Holding an item instead gave the action row with no tooltip (a hat, image 221), so hold is a way round it, if you
  know. The same tooltip showed up uninvited at 844x390 and 922x423: opening the Shop, a Copper Studs card sat over
  half the screen though I never tapped that row (images 228, 236). I don't know what triggered it.
  Likely Chrome's emulated hover after a tap, which real Android also does. Worth one look on a real phone.

P3. Leave and Disband are too easy to hit and can't be taken back.
  - Hunt bar at 667x375: Summary is x 501-582, Leave is x 589-644, 7 px apart, both 40 px tall.
  - Leave has no question. The first time it ended in under 2.5 s. Mid-fight it shows "Leaving in 33s" and the
    button is disabled, so a second tap does nothing. I found no way to change my mind.
  - The bar squeezes as it counts: the name becomes "Mill ..." and a tooltip gets stuck behind the Details panel.
  - Disband: one tap, no question, party gone (see P4).
  Opinion: on a couch this is the one I'd curse at. I wanted Summary.

P4. Party sheet: Leave party and Disband are outside the sheet at every phone size.
  Measured on the Your party tab:
    667x375  Leave party x 629-728, Disband x 734-810, sheet ends x 658
    844x390  Disband x 831-907, sheet ends x 835
    922x423  Disband x 909-985, sheet ends x 913
  Reachable only by dragging sideways inside the sheet (scrollWidth 460 vs 308). Nothing hints at it.
  Image 169 shows "Le" at the right edge. This one breaks "every field reachable without guessing".

P5. Roll of Honor and Report a problem are buried on a phone.
  - Laptop: each has its own icon in the top bar (trophy, warning sign).
  - Phone: neither is in the 15 icons. They sit under Heroes > All characters > "Also here", about 700 px down
    a 235 px pane. A blind person would not look there. I found it by searching every window.
  - Report a problem is also a button in Settings > Help, below the help text. That's a sensible place for it.
  - Both open and fit fine once found. Report's text box and Send fit at 667x375, without a keyboard.

SMALLER

P9. Create account: "Back to sign in" falls off the bottom.
  y 373, 44 tall, at 667x375 and 844x390. I found nothing that scrolls on that page at 667. The text above says

P10. Action bar at 667x375.
  - Slot 5 is half hidden. Slots 15 and 6-10 and 16-20 sit behind a ">" page button (24x74).
  - The preset name reads "Pre...". Rename is 36x21 and Closer/Farther are 30x36, right beside each other.
  - It sits exactly where a resting thumb goes. Tapping a slot mid-hunt opens a full-screen setup sheet that hides
    the fight. I tapped the shrimp expecting to eat. Your hero eats on its own, so the tap only opens setup.
    Opinion: that's where my thumb will land by accident. It costs a Cancel, not a mistake.

P11. Icon bar has no words.
  15 icons, 40x40, edge to edge along the top, at all sizes. Their names are only in title text ("Hunts (H)",
  "Market (M)"), which a phone never shows. Touch works; you learn them by opening each. Windows give their names.

P12. Hunt view at 667x375 is crowded.
  Party panel, hunt bar, coach and the Details panel all float on a 651x325 canvas with a 100 px action bar below.
  - In images 119 and 120 no monster was visible. In image 133 a rat and a wasp were, partly under the panels.
  - Details panel shows about 96 px of stats, its tab "Taken" is cut off, and "Cash in" covers its close X (image 134).
  - Details stays open after a reload. Sheets cover the hero (at 667 he's half under the sheet in town).
  - 844x390 and 922x423 read fine.

P13. Refusal text squeezed to one word per line.
  667x375, Hunts, party with an offline member: "Nobody went in: Pantry is away." runs down a 45 px column beside
  Start with party, name clipped (image 210). Same squeeze: the Bones row (image 143), "Nothing goes on by
  itself" (image 146), Shop "0 in / the".

P14. Party invites are quiet.
  - The Party window says "only players you invite can join" and has no way to invite. The way is tapping a name in
    chat (45x20 target), then Invite to party. All characters mentions it, far down. Not for someone with no chat history.
  - Inviter: the menu closes and nothing on screen says it was sent (image 164).
  - Invitee at 844x390: a red 1 on the hero tab and a dot on System, no toast (image 165). Join and No thanks are fine.
  - A red 1 stayed on Heroes and on the hero's row afterwards with nothing telling me what it wants.

P15. Mouse words on a phone.
  Storage: "Shift-press moves all of it. Right-click (or hold)". Friends: "Right-click or hold a name".
  Hold exists, but Shift has no touch version. I did not test moving a whole stack in Storage by touch.

P16. Small but real tap targets (under 36 px tall). Many are 30 px: Plant, Search, Show in the finder, Add friend,
  Save current setup, Ground and Parties-with selects, search boxes, sort selects, armor weight chips. Close X is 32x32.
  Tabs, Got it and coach buttons (44) are good. There's a 13x13 "on" checkbox in the bag, and the alert checkboxes
  look the same size. I did not test whether their labels take the tap.

P17. Not proven, please check on a real phone: html and body have overscroll-behavior auto and touch-action auto.
  A downward drag on the world might trigger pull-to-refresh and reload the game.

WHAT WORKED
- Portrait: a clean "Turn your phone sideways" screen.
- Reload mid-hunt at 667x375: back in the hunt, clock and panels right, in about 6 s, no errors.
- Touch scrolling, the Join button, Hunt again, Equip upgrades, buying, listing and selling all worked by thumb.
- Tapping a chat name opens a proper menu, no hover needed. No text under 12 px on the town screen (the one screen
  I counted).
- No console errors at any point I read them, on the phone or laptop sessions, including all of the soak.

4. TOP 5 TO FIX, RANKED
1. Coach cards on small phones (P1). Scroll them with the content or shrink them to a line, and stop chaining a new
   one straight after Got it. First hour, every new phone player, and it hides the thing it names.
2. Quiet the idle town. A style recalc every frame, about 30 DOM changes a second and 18 requests a minute (6 of them
   hr_town_move) with no input. 44 fps and 299 ms long tasks at 6x. This is the likely battery and server bill.
3. Make Leave and Disband safe (P3, P4). Move Disband and Leave party inside the sheet at all three sizes, allow
   cancelling "Leaving in 33s", and put space between Summary and Leave.
4. Tapped items (P2). No hover tooltip on touch, or put it where it can't cover the action row. Fix the blind second tap.
5. 379 script files on first load, and repeat calls on reload (hr_load x3 and friends). 43 s on simulated slow 4G
   at 4x CPU is where a phone player gives up. Measure this on live first, since H1 inflates my reloads.
   The 6x frame rate in party hunts (39 fps, 5.1 s of long tasks in 25 s) comes next.

Runners-up: put Roll of Honor and Report a problem where a phone can see them (P5); Weighhouse Refresh and sell-sheet
room at 667 (P6); the 16 px swatches (P8).

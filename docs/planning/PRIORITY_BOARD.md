## Decisions
_Tyler's rulings live in rulings-of-record; designer rulings inside them are recorded here._

**2026-10-21: the four loot slots (L1), sample = Meadowbrook Thicket + the Sewer (lane/c-loot-loop).** Every monster drops: coin; ONE junk row (its own product, over 15%, sells, may also be a craft input: wolf meat cooks, pelts build rooms, bone chips feed Smithing and Prayer); bones only if it has bones (the Sump Slime lost its bones row); ONE imbuement item that no other monster drops (`src/data/imbuements.js`: uncommon odds, 5-15%, tier from the monster's Level: 1 below 30, 2 from 30, 3 from 80, provisional; book value 2 g per Level, raw, so the vendor bids a fifth of that); ONE equipment row (unchanged). Rares ride only on the carrier (Old Grayjaw: Alpha Fang; the Rat King: Crown Shard). The wolves give the physical kinds (Keen, Life Leech, Ward) and the Sewer gives the caster and element kinds (Mana Leech, Element Ward), so the two grounds differ in what they supply. **Imbuing is not built (L4).** Until it is, an imbuement item drops, stacks and trades, and the loot filter keeps it, because selling is opt-in per item and it is not `junk`. The vendor is its only use (VENDOR_TRASH, to shrink when L4 lands), and its text promises nothing. **Not in this sample:** the relic slot (it needs a relic item with a use, plus `relicMult`, which `member.js` already carries, applied in `hunt-rewards.js`; that is Systems' work), the Warrens and the Mill Cellar (`IMBUE_PENDING`, next batch), and the items' own art (each one uses the nearest creature part's icon, listed as a twin in `tests/pixel-icons.mjs`; handed to the Asset Director). H10 "one family per hunt" was superseded on 10-20 (a hunt may mix families), so the Sewer keeps its mix. Evidence: one seed, one hour, real settle. Thicket solo L5: coin 1,272 to 1,269 g/h, commons 601 to 591 g/h (Grayjaw's Dire Fang left; her pelt is now certain and the Briarcoat's is 30%, so the Thicket still nets more than the Cellar before it: forest-cave's 6-seed median is 1,802 g/h against 1,738), items 336 to 337 an hour (32 of them imbuement). Sewer party of four L8: coin 2,446 to 2,448 g/h per hero, commons 179 to 197, items 112 to 112 per hero an hour (8.8 of them imbuement). Healer +128% to +130%, Sewer solo floor +101% to +104%: every margin keeps the +25% minimum. **Gray Wolf coin [2,5] to [3,5] (lane b-supplies-bury): KEPT.** The starter bar eats at 60%, so the Thicket hero buys about twice the shrimp (store 80 to 164 g/h). With only the pelts above, the Thicket nets 1,714 g/h against the Cellar's 1,738 (forest-cave, 6-seed median); with the coin it nets 1,777.5. Coin is the lever because the bill is paid in coin and the Gray Wolf is the most-killed wolf. Merged numbers: Thicket solo coin 1,329, commons 591, store 164; Sewer party per hero coin 2,365, commons 201, Healer +140%, Sewer solo +71%.
- **2026-10-22, one setup = its own bar + its gear (designer, inside Tyler's 10-18 C4 "action sets (presets) need real save/name/rename/delete/switch UI"; CONFLICTS 2026-10-22 RULED).** Cause: the bar (action set) and Hunt prep's saved setups were two named lists, and no client path reached a second bar, so every setup shared one bar. Rulings: (1) one concept, the SETUP: a name, its own action bar (slots, conditions, target, stance, chase, distance, gold rule) and the gear it wears; 5 per hero; (2) the hero is always on one setup; the bar's plaque reads "Setup <name>" and edits that setup live (remembered, never re-saved); (3) Hunt prep: Use = wear its gear and switch to its bar; Save here / Save current setup = what you have on becomes that setup; both town-only; (4) Delete never takes the setup in use (marked "In use", no Delete); (5) rename on the plaque or in Hunt prep is the same name; (6) density stays on the hunt picker. Owed (Systems): density stored per hero per ground instead of in the set's options.

- **2026-10-22, Level XP and training XP apart (designer, inside Tyler's 10-22 "divvy up level exp vs training exp" + "an option tab for premium players that shows skill exp").** Cause: the analyzer's "XP gained" / "XP per hour" and the picker's best summed the Level's XP with every combat skill's; two party heroes with identical Level XP (tyler and Fingerguns, 205,093 each) read different totals because their Melee and Defense XP differ by use. Rulings: (1) every player-facing XP figure is Level XP unless it says otherwise (analyzer Session + Per hour, hunt summary card, picker personal bests, combat log "gains N Level XP"); (2) training XP (Melee, Ranged, Magic, Defense) lives only in the premium analyzer's Training tab: a total, a total per hour, and one line per skill (its XP and its XP an hour); free characters see Level XP only; (3) no skill XP ever floats over a head: the "+XP" floater is and stays Level XP (Settings label "Level XP gained"); (4) "Raw XP per hour" becomes "Level XP per hour before bonuses" (premium); (5) personal bests are Level XP per hour, and the bests already folded on the old sum are re-folded once (2026-11-10-hunt-xp-split.sql). Party role bonus (Tibia shared-XP math) parked: Tyler 10-22 "working as intended".

**2026-10-21: Mosslight Glade, the solo ground for levels 16-31 (H11; lane/c-ground-16-31). Built, fitted and server-catalogued, but STAGED: it is not listed and the server refuses it until its style-1 art lands.** *The gap:* today a solo hero at 26-31 has no ground in band, and the Hunts window recommends the Mill Cellar at Level 31. *The place:* Tyler's workbook H-003. It lies upstream of the Thicket, where the Meadowbrook rises from springs under the Warrens' copper hill. The goblins wash their ore in the spring run, and the copper water makes the moss glow. The map has two banks with six clearings: Crow Field, Poachers' Camp, then over the goblins' sluice boards (the one 1-tile choke) to the Sluice, the Mosslight Springs, Rook's Hide (a ruined spring-house) and Vine Hollow, then back over the ford. It is a solo ground, and up to three more heroes may come. *Creatures:* mixed families, as ruled on 10-20.
- Carrion Crow (L16, common): it follows the poachers' kills. Arrows bring it down.
- Glade Poacher (L19): it hits hard and is fragile. It takes pelts off the Meadowbrook wolves.
- Walking Vine (L22, the tank): slow and woody. A blade or fire answers it.
- Goblin Sluicer (L26): it pans the Warrens' copper and is soaked, so fire hisses off it.
- Rook the Poacher (L29): he holds the spring-house and is the one rare carrier.

Of Tyler's workbook creatures, the Mischief Sprite, the Pixie Trickster and the Briarling were folded out as generic fey. Fey belong to the Thornwild, and Greenhaven is roads, greenwood and ruins.

*Four slots:* each creature has one junk row (Bone Chips, Wolf Pelt, Magic Essence, Copper Ore, wolf meat) and one imbuement item of its own: Crow Quill (Keen), Snare Wire (Life Leech), Mosslight Tendril (Mana Leech), Sluice Crust (Ward), Rook's Moss Charm (Element Ward). The ground drops all five gear slots, about one piece every 1.5 h per hero: studded boots, a longbow, an iron helm, an iron platebody and studded legs, so the Ranger's line gets its first source. The rare is Rook's Whistle, about every 15 h per hero.

*Evidence (real settle, six seeds, per hero-hour at each floor):* net gold rises from the Sewer's party at 2,248, to the glade solo at 2,907, to the Warrens' party at 3,428. Level XP rises from 36,579 to 55,075 to 147,321. A ladder-kit Melee at Level 16 lives the hour on all six seeds. A practice-sword hero in Thicket leather dies on one of six seeds, at 33 minutes. At Level 24 the glade gives more XP than the Sewer solo (59.8k against 46.0k) and less gold (3,436 against 4,318), so the two grounds compete on different axes. At Level 8, below its range, the glade costs gold and the hero dies. Levelling from 16 to 32 here takes about 23 h.

*Weak spots (named):*
- Magic Essence's crafting uses open at Level 60 and up, so at 16-31 it is a stockpile and vendor gold.
- The ranged and magic bag guides are unplayed.
- With the ladder kit, the bill at the floor is small (150-250 g/h). The margin minimum holds, but the glade is easy for an iron-clad hero.

*To open it:* the Asset Director paints the ground and draws the five creatures' walks (list below). Then the hunt loses `staged`, the map moves to WORLD_MAPS, and the catalogues are regenerated. `forest-cave staged-until-art` holds that pairing.

### Decision: what the game now teaches (ruled and built)

**2026-10-22: the Bestiary and the collection ladders count what a player can meet, and no milestone pays gems (lane/c-monster-ladders).**

*The bug:* the Bestiary total and the milestones counted the whole 129-monster catalogue, but only 19 monsters stand on open grounds and they drop only 49 items. *The ruling:* a rung is a share of the reachable roster, so a new open ground raises every goal by itself.
- Monster rungs are sixths of `hr_hunt_roster`. Today the goals are 4 / 7 / 10 / 13 / 16 / 19.
- Item rungs are fifths of `hr_hunt_loot_roster`. Today the goals are 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 / 49.
- Both rosters are generated from the open grounds (`hunt-grounds.js reachableOf`, written by gen-hunt-sessions). Staged grounds are out.
- Only kills and loot on the roster count toward a rung.
- No rung pays premium currency (Tyler L12). The gold is unchanged.

*For Tyler (money):*

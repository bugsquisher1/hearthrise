DOMINANT STRATEGY (first day, as far as I got: Lv 1 -> 11)
 1. Lv1-5: Mill Cellar, density "+" until it greys out (8 each). x2.1 XP vs as built and still +20% balance.
    Pick two axes (+11% XP vs sword, eats no food at Lv1) — put arrows on the bar first if you insist on the bow.
 2. Burn every Bone in the Great Hearth. Never sell them. 279 bones = Inner Flame 8 = +13.7% regen forever, and that
    regen is what let Tally hunt Wickstead with ZERO food.
 3. Lv8+: Wickstead. As built for gold (~4,000 gp/h, no supplies), 8-each for XP (~42k XP/h, ~3,100 gp/h).
    Skip The Sewer solo (1,620 gp/h, eats 176 shrimp/h).
 4. Buy every stamina refill (~375-425 gp per hour of hunting vs ~4,000 earned).
 5. Re-enter hunts every 4 h (they stop at 4:00 whatever your stamina says).
 6. Hoard Wheat from the Mill and sell it 1,000 at a time (10 gp each — the best "loot" I found). Use "Sell all
    loot" for junk, then sell Wheat/Silk/gear by hand.
 7. Slot 3 at 15k (about 3 h of 2 heroes), slot 4 at 40k (about 4 h of 3 heroes + selling). I reached 4 heroes
    in about 7 hours of game time. Slot 5 at 120k is the first real wall.

NUMBERS THAT DISAGREED
 - "Up to 4 can play at once" vs 2 slots on a new account (needs 15k + 40k gold; not said where it's promised).
 - Density "+" (More monsters) on "As built (2 to 3)" goes DOWN to "1 each". "2 packs of 8" next to "Up to 8 a pull".
 - Shop: "Sell all loot · 423 gp" over a list worth 1,215 (it skips crafting materials, silently).
 - Shop: "Rat Tail ×2,004 · 1 gp each · Sell · 1,000 gp" (hidden 1,000-per-sale cap); "Sell for 10,000" paid 10,012.
 - Analyzer/hunt bar/summary/"This hunt" show an older, ended hunt after a hero switch or time away (every hero,
   every time). The worst one, because it's the screen I trust most.
 - Two overnight hunts ended at exactly 4:00 with ~6 h of stamina left; no message, no summary.
 - Supplies: own food shown as "free"; own arrows not listed at all; per-row costs and per-row loot values "–".
 - In-hunt max HP stuck at the entry value after level-ups (110/150 at Lv5 -> 190/190 in town).
 - HUD "Food x0" while the hero eats store shrimp; coach toast "You have 0 left" frozen for 30+ minutes.
 - Fishing "caught so far" vs bag count vs XP drift by up to 3 at the same moment.
 - "Hunt again" -> "You no longer have that many." (of what?)

WHAT'S POINTLESS (for gold, so far)
 - Kill-task gold (100-1,090 gp a day). Farming turnips. Selling bones. Offensive stance on Mill Cellar (same 264
   kills as Balanced). The Sewer alone. Fishing+cooking at Lv1 roughly ties a hunter, with more clicks.
 - Not pointless, just unclear: armor weights. Heavy/medium/light percentages exist on Tamsin's page ("up to
   +28% HP for a full set") but the bag shows "+0% max HP" lines and never a number per piece.

WHAT'S THE ACTUAL CHASE AFTER A WEEK
 - Slot 5 (120k) and keeping 4 heroes in stamina refills. Rare drops (Rat King's Crown 0.1%, Grayjaw's Ruff
   0.17%) and the trinket table at Wickstead. The House tiers (Homestead -> Castle) — this is where Woodcutting and
   Mining finally pay, and it's the first sink big enough to matter. Mosslight Glade (16-31) and Coppercap (32-50)
   for the next XP tier. Inner Flame levels from bones.
 - Missing for me: a reason to keep the gold. After slot 5 I don't see a gold sink besides refills.

WOULD I KEEP PLAYING?
 Yes, for at least the week. The away-rate matches the watched rate, the gold arithmetic is honest to the gp
 wherever I could actually see both sides, the hunt card's drop % and supply costing are better than most idle
 games ship with, and density vs stance vs ground is a real optimisation space (Wickstead 8-each vs as built is
 a genuine trade-off). Four heroes ran at a flat 60 fps on 2560x1440 with no stutter. What would make me quit is
 the receipts: an idle game lives on "you were away, here's what happened", and right now I mostly came back to a
 stale panel and had to divide purse deltas by hand.

THE ONE THING I'D TELL THE DEVS
 The analyzer is the best thing in your game and it lies after every hero switch or time away. Make the panel
 always show the hunt the hero is IN, and give every ended hunt (including the 4 h stop) a summary I can find.

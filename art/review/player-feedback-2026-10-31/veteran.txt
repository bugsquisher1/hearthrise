Posted by: an old Edron knight (20 yrs Tibia on and off, some OSRS, some Huntera). Desktop, 1920x1080, Chrome.
Played on a local copy of the real game; waiting skipped with a time-skip, results are what the server gave me.
Screenshots: art/review/player-feedback-2026-10-31/veteran/NN-name.png (15 kept, half size).
A tag like [shot 05-cellar-analyzer-30m] names the screenshot for that moment; other [tags] are moments I

=====================================================================
DAY ONE — SIGN-IN AND CHARACTER LIST
=====================================================================

[shot 01-signin] Opened the page. Crest on the left, a small sign-in box on the right, the rest of a 1080p
screen is black. Loaded in about a second, no hitching. The crest is nice. It looks like a launcher, not a
game, which is fine for a login. Making an account was three fields and done; no captcha, no fuss.

[characters] "Your characters. Up to 4 can play at once." ...and it shows me TWO slots. Which is it, 2 or 4?
First thing I read contradicts the first thing I see. Small, but a vet notices this stuff. If the other two are
premium or unlock later, say so on the slot ("locked — unlocks at ..."), don't just leave them out.

[shot 02-create-hero] Character creation: name, then Head/Body/Legs/Feet colour rows with 16 swatches each.
I've seen that exact four-part outfit colouring for twenty years. It works, it's what I'd want, but let's not
pretend it's new. "There's no class to pick. The weapon you hold is how you fight" — I like that. Five roles
spelled out in one line each (tank / two-hander / ranged / wand / staff healer). Clear. No stats to roll,
no 40-minute character creator. Spaces are allowed in names ("Ottmar Grimbeard"), good.

[shot 03-town-first] Into Havora. Took ~1.5 s from "Enter town" to standing in the square. Top-down pixel town,
water, a wooden bridge, cobbles, a big stone plaza. Feels like home, honestly — the camera angle and the
tile look are in the family I grew up on. Two gripes right away:
  - Scale. My knight is maybe a third the height of ONE paving stone. The plaza stones read like boulders.
    The town tiles and the character sprites don't feel like they're on the same grid.
  - Fifteen tiny unlabeled icons across the top right. Hovering gives a name, but on first sight it's a
    row of hieroglyphs. And a signpost in the middle of my capital city that says "To the sandbox". Sandbox?
    That's a dev word, not a place name.
Action bar at the bottom: 20 slots, slot 1 already has a "challenge" (shield) and slot 2 a power strike,
then shrimp, salted shrimp and turnip juice. HP 150, MP 100, Food x20, stamina 12:00h with a + to buy more,
a Charm slot. Setup "Preset 1", Target: Nearest/Lowest HP/Highest % life/Strongest/Follow the leader,
Offensive/Balanced/Defensive, Chase, Distance. OK. Now we're talking. That's a real fight-config panel,
the kind of thing a Tibia player spends hours fiddling with. Promising.

[shot 04-hunts-window] Hunts window. Six grounds: Mill Cellar 1-8, Meadowbrook Thicket 5-15, The Sewer 8-25,
Wickstead 8-18, Mosslight Glade 16-31, Coppercap Warrens 32-50. Each shows who lives there (Giant Rat lv2
common, Slime lv2 uncommon, Hive Wasp lv4 uncommon), drop chances AS PERCENTAGES (helmets 0.65% per kill,
Queen's Comb 0.072% from the wasp), the respawn ("2 packs of 2 to 3 · back in 32 s"), and the supply bill
(own food first, then store shrimp 14 gp each, juice 10 gp each, "500 gp pays the store as you use it").
  LOVED: exact drop rates and exact respawn on the spot list. In Tibia we spent years datamining that stuff
  off fan wikis. Here it's on the tin. Some people will say it kills the mystery; I say it respects my time.
  LOVED: the store auto-buys supplies as I use them. No more "ran out of mana pots at the bottom of a cave".
  WORRY: six grounds for levels 1-50. That's thin. In Tibia a level-30 has a dozen viable spots per vocation.
  If there's only one spot per band, there's no "spot", there's just "the next one", and no reason to fight
  over a respawn.
[character sheet] Character sheet: Melee/Ranged/Magic/Defense/Inner Flame skills, each 1, 15 XP to 2.
"Next at level 15: Copper Whetstone". Damage 1-4, hit 60% vs no armor, Defense 3, Dodge/Absorb/Ward.
Bestiary 0 of 30. Speed 3.2 tiles/s. Capacity in bag SPACES (100), not weight. I'll live without cap
management, but I'll miss it a bit — cap was half the decision about what loot to keep.
[inventory] Paperdoll on the left (head, neck, body, legs, feet, two hands, rings, etc), grid bag on
the right with shortcuts printed underneath (/ search, Delete sell, L lock, Ctrl+A). Keyboard bag handling!
Someone on the team has actually played these games. "Dispatch loot: worth 206 gp (9 unpriced)" — a
one-button sell/deposit. Good.
[gathering & crafting] Gathering and crafting: woodcutting, mining, fishing, farming, cooking, crafting,
smithing, runecrafting. Each with "next at level X: thing". That's OSRS DNA (the 83 XP to level 2 is a wink
at the OSRS curve, I see you). We'll see if any of it feeds the hunting, or if it's eight parallel bars.
[shop] Shop sells gloves, belts, a ring, a cape, a necklace, tools, seeds. "Helmets, bodies, legs and
boots come from monsters." Good — armor is loot, not a shop list. That's the right call.

FIRST HUNT — MILL CELLAR (level 1)
=====================================================================

[cellar, first seconds] Set Offensive + Chase (rats don't hurt), picked Mill Cellar, pressed Enter. 0.35 s later I'm
in. Steady 60 fps on the hunt view, worst frame 33 ms over a 10 s sample. No stutter. Good.
The cellar is ONE room, one screen: sacks, barrels, a millstone, braziers, cobwebs, a wasp hive up in the
corner. It's well dressed, honestly — nicest-looking rat room I've seen in a browser game. But it's an
arena, not a cave. In Tibia the Rookgaard sewer is a tunnel you walk; you choose which way to go, you
lure, you know where the respawns are. Here the rats spawn into a room and my knight auto-fights them.
"Your hero fights alone. You don't click to attack." Fine, that's the idle contract. I knew that coming in.
[cellar, 34 s] 34 seconds: 5 kills, 77 XP, 60% to level 2. The Hunt Analyzer on the right is... the Tibia
hunt analyzer. Session / XP / Gold / Loot value / Supplies / Balance / per hour. Not "inspired by", that's
the same panel, same words. I'll be honest: I'm happy it's here, it's the single most useful window Tibia
ever added, but if you're worried about looking like a copy, this is the first thing CipSoft fans will
screenshot. Make it yours (a ledger page? a hunting journal?) but keep every number.

[shot 05-cellar-analyzer-30m] Closed the browser, came back 30 minutes later. Level 5. 242 kills, 3,726 XP,
352 gp + 605 gp loot = 957 gp balance, 0 gp supplies (ate one of my own shrimp). XP/h 7,215, balance/h
1,853 gp. Next level in 00:39. It kept fighting with the tab closed. THAT is the hook for me — I can go to
work and my knight's still killing rats.
  - A little notice popped: "Your HP fell under 60%, so slot 3 ate a Cooked Shrimp. You have 19 left; after
    that slot 4 buys Salted Shrimp at 14 gp." That's exactly the kind of explanation I want. More of these.
  - Loot list: Rat Tail x58, Slime Gel x33, Bones x32, Small Fang x20, Wheat x16 (wheat? off a rat — ok,
    it's a mill, I'll allow it), Lamp Jelly x9, Venom Sac x7, Giant Rat Hide x5, Bronze Helm x2, Leather Coif
    x2, Silk Thread x2, Hive Wasp Stinger x1. Proper creature products. Every item has a dash "–" where I'd
    expect its value. The total says 605 gp but I can't see which line made it. In Tibia's loot analyzer
    each line shows its value. That's the column that tells me what to pick up next time. Add it.
  - The analyzer is tall and the loot list falls off the bottom of the panel; the tab row "Session /
    Training / Taken / Dea…" is clipped at 1920 wide. Fourth tab is literally cut in half.
[analyzer tabs] Training tab: Melee +1,462 (2,784/h, next level in 0:02h), Defense +401. Taken: Hive Wasp
895, Giant Rat 667, Slime 144 — the wasps are the ones biting, which matches the level 4 next to their name.
Dealt: Power Strike 537, Basic attack 438. That's real info.
  Oddity: "Dealt" totals 975 damage for 242 kills, while I've TAKEN 1,706. Four damage per kill? Either
  rats have 4 HP or the Dealt tab isn't counting the whole session. Something's off there.

[hour-one summary] Pressed Leave hunt. "Leaving in 30s" — my knight has to walk out, can't log out mid-
fight. Good, that's a rule I respect (Tibia's battle sign, basically). Summary card: 1h12, 568 monsters,
8,743 XP, 834 gp + 1,499 gp loot, balance/h 1,925 gp, XP/h 7,216. Level 6. A "Next: Feed the Hearth"
button. The analyzer said next level in 1:50 at this spot, so the cellar is done for me; it told me when to
move on without telling me. That's how it should be.

[shot 06-item-bronze-helm] Clicked the Bronze Helm. THIS tooltip is good:
  Defense +3, Ranged accuracy -1, Magic accuracy -2 ("against an empty slot" in green/red),
  "Heavy armor: adds max HP and absorbs part of each blow. This piece: +4.6% max HP, of the +28% a full
  heavy set of 6 gives", Comes from: Hive Wasp, Gray Wolf, Barn Wasp. Value: shop pays 35 gp.
  Loot plan: Keep in bag.
  That's a real item. Trade-offs (heavy = HP and absorb but hurts casting), a set bonus I can work toward,
  where it drops, what it sells for. I immediately know Leather Coif is the medium version and Apprentice Hat
  the light one. The armor-weight triangle is the first thing that made me think "ok, there's a system".
  The bag even says "Nothing goes on by itself: each piece is better at something." Respect.
  Gripe: "Equip upgrades" was greyed out with no hint why (because nothing is strictly better, which the
  text says further down, but the button should say it).
[shot 07-feed-hearth] Bones -> "Fuel all 66" -> my knight walks to the Great Hearth and burns them for Inner
Flame XP (my regen skill). That's bury-bones-for-Prayer from OSRS with a new coat. Fine. It's a sink for
junk and it ties bones to a combat stat, which I like. Bugs I saw:
  - The bag cell says 54 while the line under it says "x66 · 12 being used".
  - The status "Fueling the Great Hearth with Bones: 54 Bones left. Your bag and Inner Flame XP update as it
    goes" is cut off at the bottom of the inventory window at 1080p.
  - "Fuel all 66" stays lit and pressable while the line below says "Already fueling ... Stop it first."

[item menu / loot plans] Right-click an item: Loot plan (Keep in bag / Deposit in storage / Sell) and On pickup
(Don't pick up). A loot filter AND auto-sell rules per item. Set rat tails, fangs, venom sacs, stingers,
hides, cores and wheat to Sell, pressed Dispatch loot: "Sold 7 for 1,108 gp." That's a loot bot I don't get
banned for. Wonderful. This is where the "wasted time" in old MMOs went to die, and I don't miss it.
Item flavour text: hovering everything I looted, I found
  - Giant Rat Hide "for the imbuer who wards armor" — Used for: Sell it at the shop.
  - Sticky Core "for the imbuer who wards gear against the elements" — Used for: Sell it at the shop.
  - Venom Sac "milked for poisons and blade coatings" — Used for: Sell it at the shop.
  The text promises an imbuing system (yes, I know exactly which game that's from) and the item says
  "vendor it". Either build the imbuer or stop writing cheques the game can't cash. Right now it reads like
  the description was written for a game that doesn't exist yet.
  - Best money in the cellar is WHEAT at 10 gp each (54 = 540 gp) — more than every rat part combined.
    Rats dropping the highest-value loot as grain is funny, and honestly kind of charming for a mill.
[shop gear] Bought Bronze-Rimmed Shield, Bronze Belt, Copper Ring, Copper Studs, Traveler Cape (1,020 gp).
  - Shop tooltips for the Bronze-Rimmed Shield, Traveler Cape and Hunter Necklace show NO stats at all.
    The cape says it "eases the road and sharpens what you learn" — sounds like an XP bonus? Tell me the
    number. I paid 300 gp for vibes.
  - Every shop item says "Value: The shop has not priced this yet." The shop I'm standing in, that's
    selling it to me, hasn't priced it. Reads like a placeholder.
  - Gloves are medium armor ("makes more blows miss you"), belt is heavy. 2 heavy pieces = +9.3% max HP.
    Max HP went 209 -> 218. Melee skill 8, Defense 5, Inner Flame 6 (+10.7% regen from burnt bones).

=====================================================================
PUSHING TOO HIGH — AND NOT DYING
=====================================================================
[knight in the glade] Knight, level 11, alone, Offensive, into Mosslight Glade (16-31, "This hunt is above your
level, expect losses"). I expected to die inside 20 minutes and write a paragraph about the death screen.
1h31 later: level 12, 57,686 XP/h (more than the party Sewer!), 4,406 gp/h, HP never under 282/308, five
shrimp eaten. No losses. Character sheet explains it: "Absorb 50.1% of each blow" from five pieces of
starter BRONZE. Half of every hit gone at level 11. Heavy armor is way overtuned.
[healer in the glade] Fine, the squishy one then. Healer, level 9, light cloth, alone, Mosslight Glade.
41 minutes: 57,935 XP/h, HP dipped to 176/230 once, self-heals with Greater Mend, 1,388 gp/h of runes and
juice, +2,331 gp/h balance. Didn't die either.
  So: going FIVE TO SEVEN levels above a ground's floor is the best XP in the game and it's free. "Expect
  losses" is a lie. The level ranges on the hunt list don't mean anything. In Tibia, the first lesson is
  that the wrong cave kills you; that fear is what makes a good spot a good spot and makes a party worth
  having. Without risk, the whole "pick your spot, read your analyzer, decide if it's profitable" loop
  collapses to "pick the highest one that lets you in".
  I wanted to die. I tried. I couldn't. I'd call that the biggest design problem in the game.
[warrens] Coppercap Warrens (32-50, "A party of 2 to 4, or alone from Level 45"): "You are too far below
this hunt to enter it yet." Fair gate. BUT in a party the "Start with party" button stays lit; I pressed it
and nothing happened. No message, no refusal text, nothing. Grey it out or tell me why.
  Also: Mosslight Glade still says "No best yet" for both heroes after 1h31 and 41 min there (Wickstead and
  the Sewer recorded fine).
[shot 14-glade-healer-solo] "The copper water makes the moss glow" — the Glade looks like the Thicket: grass,
dirt path, oaks, a brazier. I didn't see glowing moss or copper water anywhere. Write the map to match the
card or the card to match the map. And the "Plant your land" coach bubble has been sitting on top of every
hunt view for eight hours now. It never left. It's covering the fight.

THE VERDICT
=====================================================================
WHAT I LOVED
  - The action-slot condition editor. "Cast X when [the HP of] [the lowest ally] [is below] 70%", stacked
    conditions, slot priority, rune source per slot. It's a healing/attack script I write myself, inside
    the rules. This is the deep system, and it's genuinely fresh in a browser idle game.
  - Item tooltips: stats against what I'm wearing, heavy/medium/light trade-offs with set bonuses, where it
    drops, what it sells for, what it crafts into. The armor-weight triangle is the best idea in the loot.
  - Exact drop rates and respawn timers on every hunting ground card. Respects my time.
  - Loot plans + Dispatch loot (Sell / Deposit / Don't pick up). The cleanest loot handling I've used.
  - Hunts keep going with the browser closed. Two heroes hunting at once. Partying my own heroes in two
    clicks.
  - The Sewer with the Rat King: a real dungeon layout, a named boss you need a party for, and a loot table
    with a crown, a buckler made of a drain grate and a knot that turns the town's fire blue for everyone.
  - The world writing. Mill rats -> the sewer under town -> "the rats answer to someone" -> the Rat King.
    The brook in the Thicket rises in the Glade. Creatures drop their own body parts. It hangs together.
  - The hunt analyzer itself. Yes it's Tibia's, and yes I'm glad.

WHAT FEELS COPIED OR THIN
  - Copied (and I don't mind most of it, but you should know how it reads): four-part outfit colours,
    the hunt analyzer panel word for word, runes for spells, 12 h stamina with paid refills, kill-task
    board, burying bones for a regen skill (OSRS), the 83 XP to level 2 on every trade skill (OSRS), item
    text about "the imbuer" (Tibia). Tibia vets will spot every one in their first hour.
  - Thin: six hunting grounds for levels 1-50, one per band, so there's no choosing a spot, only the next
    one. No danger. No weapon drops until hour seven. Supplies that don't matter for a knight. "Imbuer"
    flavour on items that only sell. The Glade card says glowing moss and copper water; the map is a green
    forest like the Thicket.

THE ONE THING I'D TELL THE DEVS
  Make hunting dangerous. Right now the best XP in the game comes from walking five to seven levels above a
  ground's range, alone, and nothing can kill you (bronze heavy armor absorbs 50% of every blow at level
  11). Without risk the analyzer has nothing to measure, supplies don't matter, parties are just multipliers,
  and "expect losses" is a lie. Tibia is a game about the fear of the wrong cave. Give me that fear and
  every good system you've built (the editor, the analyzer, the armor triangle, the party grounds) suddenly
  has a reason to exist.

BEST SYSTEM: the action-bar condition editor ("Configure action"). Runner-up: item tooltips + armor weights.
WORST SYSTEM: risk and death — there effectively isn't any. Close second: the hunt analyzer's reliability
  (frozen on an old hunt after reload, three hunt summaries missing, no end reason when stamina ran out).

WHAT I EXPECTED THAT WASN'T THERE
  - Dying. A death screen, a death penalty, a reason to bring a healer.
  - Weapon upgrades early, and boss rares that are worth more than gold.
  - Other players: a market, trading my Rat King's Crown, seeing another party in the Sewer, fighting over a
    respawn. "2 online" was always just me.
  - Supplies as a real cost for a melee character (food/potions mattering on the knight's analyzer).
  - Per-item value in the analyzer's loot list (every line is a "–").
  - The imbuing system the item descriptions keep promising.
  - Someone pointing me at Tamsin's kill tasks instead of the turnip patch.

WOULD I STILL BE PLAYING IN A MONTH?
  Honestly: the first week, yes. The condition editor and the Sewer party night are the real thing, and
  being able to leave two heroes hunting while I'm at work is exactly my kind of game. A month? Not as it
  plays today. By day three I'd worked out that the answer to every hunt is "the highest one that lets me
  in", nothing can kill me, and the loot I care about is locked behind level 15 or sells for gold. That's a
  spreadsheet, not a hunt. Add risk, a few more grounds per level band, early weapons and a player market,
  and I'd put the serious hours in. The bones are good.

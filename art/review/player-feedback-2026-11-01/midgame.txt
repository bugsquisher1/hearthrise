          plentiful. HP stayed 94%.
  Weapon style: two axes (the weakest "Practice" tier) 126k vs sword+shield 79k at the same density and ground (+60%). Confound: Aldric went

          end HP 42% instead of 94%. The "Taking a beating?" coach tip fires. Axes were never abandoned afterwards, so for XP/h shield-and-sword
          never competed again. No shield bonus I can see pays for 60%.
  Armor weight (Tamsin's choice, Heavy/Medium/Light): I picked Heavy once, +18.6% max HP shown on the bag. I never saw numbers that let me
          compare weights; no way to test cheaply. Untested.

9. OPINIONS (marked)
================================================================================
 OPINION  The ground ladder is a good chase while it lasts: seven times the XP from Cellar to Glade, readable "Best N Level XP/h", honest "Above your
          level" labels. I never felt lied to on XP.
 OPINION  Level 10-25 is a treadmill with one lever (the Hunts window). The two levers I found (density, axes) are worth +110% combined and are
          hidden: a density stepper inside the Hunts detail and a weapon in the starter bag. Tell the player. Without them the hero sits at 58k XP/h.
 OPINION  Gear is where the game thinks it is a hunter MMO and then does not deliver: no armor tier shows up between Level 5 and Level 25 except through
          drops I could not use, and the sheet's Defense and Dodge hardly move. The crafting route (Smithing 15 etc.) is not signposted, so a hunter
          never sees it.
 OPINION  Gold after Level 10 is a number that grows with no outlet except stamina. 61k at Level 25 is a smell. A sink that matters (armor tiers, boss
          entry, a real house step) is missing.
 OPINION  Party design is smart (4 classes, 20% less XP when missing) but the alt-carry path is a trap with a Level 1 alt.
 OPINION  Coppercap should be the next chase, but solo it is a loss; the game should say so before I enter ("supplies about 6,500 gp/h at your level").

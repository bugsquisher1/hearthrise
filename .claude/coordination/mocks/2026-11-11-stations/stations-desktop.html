Crafting stations, desktop 1920 x 1080

Static mock. Every station (the forge and anvils, the crafting benches, the kitchen, the rune altars) opens one window: Gathering and crafting, on its skill (src/shell/windows/work.js). The before shots are that window on main v0.2.118, served by tools/qa/local-world.mjs, for Mira at Smithing 18, Cooking 12, Crafting 9 and Runecrafting 4, with some things made, some she can make and some locked. The after shots are the same page with only the window's body changed. Same frame, same width, same place, same fonts, same 32 px item art. Every number is the game's own.

The problems, worst first

  1. A wall of text. At 1920 x 1080 the Smithing window shows 3 of its 17 rows, and its list scrolls 2,615 px in a 693 px body. On a 922 x 423 phone it shows none: the intro fills the first screen. Each row runs 4 to 6 lines, and the bench fee sentence repeats on nearly every row after the intro already said it (phone shot).
  2. Can make, can't make and locked look alike. At Smithing 18 the list holds 8 she can make, 7 she's short for and 2 locked, mixed. Only a dimmed "Start" tells them apart, and "Start" looks like plain text.
  3. Short of materials, it names one input in a long sentence. Stitch Houndskin Cloak never says it needs a Bear Pelt. The Wolf Pelt line is 4 lines of link with a lone "." on a line by itself. The runes repeat "For your own use. The shop and the Weighhouse won't take it." six times (shot).
  4. A row doesn't say what the thing is for, or how many you have. Cook Wolf Meat never says it heals 72. Iron Hatchets never say you need Level 15 to wear them (Mira is Level 1). Her 12 Cooked Shrimp never show. The skills list already knows: "Cooked Trout (food, heals 168)".
  5. The running job scrolls away. "Making Bronze Bar: 1 made so far, 12 Copper Ore left" sits above the intro. Scroll to the row and it's gone, and the row only says "Working on it now" (shot).

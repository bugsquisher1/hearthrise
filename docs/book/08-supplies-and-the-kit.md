# 8. Supplies and the kit

What your heroes burn while they hunt, where it comes from, and how the plan keeps it stocked.

---

## 8.1 The kit: four lines
**[New in 0.3]** for the shape, **[Unchanged]** for food and charges

Every hero carries a kit of four lines. Two are the floor, two are decisions. That's the whole supply game.

| Line | What | Who makes it | In 0.3? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meal** | Food and drink. The only things that restore health and mana | Cooking | Yes |
| **Charge** | What your style burns: whetstones, arrows or bolts, runes | Smithing, Woodcrafting, Runecrafting | Yes |
| **Imbuements** | Three timed pieces for the ground (chapter 6) | Artificing | Yes |
| **Draught** | One per hero, picked for the ground. Never a heal | Alchemy | **[Later patch]** |

No fifth small percent is ever added.

**Kit cost.** The aim is that supplies eat 20% to 30% of what a hunt pays, for every style. Today a mage's first hunt can lose gold. Lane W1 evens out what each style's charges cost.

## 8.2 Food
**[Unchanged]**

- Food restores **health**. Half at once, half over 6 seconds.
- Only food heals. Nothing from Alchemy ever will (Tyler, CR-Q2).
- Your bar eats by itself. New heroes eat under 60% health.
- Food also sets how fast a party gets ready for the next pull (chapter 5), so Cooking has the steadiest demand in the game.

## 8.5 Charges
**[Unchanged]**

Each style burns its own charge.

**Melee: whetstones.** Coarse (1), Copper (15), Iron (30), Steel (45), Mithril (60), Rune (75), Dawnsteel (88).

**Ranged: arrows and bolts.** Bronze Arrows (1), Barbed Arrows (15), Steel Arrows (30), Mithril Arrows (45), Rune Arrows (60), Emberhead Arrows (75), Dawnpoint Arrows (88). Iron Arrows and Iron Bolts sit between Barbed and Steel. Precision Shot uses 1, Raking Shot 2, Flame Volley 3.

**Magic: runes.** Six families, five grades each.

## 8.6 The store's basics
**[Changed]**

- **The store never runs out.** A hunt never stops for stock. When your own supplies run out, the plan switches to the store's version (or ends the hunt, your choice).
- Store food and drink are plain versions: **Salted Shrimp**, **Thin Turnip Juice**, and so on up the ladder.
- **In 0.3 the store sells only the floor.** Its basics are a tier behind what players make and cost 1.5 times as much. Crafted goods are better and cheaper, and they run out.

**Today the store is wider.** It sells cooked fish, every juice, every arrow tier, and every rune grade from Chalked (1 gold) to Graven (5 gold). Its basics work at 75% of the real thing for twice the price.

Not final: the switch to "basics only, a tier behind, 1.5×" is lane K1. See the contradictions list for the two store rules in the data.

## 8.7 Restocking
**[Unchanged]**

- Your plan restocks before each hunt: from **Storage first, then the store**, by rule.
- The bag guide aims for about 3 hours of supplies per hero in the bag.
- Supplies from Storage are used before anything is bought.
- A gold floor (default 300) stops the plan buying when you're nearly broke.

**[Later patch]:** with the market, stock rules become standing buy orders, so your company buys what it burns from other players while you're away.

## 8.8 Draughts and the camp pot
**[Later patch]**

- **Draughts** (Alchemy): one per hero, an answer to the ground, one an hour while its rule is wanted. Never a heal.
- **The camp pot**: one per party.
- Imbuements, draughts and the camp pot share one small cap. Three imbuements fill it on their best ground, so a draught is chosen **in place of** an imbuement, never on top. The hero card shows "Consumables 6 / 6". Your plan never buys a line that would add nothing.

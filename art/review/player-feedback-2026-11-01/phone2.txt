== 5. OPINION (marked) ==
OPINION: the single biggest phone cost is vertical space. Top bar (40) + title row (50) + action bar (100) leave about 200 px for the world, and a sheet gets 190 px of scrolling body. The layout is clearly built for this (sheets, sticky buttons), but every list (shop, bag, tally) is a long thin scroll.
OPINION: the hunt is the best part on a phone. Pull-and-watch is exactly one-thumb. The "While you were away" card and the summary give the numbers I would want on a bus.
OPINION: the colour swatches and the action bar slots are the only things where I would mis-tap daily.
OPINION: 29 fps at 4x throttle in Meadowbrook with 51 percent of the main thread in long tasks would feel janky on a low-end phone; Mill Cellar is smoother (40 fps).

== 6. LIKED ==
- Portrait: a clear full-screen "Turn your phone sideways / Hearthrise plays in landscape" with the crest; rotating back restored the same screen without reload, the hunt kept running (screenshot 37).
- Landscape-only design with a top icon bar (44x40 per icon), slide-out sheets, a 40x40 close X on every sheet and a sticky action (Enter, Equip) at the bottom of the detail.
- No hover, keyboard shortcut or right-click was needed anywhere. Long press on a slot is the touch answer to hover, and it shows real detail.
- Hunt screen is readable: HP and MP in the orb and the party card, cooldown numbers on the bar, "miss" and damage numbers, the hunt clock, Leave and Summary in the top strip.
- "Next: Plant your land", the pulsing icon in the top bar, and the coach cards say what to do next without a tutorial wall. "Press the Mill Cellar, then Enter" matched the real controls.
- Away return: "While you were away (8 h 1 min), Pippa hunted Mill Cellar: Level 6 to 8, +60,880 Level XP, +5,751 gold, 3,953 kills. Still hunting, with 2 h 50 min of stamina left." plus "See the hunt".
- The hunt detail's "Before you go" lines (food, drink, stamina, gold, and the Fire Rune warning) are clear and honest.
- Equip result text: "You fight as a Ranger now, and your bar has this weapon's moves."
- Items show a comparison card (Practice Bow against Practice Sword) when tapped.
- Mining text: "It keeps going while you are away. A hunt stops it." Exactly what a bus player needs to know.

== 7. HARNESS ODDITIES ==
- The first start replays the whole migration chain (about 20 minutes with other lanes running; I raised the process priority to get through it).
- Sign-up says "Check your email" but the harness confirms accounts at once, so it never tests the email step.
- The 1 h return: my first page.goto timed out at 30 s under 4x throttle (the page had actually loaded); the open tab renewed the away stamp, so the "While you were away" card for 1 h was never shown. The 8 h return (unthrottled) showed it correctly.
- Playwright has no soft keyboard; I used insertText and, separately, shrank the viewport to emulate it.
- Pointer and touch events are synthetic: no finger size, no palm rejection, no real scrolling momentum (my swipes have no fling), so "swipes needed" overstates a real flick.
- performance.memory needs --enable-precise-memory-info (set); heap is quantised.

== 8. TOP 5 ==
1. Give the sheet bodies more vertical room (or collapse the Town/Home row and coach cards while a sheet is open): 190 px of scroll body is the dominant phone cost.
2. Put the action first in the long sheets: Tamsin's board above the armour-weight setup (or move the picker beside a disabled Take), Hunt again / Open bag at the top of the summary, shop section chips (Gear / Tools / Seeds) to jump instead of 2,000 px of swiping.
3. Make the touch targets real: hero colour swatches (16 px) as a bigger grid or a picker sheet; action bar and bag slots 36 px to 40+; chat channel tabs 20 px to 40.
4. Close the long-press tooltip on release (or add "tap anywhere to close") and surface the loot "..." menu next to Sell.
5. Look at the Meadowbrook hunt cost on a low-end phone: 149 long tasks and 51 percent of the main thread at 4x, plus a 28 s signed-in reload at 4x (426 requests).

== 9. VERDICT: would I keep this on my phone? ==
Yes, for the hunting loop, with reservations. Everything I needed was reachable by tap, swipe and long press with no keyboard, hover or right-click, the portrait guard is graceful, nothing was lost across reload, close, away or rotate, and the away report is exactly what a bus player wants. What would make me drop it on a phone: the 190 px sheets (shop, bag and tally are long thin scrolls), the 16 px colour swatches, and a 28 s signed-in reload at 4x throttle (the load is request-count bound: 426 files). I would not rate it by what I could not test: real fingers, a real soft keyboard, real mobile network and thermal throttling.

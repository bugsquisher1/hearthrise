Talents: the Roads (layout sketch, widened)

Rough layout for docs/design/redesign/17-talent-tree.md. One panel component: a window on desktop, a sheet on a landscape phone. Seven countries in a ring of uneven regions around Havora. Square stones are milestones, ringed dots on dashed side paths are waystones, diamonds are crossroads (3 options each), dashed diamonds on border roads are hybrid crossroads, small houses are border towns, red standing stones are vows (dashed for hybrid vows), three dots are an open road. Gold is a sample Shield hero at Level 34 who took the Shield and Restoration border town. The art lane owns the final look.

Desktop window, 1180 x 660

Phone sheet, landscape 922 x 423 (zoomed on the Shield and Restoration border, pinch and drag to move)

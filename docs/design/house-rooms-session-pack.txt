#   * No AI label.

# Recraft custom Style anchor for these, seed it from the approved 1024px

=========================================================================
 2 · THE COMPOSITION LAW — measured off the rendered mount, not assumed
=========================================================================

  THE MOUNT. Each room is a card in a responsive grid
  (.hh-rooms: repeat(auto-fill, minmax(268px,1fr)), homestead-rooms.css:31).
  The illustration sits in .hh-room-art, which declares:
      aspect-ratio: 620 / 190   (= 3.26 : 1, a WIDE SHORT band)
      object-fit: cover  (on .hh-room-img)
  So a card ~268-400px wide renders the art at ~82-123px tall. The card TEXT
  (room name, current rung, effect, cost) renders in a SEPARATE block BELOW
  the art — the illustration is NOT painted behind the body copy. The only
  thing that overlays the art is a small level/lock BADGE chip in the
  TOP-RIGHT corner (.hh-room-badge).

  WHAT THAT MEANS FOR THE PAINTING:
    (a) Deliver 16:9. The mount is 3.26:1 and uses `cover`, so a 16:9 source
        is cropped to its CENTRAL HORIZONTAL BAND — the top ~22% and bottom
        ~22% of your image are crop tolerance and will usually not be seen.
        Mass every key object (hearth, anvil, bookcase, altar) in the MIDDLE
        THIRD vertically. Do not put the one readable thing near the top or
        the floor edge.
    (b) The subject must read ACROSS THE WHOLE WIDTH — one wide interior, not
        a single centred hero object floating in a void. These are 268px-wide
        cards; a scene fills them, a lone prop looks lost.
    (c) Keep the UPPER-RIGHT CORNER calm and unbusy — the level/lock badge
        sits there. A dark rafter or plain wall in that corner is ideal; a
        bright window or a face there fights the chip.
    (d) These rooms are OWNED and lived-in — warm, lit, occupied. Unlike the
        combat backdrops (which are "silent and long deserted"), a kitchen
        with a fire going and a pot on the crane is exactly right. The
        homestead is the cosy half of the game; paint it that way.

  DELIVERY SIZE: 1600 x 900 is ample (the mount never exceeds ~400px CSS wide
  at DPR 2 = 800 device px). Convert to .jpg quality ~82 at wiring; budget
  <= 140 KB each, ~1.1 MB for all eight.

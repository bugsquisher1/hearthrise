THE MORROW HILLS' ART RULES (lane/b-morrow-art, 2026-11-19). Read before drawing, importing or placing any Morrow Hills art.
Carter's Rest and the Ox Road, and every Morrow ground after them. Learned on the sample (lane/b-morrow-art-sample, three rounds,
art/review/morrow-sample/ on that branch) and ruled by its unanchored reviews. ART_DIRECTION.md and src/data/world/art-budget.js
stay law; these add to them.

FLOORS AND BODIES
1. The Hills paint dim. The moor is shaded 3 steps (median 0.028, the Hold's cobble is 0.027), the road 1 step (0.027). A mid floor
   (0.07-0.09) and the moor as generated (0.17) are dead zones: no body reads 3:1 on them.
2. Bodies import with lift 2 (port-pixellab.mjs unit --lift 2). On the dim floor the Troll Picker reads 3.7-3.9:1.
3. NEVER place a spawn on bright grass (0.17 and up, the Hold's hill): a lifted troll reads 1.4:1 there.
4. Lift the body only, never what it carries. A sack, a slate, a ladle or a trough keeps the value it was drawn at: the lift made
   the Picker's sack the brightest thing on him, louder than the lichen accent (ART_DIRECTION section 3), a loot bag at x2.
5. A road must still show in greyscale. Its value cannot equal the moor's: give it a worn, lighter verge and pebbles a step up,
   so it does not rest on hue alone (red-brown against green is the pair colour-blind players lose).

WALLS
6. Repeating wall slices are full class height (wall 32x60, stub 32x24 at 20-24 px). A tumbled or broken end is its own end
   piece, left and right, never a slice of the run (a 12 px slice in the cycle leaves a gap every six tiles).
7. A wall face is lit from the upper left like everything else: a lit edge on the cap, lit stone tops, the foot a step darker.
   Boards, doors and shutters sit on the wood colours (earth ramp), never on the stone greys, or the opening vanishes at x2.

CREATURES
8. The trolls are one lanky body plan told apart by what they carry. Family accent: lichen green (the leaf and moss ramps), at most
   15% of a body, on interior skin only (the ring inside the outline stays skin so the lift can carry it).
9. Every facing's still and idle frames stand 40-42 px (the character class 36-40 +10%). What a creature carries shows in every
   idle frame and in every facing's still (a hand pass pastes it from a frame where it shows).
10. A side attack whose prop drifts (v3 turned the Picker's sack to stone, doubled it, faced the camera) is redrawn from a profile
   keyframe SKETCH of the strike, made from a still where the prop shows; animate_image from the still to the sketch.
11. Heads are drawn from the finished body's south still (the body crop as a style image), never before it.

PROCESS
12. Batches of at most five creatures. After each batch an UNANCHORED review by a fresh reader (the images and ART_DIRECTION only),
   then the fixes, then the next batch. A piece that misses once gets a free sketch before it is generated again.
13. Subscription generations only; paid credits stay $0.

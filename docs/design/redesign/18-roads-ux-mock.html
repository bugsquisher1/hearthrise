Talents: the Roads, UX mock

Companion to docs/design/redesign/18-roads-review-ux.md. Same geometry as 17-talent-tree-sketch.html (read from its map at run time), redrawn in three zoom tiers with one node language, screen-size labels that never shrink with zoom, and a route preview. Sample hero: Shield, Level 34, 2 points, the Shield and Restoration border town owned. Every label is at least 14.5 px (the --t-micro floor).

1. Desktop 1180 x 660, Road view (opens here, on your frontier), route preview to a far crossroads

2. Desktop 1180 x 660, Country view (zoomed out): roads and decisions only, progress per country

3. Desktop 1180 x 660, Node view (zoomed in): crossroads options and milestone values on the map

4. Phone 922 x 423, Road view, a crossroads tapped: the route draws, the sheet slides in from the right

5. Phone 922 x 423, Country view, sheet closed: tap a country to zoom to its roads

6. Node language: shape and size say the kind, fill says the state

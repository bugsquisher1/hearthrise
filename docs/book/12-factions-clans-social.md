# 12. Factions, clans and social

Town, chat, friends, clans and the castle, factions, ladders, titles and the town's own people.

---

## 12.1 The town
**[Unchanged]**

- Havora is a shared town. You see other players' heroes walking around it.
- The town shows up to 120 other players at once. The redesign splits towns into shards so each one feels busy without costing too much.
- The Great Hearth burns in the square. Named fuels color it for everyone.
- Working heroes are seen at their benches.

## 12.2 Chat
**[Unchanged]**

| Tab | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Global** | Everyone online |
| **Party** | Your party (when you're in one) |
| **Clan** | Your clan (when you're in one) |
| **System** | Server notices, alerts and Rare drops. Read only |
| **Combat** | Your combat log. Read only |

- **Whispers:** start a line with "w:" and a name. Up to 6 whisper tabs.
- A message is up to 240 characters with up to 3 links.
- Global is sent in small batches every 5 seconds. When it's busy, a **slow mode** kicks in: a 10, 30 or 60 second gap between your lines.
- There is **no Local tab** and **no live party finder** (Tyler, S-2 b). The hire board takes that job later.

**[Later patch]:** a channel for your faction side.

## 12.3 Friends and players
**[Unchanged]**

Right-click a player for: Inspect gear, Message, Add or Remove friend, Invite to party, Leave party, Block or Unblock. A Trade button exists but is hidden.

- A brand-new account waits 24 hours before it can send a friend link.
- Right-click a monster to Look at it.

## 12.4 The Town Crier
**[Unchanged]**

- Pay to have a line cried to the whole town.
- 1,000 gold, then 2,000, 4,000, 8,000 and 16,000 for each more that day. Up to 5 a day.
- Opens at Level 10. Up to 120 letters. 30 seconds between cries.

## 12.7 The castle
**[Later patch]**

Tyler (2026-11-21): "I would really like to keep arenas and the full castle, but if it needs to be delayed, that's fine if we still account for it."

- A castle the clan builds: **five buildings of ten levels** each, about a year of work.
- **Labor:** members' heroes put town hours in. Only the normal work day counts, never bought blocks.
- **Gold gifts** count, and lift the whole clan.
- **Upkeep:** a weekly bill that switches buffs off if unpaid.
- **Votes and a treasury.** Clan gold only a server call can move, with a ledger line for every gift and spend.
- The castle makes goods (like rations) and gives buffs, inside the clan's small share of power.
- It opens one building at a time, after clans are seen to form.

Not final: which building comes first, every size.

## 12.8 Factions: the First Flame and the Last Ember
**[Later patch]** (with the Morrow Hills)

**How it plays:** once a hero of your company has killed Lord Krell, Holly and Quartermaster Venn both ask you up the road to Morrow. You pick a side for the whole company. Hunting your side's grounds in the Morrow Hills earns standing. Standing unlocks your side's outfit, a banner for your house and a mark by your name. Every week the two sides are tallied on the Hills, and the side that turned out more hangs its banner over Havora's north gate. Your side has its own chat channel. Nothing about a side makes a hero hit harder.

**The story.**
- In Greenhaven the dead only rest if they're burned at the Great Hearth. Krell's priests burned his soldiers on the Pyre Field with no rite, and Krell buried the company he betrayed at Hask's Ford. North of the March, Gault raises the drivers and oxen of the Ox Road.
- **The First Flame** follows Holly, who learned the rite from Rowe, the Keeper at the Great Hearth. Holly carries the Hearth's fire north to give Morrow's dead a proper rest. Motto: "Preserve what was". Her camp is Holly's Vigil on the Ox Road.
- **The Last Ember** follows Quartermaster Venn, whose company died at the Ford when the old way failed it. Venn wants Morrow for the living. Harry drives the cart. Motto: "Forge what comes next".
- Neither side is evil. Havora belongs to neither.

**The rules.**

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Who picks | The company, once, for all its heroes |
| Joining opens | After any hero of yours kills Lord Krell |
| Joining costs | Nothing |
| Standing | One point per hero-minute on your side's grounds. Shown in hours |
| Standing is spent? | Never. It's a record |
| Changing sides | Hunt 40 hero-hours on the new side's grounds, then finish the swap. 7 days between swaps. Your old standing waits for you |
| Party with the other side | Always allowed |

**Your side's quartermaster** (all looks, no power):

| Standing | First Flame | Last Ember | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joining | Your side's mark by your company name | The same | Free |
| 40 hours | Flamebearer outfit | Emberkeeper outfit | Free |
| 120 hours | A side banner for your house | The same | 20 hours of wage |
| 200 hours | Hearth Shield (add-on) | Harry's Lantern (add-on) | Free |
| 400 hours | A side cloth for a mount | The same | 30 hours of wage |
| 600 hours | Holly's Light (add-on) | Kept Coal (add-on) | Free |

A four on its side's grounds earns 40 hero-hours of standing a day.

**The week on the Hills.** Each side's score is the average of its companies' standing that week, each company capped at 40 hours. The higher average wins its banner over the north gate for a week. Headcount and bought hours can't swing it.

**Which grounds pay which side:** Carter's Rest and Wyvern Roost pay the Last Ember. The Ox Road pays the First Flame. A First Flame partner for Wyvern Roost (the Night Shift) isn't drafted yet.

**What's cut:** faction skills, faction vows, faction-only drops, side-only grounds, spendable standing and any bonus for the smaller side.

**Today:** factions exist per hero with a switch that's off. Their screens stay dark in 0.3.

## 12.9 Ladders and records
**[Changed]**

**Today: the honor roll.** 16 boards, top 25 each: Level, Total level, each combat skill (with Inner Flame), each gathering and crafting skill, and Gold Given to the town.

**In the redesign:**
- **Records:** the deepest depth held on each ground, by Risk setting, kept and shown in your house and ranked.
- **Ladders:** deepest depth by ground, Tower floor, first kills.
- Every ladder ranks companies **inside Level bands of 20**, by the company's average hero Level. Bought hours move you into the next band sooner, never up your own.
- No board ranks raw Level or XP. Level 200 is a moment the town sees, not a race.
- A ranked entry shows the company's bars, with **Copy build**.
- Titles, the chronicle and the honor roll merge into one place for what a company has done.

Not final: the redesign's ladders ship with the social layer. Today's honor boards rank raw Level, which the redesign rules out. See the contradictions list.

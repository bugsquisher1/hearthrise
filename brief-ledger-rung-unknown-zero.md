## 1. ROOT CAUSE (verified; both skeptics hold; corrections applied)

1.1 Where the counts come from. `bestiary.kills_by_monster`/`kills_by_class` and `collection.found` are built in supabase/functions/hr-accrue/index.ts:736-746 and :849-865 and emitted at :1326-1327 (accrued aux), :1353-1354 (accrued:false idle — the common boot reply), :1531-1532 (replayed) and :1548-1549 (accrued). The `clamped` forfeit reply at :1500-1503 carries NEITHER block, so even a successful settle can leave the counts unknown. hr_load is a thin wrapper over hr_state_of (supabase/migrations/2026-08-11-apply-engine.sql:288-306) and hr_state_of carries neither count (bestiary-trophies.js:329-335 says it was dropped on purpose).

1.2 Where the client stores them. ONLY in accrue.js settle(), and only when the outcome is `accrued` or `nothing` (src/net/accrue.js:1056-1074: hrNoteServerBestiary :1059, hrNoteServerTrophies :1064, HearthriseCollection.noteServerCounts :1072). The trophy mirror is written at src/render/bestiary-trophies.js:129-138, the charm counters at src/render/bestiary-charms.js:74-98, the collection count at src/features/collection-log.js:226-236. A 503 classifies as `unavailable` (accrue.js:447), a 429 as `rate-limited` (:440), a short body as `malformed` (:430-432): none of those writes the mirrors.

1.3 The boot race (CORRECTION to the diagnosis). Both the accrue call and hr_load wait on the same ensure: character.js:396-399 fires beginServerAccrual inside ensureCharacter().then, and legacy.js:2440-2455 chains beginRecordLoad onto that same promise with `p.then(_load,_load)`. The 90 s settle loop (legacy.js:2220-2226) keeps asking, so the counts stay missing only while EVERY settle fails or answers `clamped`.

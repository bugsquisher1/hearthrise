WHAT I DID, BY HERO CLOCK
Hero time ended up about 12h 46m hunted, 5h 46m rested, not the ~3 hours in the brief, because the two 8-hour
absences ran on top of it. My hands-on play was roughly the 3 hours' worth of decisions.

Sign-in and hero. Handsome crest. Create account said "Check your email for a link to finish creating your account."
Hero screen is good. "There's no class to pick. The weapon you hold is how you fight" was a relief.

Minutes 0-5, Mill Cellar. Tip: "Rats in the mill... Open Hunts and enter the Mill Cellar." A gold ring showed me which
icon. Two clicks and my knight was fighting a giant rat. "You don't click to attack." Fine by me, I came for idle.
Level 2 at about 90 seconds. DELIGHT. Rats and wasps look great.

Minutes 2-5, Farm. A tiny tab said "Plant your land". Planted two turnips. I pressed Water and it went from "4 h left"
to "2 h left". DELIGHT, a free cheap trick I found myself. Also poked every icon on the top bar, because there are 17
and none has a word on it until you hover. A lot to take in.

Minutes 5-21. Skipped 15 minutes. "Cash in: You're Level 5. Leave the hunt to go back to town." Summary: 167 monsters,
716 gp balance. "Leaving in 23s" surprised me, but I accepted it.

Town chores, about 10 steps in a row. "Next: Feed the Hearth" (hero WALKED across town, over a bridge, to burn bones:
DELIGHT, the world felt real), then "Before the wolves" (free blessings), "Sell what only pays coin", "Decide once",
"Cook your catch" (hero walked to a campfire, DELIGHT again, then "Done: 9 Cooked Shrimp, 1 burnt"), then Tamsin.
Clear every step, because the ring always showed me where. But it's a long time without my hero fighting. BORED around
"Decide once". I'd have closed the tab if I were tired.

Next 30 minutes, Meadowbrook Thicket. Took 200 Gray Wolves from Tamsin, heavy armor picked. Wolves 106/200 after 30 min.

First 8-hour absence (page closed). Came back still hunting: 08:34:48, Level 10, 4,968 kills, 12,486 gp. Big number
joy, but see top-5 #2. Bought all five gear pieces for 1,280 gp, "Put on 5 pieces." Harvested turnips. Listed a Studded
Leather Body at the Weighhouse. Sold loot. Took Tamsin's Turncoats.

Next hour, Wickstead. 50/50 Turncoats inside the first hour. 28,992 XP an hour.

Second 8-hour absence (page closed). Came back in town: "Your stamina ran out." Hunt length 03:17:08. Hero had stood idle
for the rest. Level 11. This was my closest moment to quitting. "Next: A spell costs runes" was a very long tip for a
tired returning person, but the steps worked and Flame Strike showed on slot 6.

Last 32 minutes, Mosslight Glade, because it said "This hunt is above your level, expect losses." and I was curious.
No death, no losses: 66,279 XP an hour, 4,092 gp balance an hour, Level 12. Then I stopped.

I ended with 22,355 gp and no idea what I'm saving for.

  moves clocks backward. Almost certainly harness.
- "1 online", empty Weighhouse ("The scales are empty"), "No one is selling these" on every line, "Pip holds first
  place." I was alone. Says nothing about a real market or a real roll. My listing never sold, for the same reason.
- "Paid today: 2 of 3" never reset across 16+ hours, and the stamina shop counter likewise: the harness header says a wait
  never crosses a day key.
- "Check your email" then sign-in works with no email: harness confirms at once.
- I never saw anything under the "While you were away" alert listed in All characters. It may need real elapsed time or a
  notification the harness doesn't send. So top-5 #2 might partly be the harness. Check it on live.
- Per-hour figures come from skips run through the real engine. I trust them as rough, not exact.
- A leftover "Market M" label over the screen after a skip was only my mouse parked on that icon.
- Mosslight's rate has a change in it: Flame Strike was added just before, and the tip says that alone earns "about 15%
  more". One 32-minute sample.

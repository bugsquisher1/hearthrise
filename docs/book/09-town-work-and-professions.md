# 9. Town work and professions

When the hunting day ends, your heroes walk home and work. This chapter covers the work day, the trades, every recipe and gathering spot, and what's coming later.

---

## 9.1 The idea
**[New in 0.3]**

Crafting sells hunted hours. Gear never breaks, so crafting can't live on replacing gear. It lives on the one thing this game burns without end: four heroes an account, hunting for hours every day.

- **Drops give the item. Trades improve it and keep it running.**
- Every trade makes something a hunter keeps using up.
- The store sells the floor forever, and only the floor (chapter 8).

## 9.2 The work day
**[New in 0.3]**

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Town work a hero can do each server day | 8 hours |
| With Premium | 10 hours (outside 0.3) |
| Bought work blocks | 2 hours each, up to 4 a day, each dearer than the last |
| When the day's work runs out | The hero waits in town. The next day opens a fresh cap |
| Time not working | Costs nothing |

A bought work block is pricey on purpose: about 0.5 hours of wage an hour, more than the goods are worth. You buy trade XP and stock, never more gold.

Not final: bought work blocks are lane B1. The cap is built.

## 9.3 Trades belong to the company
**[New in 0.3]**

- Every trade Level is **owned by the company**, not by each hero. One Cooking Level for the account, used by every hero who cooks.
- Trades cap at **99**.
- Combat skills stay each hero's own.

Tyler (CR-Q1 a): trades on the company, up to 99.

**[Later patch] Masteries:** at most three Masteries per account, so no one account is best at everything and trades stay worth buying from each other.

## 9.4 The trades
**[Changed]**

Ten trades: four that gather and six that make.

| Trade | Kind | In 0.3? | What it's for | Who burns it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mining** | Gather | Yes | Ore, coal, rune essence | Smithing, Runecutting |
| **Woodcutting** | Gather | Yes | Logs | Woodcrafting |
| **Fishing** | Gather | Yes | Fish | Cooking |
| **Farming** | Gather, on plots | Yes | Crops and herbs | Cooking, later Alchemy |
| **Cooking** | Make | Yes | Meals and drinks, the only heals | Every hero, every pull. The biggest burn in the game |
| **Smithing** | Make | Yes | Bars, whetstones, arrowheads, Temper Kits, jewelry, tools | Melee every hour, every arrow, every Temper |
| **Woodcrafting** | Make | Yes | Planks, arrows and bolts, ward charms, cloaks | Ranged every shot. The house |
| **Runecutting** | Make | Yes | Runes | Casters and healers, every cast |
| **Artificing** | Make | Yes, imbuements and carry-over only | Imbuements, carry-over, later Trait jewelry and salvage | Every imbued piece every 20 hunted hours |
| **Alchemy** | Make | **[Later patch]** | Draughts, never a heal | Every hero who wants the ground's answer |

**Names in transition.** Today's screens say **Crafting** (planks, arrows, cloaks, tempering salts) and **Runecrafting** (runes). The redesign splits and renames them: Woodcrafting, Runecutting and Artificing. "Runecrafting" was another game's word.

**Hunters no longer gather.** Gathering is town work only.

## 9.5 What your heroes do after the hunting day
**[Unchanged]**

The rest plan sets it once, per hero.

| After the hunt | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Job** (default) | Works a gathering spot or a bench |
| **Wait** | Rests in town |
| **Train** | Goes to the Training Yard (chapter 3) |

- A gathering job runs for a count (20, 100 or 1,000) or a standing job with no count, up to 24 hours.
- When the job's count is done, the hero waits.

## 9.6 Recipes on hours of work
**[New in 0.3]**

- In the redesign, every recipe is a share of the work behind one hunted hour. Its price, XP, time and materials all come from that one number and the wage.
- A traded good carries the band of its materials, so a new player's hour of work isn't priced like a far player's.
- Recipes ship as a sample of five first, then batches of 20 or fewer.

Not final: lane K1 rebuilds the recipes on hours of work. The lists below are today's.

## 9.7 Bench tax
**[Unchanged]**

- The public benches in town take **15%** of what the output sells to the shop for, on Smithing, Crafting, Cooking and Runecrafting.
- Own the matching room in your house (Forge, Workshop, Kitchen, Library) and that skill's tax goes away.
- Tiny fees under half a gold carry over and are paid when they add up.
- The Great Hearth is a public fire, never taxed.

## 9.15 Runecrafting (becoming Runecutting)
**[Changed]** (32 recipes today)

- **Mine rune essence** (Mining, no input), then **cut blank runes** (Level 1) or **split** them (Level 12).
- **Bind runes:** six families (Earth, Water, Fire, Ice, Poison, Rupture) at five grades: Chalked (1), Scored (15), Etched (30), Carved (45), Graven (60).

In 0.3 runes are a crafted supply and the store keeps only the floor.

## 9.16 Artificing
**[New in 0.3]**

- Replaces today's general Crafting bench for anything rolled or timed.
- **In 0.3:** makes imbuements (from a rare part of another ground plus remains) and carries Traits over to new gear (chapter 6).
- **[Later patch]:** jewelry and cloaks that roll Traits, salvage.

## 9.17 Alchemy
**[Later patch]**

- Makes **draughts**: one per hero, picked to answer a ground. Never a heal.
- Uses herbs from Farming and some creature parts.

## 9.18 Tools that wear out
**[Later patch]**

Tyler's idea (2026-11-22).
- Each trade has a tool. A crafted tool breaks after 12 hours of use.
- The plan keeps spares and swaps one in by itself. The return card says what broke.
- A tool costs about a twelfth of what an hour of work makes, so Smithing and Woodcrafting always have buyers.
- Gear never breaks. Only tools.
- The Crowns store sells tools that never break, with their own look and work animation. They work like the best crafted tool the hero can use, never better (chapter 14).

## 9.20 The self-supply rule
**[Unchanged]**

A material is made where its tier opens. No recipe ever asks for something you can't make yet at that Level.

## 9.21 Is there enough work?
**[New in 0.3]**

- Players who hunt a little have spare town hours and can sell what they make. Players who hunt all day have too few and buy. That's a real reason for two kinds of players to trade.
- Spare work can always be sold to the store at its floor price. That adds under 3% to the gold hunts make.
- The castle and the hire board will be the third and fourth uses of a resting hero's hours, later.
